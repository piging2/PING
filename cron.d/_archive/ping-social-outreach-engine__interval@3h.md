PRIORITY STATES (2026-09-11): every prospect carries one priority_state in the JSONL record. HOT means multiple fresh signals plus strong fit plus a meaningful signal. WARM means good fit plus a meaningful signal. WATCH means interesting but insufficient evidence. SUPPRESS means no, cooldown, contacted too recently, or unsuitable. Every sweep asks: "What is the highest-value eligible action for this business right now?" Never: "What can I send today?" Do not increase message volume simply because more signals exist; caps and ladders are absolute.
