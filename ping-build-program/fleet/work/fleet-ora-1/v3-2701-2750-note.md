highest oom_score. With oom_score_adj=0 on all three containers, the victim is whichever
holds the most RSS at the moment. Observed order: ping-gateway (684) > ping-postgres (668) >

necessary victim instead of the whole host.

killer fires instead of the system thrashing. No graceful degradation exists at the kernel
level; backpressure must be implemented in the services (connection limits, queue bounds).
