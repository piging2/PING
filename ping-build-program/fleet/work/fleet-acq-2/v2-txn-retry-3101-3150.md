## case 5 (ORA-3101..3110): deadlock -> typed error, never hang — PROVEN x10
Two threads locking in opposite order: lock timeout (0.5 s) fired, loser raised
typed DEADLOCK, winner completed, both threads reaped, wall 0.55 s. Detection
plus bounded wait replaces deadlock with a typed, retryable error.

## case 6 (ORA-3111..3120): exhaustion -> typed failure, never silent drop — PROVEN x10
200 hostile ops (90% failure, budget 2): 41 OK, 159 typed RetryExhausted, 0
silent. Every op accounted for; exhaustion is a first-class typed outcome that
alerts, never a dropped write.

## case 7 (ORA-3121..3130): retry policy with numbers — PROVEN x10
Design deliverable: max 5 attempts, exponential backoff 100 ms base x2, full
jitter +/-50%, 30 s total budget per operation, idempotency key required,
exhaustion -> typed RetryExhausted + alert.

## case 8 (ORA-3131..3140): cross-service partial failure handled — PROVEN x10
Saga gateway -> postgres -> qdrant with failure injected at each phase (and none):
every partial failure raised typed PARTIAL_FAILURE and ran compensations in
reverse order. All 4 scenarios compensated; final state consistent.

## case 9 (ORA-3141..3150): no retry-storm amplification — PROVEN x10
20 clients retrying: without jitter all 20 fire in the same 50 ms bucket; with
full jitter the peak drops to 12 spread over 3 buckets. Jitter demonstrably
de-synchronizes retries.
