# Humanness Golden Eval: Scoring Sheet

NOLAN-HUMANNESS-GOLDEN-EVAL. Listen to each clip and score the 12 dimensions 1-5.

Scale: **1** = poor / distracting | **2** = weak, noticeable problems | **3** = acceptable, minor issues | **4** = strong, near-human | **5** = excellent, indistinguishable from a real recording.
