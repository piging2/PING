## Highest-risk overlaps (top 5)

### Overlap 1 — THREE parallel identity namespaces, no bridge table (CONFLICTING)
Three live identity-ID schemes coexist with no observed mapping between them:
- **A. OIDC auth lane:** `deriveIdentityId` → `uuidv5(FYD_IDENTITY_NAMESPACE, "provider-account:"+provider+"::"+issuer+"::"+subject)` — src/fyd/identity/principal.ts:57. Used by Google callback/link/unlink/session routes.
