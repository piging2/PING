## Content humor check (2026-09-19, Nolan's standing principle; rescored 2026-09-19)

Humor is a core ingredient, not a checkbox. Every post attempts a humorous or
playful beat, scored as HUMOR OPPORTUNITY 0-4:
0 = genuinely inappropriate (serious posts may legitimately score here)
1 = subtle/lightheartedness
2 = natural wit
3 = strong comedic angle
4 = highly shareable humor
A serious post may score 0-1. A mundane operational topic should normally be
pushed toward 2-4. Never force humor merely to satisfy the metric; a forced
score is a failed check.

The beat can be a clever observation, playful wording, an absurd contrast,
self-aware humor, a tiny unexpected detail, a funny pig-related moment, a
humorous reaction to something in the post, dry/witty commentary, or a
deliberately ridiculous comparison. It must feel native to PING Social, never
bolted onto a generic caption.

PING humor is: observant, dry, clever, occasionally absurd, self-aware,
blue-collar friendly, technically literate. Never corporate, never desperate.
No repetitive pig puns, no generic AI humor.

Quality bar: the post still works if the reader ignores the joke. The humor
makes the post more memorable, never obscures the actual content.

Do not: force a joke into every sentence; make every caption sound like the
same comedian wrote it; manufacture fake enthusiasm; sacrifice factual
accuracy; fabricate experiences, events, or observations; use humor that could
create reputational or safety problems; turn the system into a joke generator.

Gate: no caption enters queue.json until it is scored and the score fits its
mode. For every proposed caption, answer in the post's decision record:
1. WHAT IS THE ACTUAL CONTENT?
2. WHAT IS THE PING SOCIAL ANGLE (mode)?
3. WHAT IS THE HUMOR OPPORTUNITY SCORE (0-4), AND WHY?
4. DOES THE SCORE FIT THE MODE, OR WAS HUMOR FORCED?
5. DOES IT FEEL ORIGINAL RATHER THAN TEMPLATE-GENERATED?
6. DOES THE HUMOR (OR ITS DELIBERATE ABSENCE) SERVE THE POST?
7. IS ANYTHING FABRICATED JUST TO MAKE THE JOKE WORK?
Record the outcome: score + one-line justification, or rewrite notes.

## Post-publish learning loop (2026-09-19, Nolan's standing procedure)

After every successful post: OBSERVE (reach, reactions, comments) -> PUBLISH
(done) -> VERIFY (live as the Page, no duplicates) -> REVIEW (what worked,
what did not) -> IDENTIFY ONE IMPROVEMENT -> PATCH (smallest safe change to
this file, the queue, or the worker) -> TEST -> RE-CERTIFY. Every post teaches
the system exactly one thing. Log the lesson in the daily memory log and, when
it changes a rule, in this file.
