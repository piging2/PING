Date: 2026-09-30. Lane: MarketingOS / technical marketing intelligence.
Task frame: Nolan's directive, 2026-09-30: "Continue the research of all cutting edge CRMs agentic marketing and business systems think of how we can harvest the best of the best keep differentiating us from the rest of the ecosystem be better than everything continuity above all."
This is RESEARCH, not roadmap. Harvest is not integration.

Method: five isolated web-research subagents ran in parallel over modern CRMs (11 systems), agentic sales systems (9), agentic marketing/conversational systems (5), and business operating systems (9). A sixth agent swept all 35 repo worktrees for existing PING/FYD capability overlap (the SEARCH step of the harvest pipeline, per standing pre-sprint law). ## Conventions used throughout

Evidence grades: **vendor-described** = claims from vendor pages, blogs, press releases. **independent** = corroborated by independent sources (official docs, engineering blogs, third-party reviews, open repos, multi-source). **unreplicated** = single source only. The label always says which of these backs each claim.

Continuity grades: **A** = event-sourced, immutable history, provenance, replay. **B** = partial (audit log, version history, or a real activity timeline with some provenance). **C** = snapshot amnesia (current-state store with an activity feed bolted on, no replay).

Harvest pipeline: REACH (can we learn/use this without a dependency?) / NEED (does it serve our audiences: home-service owners, technical buyers?) / SEARCH (does the repo already have it, in whole or part?) / COMPETING (does it compete with an existing PING authority?) / TESTED (production or demo-grade?) / SMALLEST (the primitive, not the product) -> verdict **ADOPT** (take as-is) / **ADAPT** (take the shape, rebuild our way) / **REJECT**.

## Executive summary

34 systems surveyed. Headline finding: **no surveyed system grades continuity A.** The best in the field grade B: genuine activity timelines, audit logs, approval provenance. But every one of them sits on mutable current-state storage; none of them can replay a past decision deterministically from an immutable event plane. The event-sourced, replayable slot is unoccupied in the market. That is the opening, and it is exactly Nolan's directive: continuity above all.

Second finding: the industry has converged. Constraints move earlier in the pipeline (pre-generation constraint injection is now the shared answer across Fin, Jasper, Writer, Copy.ai, Mutiny). Resource guardrails on agent spend (credit caps, token budgets, dry-run estimation) are becoming table stakes. Permission-aware retrieval is enforced at query time by the best systems (Notion, ClickUp, Asana). Approval gates and draft-by-default on external effects are now the expected trust surface (Lindy, D365 Cowork, Copy.ai, HubSpot Agent Hub). None of them combine these with evidence binding or replay. That combination is the harvest.

Third finding: counterparty risk is everywhere. Three of nine agentic sales systems are now owned by platform giants (Common Room -> Zoom Jul 2026, Warmly -> HubSpot Sep 2026, Qualified/Piper -> Salesforce Apr 2026). Drift has unverified shutdown chatter (Sep 2026). Whole-product adoption is off the table across the board; we harvest mechanisms, never platforms.

The full per-system writeups live in `notes/crms.md`, `notes/agentic-sales.md`, `notes/agentic-marketing.md`, `notes/business-os.md`, and `notes/repo-overlap.md`. This report is the synthesis: what each system contributes, how we do it differently, and what we deliberately do not take.

## The convergence map

What the industry already does (table stakes, converge with): agent execution guards (budgets, dry-run, conditional gates), approval gates with named approvers, draft-by-default external sends, permission-filtered retrieval with citations, typed observation-to-entity binding, derived fields computed from activity, deterministic-vs-agentic step separation.

What nobody does (our open lane): bind every derived claim to its support set on an immutable evidence plane. Deterministically replay any past decision from the same evidence. Make owner corrections governed overlays that survive regeneration. Record approval itself as a replayable event rather than a UI moment. Score with published data floors and first-class UNKNOWN instead of a fake number. The market gives you the number or the audit log; nobody gives you the evidence set and the re-run.

## 1. Modern CRMs (11 systems)

### Attio
Mechanisms: (1) Lists, curated single-object collections where process state lives on the membership edge (the list entry), not on the record; idempotent entry ops (`entries assert`) as first-class API semantics. (2) Workflow trigger/action algebra on record and list-entry events with a small fixed action vocabulary. Evidence: **independent** (open-source CLI docs, third-party playbook repos, independent reviews; marketing framing vendor-described). Continuity: **B** (real activity timeline + webhooks, field history a sidecar). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: process state on the membership edge, never on the record. Differentiation: our list entries are evidence-bound events with causal links that replay into entry state; Attio's entry state is a snapshot with a timeline bolted on. Do not take: the custom-object schema as a new authority, the snapshot-record assumption, freeform owner scheming without governance.

### Folk
Mechanisms: (1) Magic Fields, AI-derived attributes as first-class schema with metered budget and an explicit audit path, values marked as generated. (2) Hosted MCP server making the CRM's objects callable as tools. Evidence: **independent** (vendor pages corroborated by third-party repos and reviews; audit-UI claim unreplicated). Continuity: **B** (derived-field auditing, interaction history; corrections still overwrite). Harvest: ADAPT (1), ADOPT the pattern (2). Smallest steal: every AI-inferred value carries generator identity and an audit queue; our version binds the inference to its support set and makes owner corrections survive regeneration. Do not take: capture-without-consent UX, credit-metering as an internal architecture, folk as a tenant store.

### Clay
Mechanisms: (1) Waterfall enrichment with per-field resolving-provider attribution and per-attempt cost events. (2) Column-type data model (manual/enriched/computed/AI-generated) plus Claygent with prompts as versioned assets. Evidence: **independent** (multi-source practitioner docs and skill repos). Continuity: **C** (spreadsheet-snapshot engine; provenance per field is a seed, the table is snapshot-amnesia by design). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: every enriched fact names its provider, every attempt logs its cost; our version keeps losing providers' non-resolutions in history and resolves conflicts by evidence precedence, not overwrite order. Do not take: credit-metering internals, async table-run architecture, Clay-hosted tables as the store.

### Streak
Mechanisms: (1) Magic Columns, 50+ derived fields auto-computed from linked communication history, zero data entry. (2) Inbox-native pipeline, the record is the email thread, logging is free. Evidence: **independent** (engineering/marketing primary technical source + third-party reviews; 750k+ users). Continuity: **B** (email-thread history is near-append-only provenance for conversations; structured fields remain snapshots). Harvest: ADOPT the pattern (1), ADAPT (2). Smallest steal: derived columns computed deterministically from the communication event stream, never hand-typed; our version makes derivations projections over an immutable log so corrections are events, not silent recomputes. Do not take: Gmail/Chrome-extension lock-in, the box-equals-row model as a primitive, thread-presence-equals-truth.

### Copper
Mechanisms: (1) Bidirectional Google Workspace sync as zero-entry capture, contacts created from Gmail activity and signatures, meetings auto-appearing on timelines. (2) Suggestion-driven record maintenance, the system proposes, the human approves. Evidence: **independent** for (1) (G2 4.5, independent reviews); (2) vendor-described, unreplicated. Continuity: **B** (auto-populated timelines from the workspace stream; no event-sourced transitions). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: capture from the communication stream with one-click confirm; every auto-created record carries its source; in our version auto-creations land as *observations* (proposed state) until owner intent promotes them, so a bad auto-parse is rejectable evidence, never a polluted fact. Do not take: Google-only lock-in, capture-equals-correctness, Copper as a store.

### Close
Mechanisms: (1) Smart Views, saved filter-defined lead buckets feeding a dialer queue with a drain-the-queue cadence; Custom Activities as typed event logging templates. (2) Chloe, an in-CRM AI voice agent that acts against CRM state and writes outcomes back as typed activities with full conversation history (beta: 818,000+ calls across 306 businesses; GA June 9 2026). Evidence: **independent** (press release syndicated multi-outlet, hands-on reviews, independent dialer roundups). Continuity: **B** (strongest action-history in the survey; still snapshot state underneath). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: typed activity templates + dynamic saved-filter queues; our version puts every agent action through proposal, authorization, attempt, result, effect, verification. Do not take: outbound-call-centrism as the universal model, telephony as a knowledge authority, Close the product.

### Pipedrive
Mechanisms: (1) Activity-based selling: every deal must carry a next scheduled action; deals with no activity in 7+ days are flagged stalled, and the AI assistant proposes the specific next action. (2) Native MCP server (June 30 2026): deal/contact/org search, record CRUD, lead-to-deal conversion, pipeline insights, meeting-notes-to-structured-record as tools with OAuth scoping. Evidence: **independent** for (1) (multiple corroborating reviews, incl. an honest note it does not weight by deal value); **independent but single-source** for (2) (Business Wire launch; unreplicated). Continuity: **B** (best-in-class activity-timeline UX in the survey; still a timeline on snapshot records). Harvest: ADOPT the pattern (1), ADAPT (2). Smallest steal: staleness as a derived property, a missing next_action is itself a signal with an owner; our version makes the next action an outcome window with owner and deadline, computed by replaying the event stream, and an honestly open window is never turned into a metric. Do not take: deal-value-blindness, passive Smart Contact Data enrichment (documented misattribution, a provenance failure to learn from), Pipedrive as the store.

### Freshsales
Mechanisms: (1) Freddy AI scoring 0-100 trained on the tenant's own conversion history, with a documented data floor (~100 contacts) before it activates. (2) Lifecycle stages as API-addressable events, externally triggerable, with webhooks emitted on every transition. Evidence: **independent** for (1) (cross-vendor comparisons, honest documented limits); **independent but single-source** for (2). Continuity: **B** (rich timeline incl. behavioral signals; state remains current-value only). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: score = f(own conversion history) with a published minimum-data floor and a "not enough data" state instead of a fake number; our version binds score features to cited events, model version recorded, insufficient history a first-class state. Do not take: native-sequence tooling as outreach, suite lock-in, scoring-as-black-box.

### HubSpot (Breeze AI / Smart CRM agent layer)
Mechanisms: (1) Agent Hub + Agent Builder: governed agent fleet with per-agent access controls, credit run limits, a live status console, and approval steps; prebuilt agents over Customer/Prospecting/Data/Content/Social/AEO; September 2026 Spotlight relaunched the Breeze Assistant as coordinator of specialized agents. (2) Breeze Intelligence + Data Agent: continuous enrichment from public sources with a dedicated Data Agent whose job is record hygiene. Evidence: **independent** (battlecard repos, independent architecture writeups, ZoomInfo provider announcement). Continuity: **B** (best agent-governance surface in the survey; agent writes still hit snapshot records). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: per-agent identity with its own permission scope, spend budget, and live status; enrichment that cites its source. Note the July 2026 ToS incident as a free lesson: HubSpot attempted to pool customers' business-card contact data into a shared enrichment dataset by default and reversed it within four days after backlash (independently corroborated). Enrichment data-pooling must be opt-in. Differentiation: our agents act on the evidence plane, so every agent write is a witnessed event with a causal chain; HubSpot's agents write like privileged employees with good guardrails. Do not take: the unified Smart CRM record (already covered in prior research), per-resolution pricing as an internal primitive, default-on data pooling, HubSpot as the agent substrate.

### Salesforce (Agentforce)
Mechanisms: (1) Topics -> Instructions -> Actions: bounded agent scope with explicit routing. A fast routing model classifies input to a topic; the agent's reasoning is constrained to that topic's instructions and action allowlist (Flows, Apex, APIs, MCP tools); Atlas Reasoning Engine runs a bounded ReAct loop (plan -> retrieve -> reason -> act -> refine, reported ~7 loops per message), expands the query before retrieval, and re-plans rather than failing. (2) Einstein Trust Layer: grounding enforced as a platform invariant (no customer-data answer without retrieved org data), zero data retention with third-party LLM providers, PII masking, toxicity/hallucination guardrails. Evidence: **independent** (technical guides, learning repos, Atlas explainers; Trust Layer mechanisms consistently described across independent technical sources). Continuity: **B** (field history and audit trails; agent reasoning traces are ephemeral). Harvest: ADAPT (1), ADOPT the principle (2). Smallest steal: topic-scoped action allowlists + bounded reasoning loop + pre-retrieval query expansion; grounding as an enforced invariant, not a prompt suggestion. This is PING's "evidence before eloquence" validated at Salesforce scale (11T+ tokens/quarter); we implement it as evidence binding, not a trust-layer product. Differentiation: our grounding is structural and replayable (retrieval query, retrieved chunks, model version all recorded as events); Agentforce enforces grounded-or-silent at runtime, we add grounded-and-reproducible. Do not take: the Salesforce platform, Data Cloud as the data architecture, opaque engines as acceptable, Topics as a new authority noun.

### Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Copilot / Cowork)
Mechanisms: (1) Cowork over governed MCP plugins: the AI composes over a small set of vendor-published MCP plugins rather than touching the database; existing identity and permission enforced on every call; the agent *proposes* record updates, drafted emails, case resolutions for approve/edit/dismiss rather than silently acting; missing data produces explicit unknowns rather than fabrication. (2) Dataverse semantic layer: schema-aware agentic search so agents retrieve records the way business logic does. Evidence: **vendor-described** (official Microsoft blog, detailed and mechanism-specific; engineering blog for the semantic layer; no independent implementation reviews). Continuity: **B** (most PING-aligned governance posture in the survey; still snapshot state underneath). Harvest: ADAPT both, and treat (1) as near-standing-law: propose-don't-act, per-call permission checks, explicit UNKNOWN. Smallest steal: agent writes are proposals with approve/edit/dismiss; every tool call re-checks the caller's permissions; missing data -> explicit unknown. Differentiation: our proposal->approval->action chain is itself the durable record on the evidence plane; Microsoft enforces per call and surfaces proposals in the UI, we make the chain replayable forever. Do not take: the Microsoft stack, closed plugin ecosystems, Work IQ as an authority noun.

## 3. Agentic marketing / conversational systems (5)

### Intercom Fin (spun out as fin.ai)
Mechanisms: (1) Failure-trace debugger with back-test and monitor proposal: given a pasted failure artifact (conversation URL), the system traces every internal reasoning step, identifies the root cause (often user-written guidance creating a reasoning loop), proposes a corrected rule, **replays the corrected rule against the original failing conversation** to prove the fix, then suggests a standing production monitor for the recurrence pattern. Closed loop: trace -> root-cause -> proposed edit -> replay proof -> monitor. (2) Resolution-grounded improvement flywheel + mixed generative/deterministic architecture: resolved conversations feed a knowledge flywheel; the runtime deliberately mixes generative and deterministic workflows via a Procedures framework, with a testing suite covering simulations, regression, and manual inspection. Evidence: **independent** for (1) (VentureBeat interview with Intercom leadership, "debugger skill" of Fin Operator, an internal agent whose only job is managing Fin; corroborated detail that Operator runs on Claude); **unreplicated** for (2) as a whole (scale numbers vendor-sourced from a commissioned report; framework corroborated by DevRev). Continuity: **B** (real trace-and-replay primitive per conversation; memory lives in KB articles, no canonical event plane). Harvest: ADOPT (1), ADAPT the mixed-execution framing / REJECT the flywheel as stated. Smallest steal: **replay-as-proof-of-fix**: any proposed rule change must re-run against the exact failing artifact before adoption. This is the single strongest steal in the whole survey: the only mechanism that closes the loop with replay-as-proof; everything else optimizes generation, the debugger optimizes correction. Our version replays against the recorded event artifact, requires owner approval of the exact diff, and stores the correction as an owner-governed overlay. Do not take: the conversation-resolution-first worldview (DevRev's fork: conversation resolution vs organizational memory; volume is not evidence), the custom-model route (Apex).

### Mutiny
Mechanisms: (1) Opportunity-driven segmentation: the system mines visitor data and conversion history to recommend which segments are worth personalizing at all, pairing each with a playbook (named reusable tactic). (2) Personalization as always-on experiments with revenue attribution: every experience ships inside an A/B test; personalization and measurement are one object. Evidence: **vendor-described** for the analyzer; TechCrunch 2022 independent reporting for the ML basis; case-study numbers (Snowflake 4x account-to-meeting, +24% conversion) vendor-described, unreplicated. Continuity: **C** (a winning variant's lineage is a dashboard snapshot, not an immutable record). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: segments-as-recommendations-with-attached-tactics, every recommendation carrying its playbook and its evidence; never ship a personalization without its comparison (control, metric, observation window from birth). Our version binds every recommendation to the observations that earned it and every experiment to a claim ledger, so the system can replay why a segment won a year later. Do not take: firmographic de-anonymization machinery (privacy-hostile), cross-customer learning as the model (our learning stays tenant-scoped), the no-code editor product.

### Jasper
Mechanisms: (1) Brand-intelligence layer (Jasper IQ) as a cross-cutting constraint object: brand voice, audience profiles, style guide, knowledge base injected automatically into every generation endpoint; the style guide auto-derives from up to three brand sources; outputs are checked for voice distortion **before** the final draft, with suggested corrections, a pre-delivery check not a post-hoc review. (2) Grid: tabular orchestration of generative runs, each row a different episode/topic/segment with per-row status and timing. Evidence: **unreplicated** for (1) (consistent across vendor pages + three independent reviews, no technical deep-dive); **vendor-described** for (2). Continuity: **C** (brand memory is a mutable central store with no provenance; run history records status, not inputs). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: **named constraint objects + pre-commit conformance check**, violations surfaced as correctable diffs; rows-as-runs with per-row input snapshots and lineage. Our version makes each constraint evidence-bound (owner statement vs derived observation with dates), so the brand never silently drifts into statistical mush and disputes about who set a rule are answerable. Do not take: model-routing-as-feature, template sprawl, the website-crawl-into-mutable-blob memory toggle (the exact knowledge-staleness anti-pattern already flagged in our ledger).

### Copy.ai
Mechanisms: (1) Tables with cost-bounded, non-overwritable computed columns: CRM records flow through spreadsheet-like tables where each column is a typed operation; **dry-run before live run** (free, synchronous, write-free mode returning sample inputs and estimated cost); **hard per-run credit caps** (refuses to run without a budget; at cap the remaining cells are skipped and the run finalizes cleanly); **computed cells are write-protected** (you cannot hand-edit an AI/enrichment column's output, so provenance is machine-recorded). (2) Human-in-the-loop quality checkpoints as first-class workflow nodes: human review steps with pass/fail semantics inside the workflow graph, so the audit trail includes what a human approved and when. Evidence: **independent** for (1) (open-source GitHub skill documenting exact refusal semantics; independent 2026 product analysis; enterprise savings figures vendor-attributed); **vendor-described** for (2). Continuity: **C** (cells carry no binding to the run that produced them). Harvest: ADOPT the dry-run-estimate-first pattern and computed-cell write-protection; ADOPT the review-node primitive; ADAPT the credit-cap (expressed in our own budget terms). Smallest steal: dry-run estimation as a required first mode for any paid/batch AI step; write-protected computed values superseded only by new runs; review nodes that record the reviewer's decision and rationale as an event. Our version binds each enriched cell to its support set and keeps every superseding run as a new event in history. Do not take: the Infobase as a mutable trained-knowledge store, credit-shaped internal architecture, whole-product adoption.

### Writer
Mechanisms: (1) Brand standards as infrastructure: constraints injected at generation start, not surfaced in post-production; voice profiles encode style guides and terminology lists applied from the moment work is created, compliance-by-construction, tunable per team/product/region/audience. (2) Agent supervision suite: enterprise-wide observability with resource guardrails, including token-usage controls that cap agent spend, model-agnostic (governance sits above the agent layer). Evidence: **vendor-described** (BusinessWire releases; Forrester TEI claim vendor-commissioned, directional not evidence). Continuity: **B** (enterprise audit logs, RBAC/SSO, agent-level observability; you can see that an agent ran, not deterministically reproduce why it decided what it did). Harvest: ADAPT both. Smallest steal: the **constraint injection point** (a defined hook where named, versioned constraints enter generation context before any token is produced) and **per-agent spend/operation budgets with hard refusal** (pairs directly with Copy.ai's dry-run pattern). Our version gives every constraint provenance and version history as events; constraints are never silently edited. Differentiation: Writer governs what the agent may say and spend; we govern what the agent may claim and why; governance of expression vs governance of truth. Do not take: the proprietary-model strategy (Palmyra), multi-model integration surfaces, the Knowledge Graph as a vendor noun, commissioned-TEI numbers as evidence.

Convergent patterns across all five: constraints move earlier (Fin guidance before serving, Jasper IQ before generation, Writer standards at generation start, Copy.ai review nodes inside the workflow, Mutiny playbooks before content). The industry learned post-hoc review does not scale; pre-generation constraint injection is the shared answer. Our evidence-bound, owner-governed constraint layer is the same idea with provenance attached. ## 7. The differentiation thesis

The combined harvest makes possible a claim no single surveyed system can make:

**PING is the only agentic business system where every derived claim ships with its support set on an immutable event plane, every agent action is proposal -> approval -> attempt -> result -> verification (replayable), every derived field is a deterministic projection over pinned evidence, and every owner correction is a governed overlay that survives regeneration.**

Concretely, the harvest assembles into four properties:

1. **Evidence-carrying derived truth** (6sense's evidence-ships-with-score + Common Room's mandatory entity binding + Clay's per-field resolver attribution + Freshsales' data-floor gating). Every score, field, and inference names its evidence, its producer, and its confidence floor. UNKNOWN is a first-class state.
2. **Replayable action chains** (Fin's replay-as-proof debugger + Gumloop's dual-mode orchestration + Lindy's approval gates + D365's propose-don't-act + Copy.ai's review nodes). No correction becomes canonical without replay against the failing artifact and an owner-signed diff; agents orchestrate, deterministic workflows execute; consequential steps carry named approvers.
3. **Constraint-before-generation governance** (Jasper's pre-delivery conformance gate + Writer's generation-time injection + Notion's permission-aware retrieval + ClickUp's ACL-carrying chunks + Asana's declared AI-step IO). Named, versioned constraints enter before any token is produced; retrieval filters by requester identity; every generated claim cites its source revision.
4. **Resource and spend control as control plane** (Copy.ai's dry-run-estimate-first + hard credit caps + write-protected computed cells + Claygent's conditional gates + Writer's token budgets). No paid/batch AI step runs without an estimate; budgets refuse cleanly; computed values are tamper-evident and superseded, never overwritten.

No surveyed system has all four. The best have one or two bolted onto snapshot state. The A-grade continuity slot, the substrate none of them built, is ours to keep: we do not import their state models, their authority nouns, or their platforms. We harvest primitives and bind them to the event plane.
