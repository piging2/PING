## 1 in 7 contractors invisible to Google's AI
In July, Foxxr Digital Marketing [audited 50 home service contractors](https://foxxr.com/2026-home-service-ai-visibility-report/) across 12 states and 15 trades. It tested whether Google's AI Overviews recommend each one for its own core service across its full service area. Seven scored zero, including a mover with 181 reviews and a 4.8-star rating; more than one in four scored below 40, near enough to zero to cost them the job.

The pattern was confirmability, not effort. The nine perfect scorers kept one entity everywhere: a claimed and complete Google Business Profile, service pages naming actual towns, and third-party mentions that all agreed. For your pitch, that turns the reviews conversation into an entity conversation; the checklist is one complete GBP, one service page per town, and listings that all agree.
