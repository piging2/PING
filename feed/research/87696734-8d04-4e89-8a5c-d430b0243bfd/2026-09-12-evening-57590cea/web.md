## Companies sign the EU's AI transparency code
Any chatbot you deploy for a client has to say it is AI. That is the deployer half of the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency duties, enforceable since August 2. The fines run up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover, per [Law.com's September 8 writeup](https://www.law.com/corpcounsel/2026/09/08/eu-ai-act-article-50-new-guidance-expands-enterprise-ai-compliance-obligations/).

The European Commission has now released final guidelines spelling out who is on the hook, providers versus deployers, with real-world examples. A [voluntary code of practice](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260911-full-transparency-organizations-sign-eu-code) for showing compliance has drawn 82 providers and 152 deployers as of September 9. Anthropic said August 14 that future Claude models will watermark text to comply, which makes built-in disclosure a pitch asset.
