## Finding 1
QuoteIQ announced on September 14, 2026 the launch of QuoteIQ MCP, described as the first native Model Context Protocol connector built directly into a home service CRM. The release states it securely connects a contractor's QuoteIQ account with AI assistants they already use, including ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and other MCP-compatible platforms. Contractors can use everyday language to find customers, review account histories, prepare estimates and invoices, check unpaid balances, view schedules, book jobs, create tasks and reminders, manage pipeline activity, review business analytics, and monitor inventory. The company says the connector exposes 134 tools spanning 21 areas of a subscriber's QuoteIQ account, working with live business information. Co-founders Mike Vidan and Justin Rogers position QuoteIQ as bootstrapped and founder-owned, stating they rejected a $40 million acquisition and private-equity offer to stay independent.

## Finding 2
On September 16 OpenAI announced Sponsored Agents, a ChatGPT ad format that opens a separate, clearly labeled conversation with a business-sponsored agent, and Angi is one of the first brands running one in home services. OpenAI's help page for the format, checked on September 17, says Sponsored Agents are available only to selected advertisers in a limited alpha test in the United States and that OpenAI is not accepting early access requests at this time, with no waitlist. Angi's pilot announcement says the agent lets homeowners discuss a project and then enter Angi's service request flow to connect with local pros, with no new pricing or lead-type details disclosed. For cost context the article works from Angi Inc.'s Q2 2026 earnings release: 215.4 million dollars of US revenue against an average of 106,000 monthly active pros and 4.84 million total US leads, which works out to about 677 dollars per pro per month, roughly 15 leads per pro per month, or about 44.50 dollars per lead.
- https://aieranews.com/chatgpt-sponsored-agents-angi-contractors/ (undated)

## Finding 3
Builts.ai published a case study of a 24-tech residential HVAC contractor in the GTA that paired dispatch coordination automation with Voice AI while keeping ServiceTitan as the system of record, noting the same playbook also works with Housecall Pro or Jobber. The automation classifies new service requests from phone, web form, or chat, matches skill tags, proximity and drive time, protects schedule holds, and books the job directly in ServiceTitan with a timestamped activity log. Voice AI handles peak-hour and after-hours overflow, answering in seconds, booking routine calls, and warm-transferring complex installs, price negotiations, and escalations to CSRs with transcript summaries. Comparing four weeks pre-go-live to four weeks post-go-live, the study reports first-call booking rate rose from 46 percent to 59 percent, a 28 percent lift; missed-call rate fell from 18 percent to 3 percent during peaks; average speed to answer for overflow calls fell from about 1 minute 45 seconds to about 8 seconds; same-day dispatch rose from 22 percent to 35 percent; manual rescheduling calls fell 62 percent; and about 12 hours per week of CSR time shifted to installs, follow-up, and maintenance-plan upsells.
- https://builts.ai/blog/hvac-dispatch-automation-raised-first-call-bookings-28-percent/ (undated)
