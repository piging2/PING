## Finding 1
SRS Distribution, the building-supply arm Home Depot bought in 2024, has reached a definitive agreement to acquire Denver-based Lohmiller & Company, a wholesale HVAC distributor founded in 1990. Lohmiller runs four Colorado locations, employs 220 people, and distributes Carrier residential and commercial HVAC equipment; the owners say the company generated just under $250 million in 2023 revenue. Current owners Tim Brooks, Mark Brown and Roger Lee will keep leading the business, and SRS calls it the second addition to its new HVAC platform after the March acquisition of Mingledorff's in the Southeast. The deal page states no publication date; the item surfaced in reporting dated around August 11, 2026, with follow-on coverage August 28, 2026 noting Home Depot views HVAC distribution as a roughly $100 billion addressable market. Prospect angle: a major consolidation touchpoint in the Denver HVAC supply chain that local contractors will feel in equipment access and installer networks.

## Finding 2
Veteran Heating, Cooling, Plumbing & Electric, a veteran-owned Denver home services company founded in 2025 and headquartered at 3513 Brighton Blvd., #431, Denver, CO 80216, marked its first anniversary on August 23, 2026 by claiming status as the fastest-growing home services company in the Denver market. First-year numbers: 2,132 completed jobs, roughly 4,000 new customers, about 18,000 field hours, approximately 350 five-star reviews, a team of 69 employees with 17 wrapped trucks, and more than $1.4 million in approved HEAR (Heat Electrification and Affordability Rebate) projects helping Denver-area homeowners access Xcel Energy rebates. The company says it made no outbound sales calls and sent no email or text blasts, and that HVAC and electrical work is now booked out weeks or months in advance. As it enters year two it is pursuing licensing and permitting for expansion into Dallas/Fort Worth, has Wisconsin under consideration, and is in early stages of a capital raise. Prospect angle: a hyper-growth local shop entering an expansion and fundraising phase, a classic window for operations and communications support.

## Finding 3
On July 14, 2026, Friendly Group announced that Ultimate Heating, Cooling, Plumbing & Electric, a family-owned company serving the Denver Metro area since 1999, has joined Nytech Heating, Cooling, and Plumbing, which has served Castle Rock, Larkspur, Parker and surrounding Douglas County communities since 1997. The combined team covers heating, cooling, plumbing, indoor air quality, water heaters and drains, with the same customer-facing service teams continuing. Friendly Group framed it as continued support for local home service brands while preserving the customer-first culture that made them successful. Prospect angle: a newly enlarged multi-brand home services group in the south metro is exactly the profile that standardizes on shared phone, scheduling and follow-up systems.

## Finding 4
Brothers Plumbing, Heating & Electric, a Denver-based family-operated company providing plumbing, HVAC and electrical services since 1980, was recognized with the 2026 Consumer Choice Award in Denver, announced May 21, 2026.
