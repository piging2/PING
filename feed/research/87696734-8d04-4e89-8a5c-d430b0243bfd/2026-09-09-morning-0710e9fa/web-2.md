## Finding 1
ServiceTitan reported fiscal second-quarter 2027 results and used its earnings call to double down on its AI business, which is directly competitive with independent AI voice agents. President Vahe Kuzoyan said revenue and call volume for ServiceTitan Virtual Agents each more than doubled sequentially; the product handles voice and SMS functions including after-hours calls, overflow calls, lead response and customer messaging, and CFO Dave Sherry said adoption was balanced between first-time users and customers replacing existing solutions. CEO Ara Mahdessian described Max, the company's agentic operating system for trade businesses with more than 30 AI-enabled capabilities, as the new investment priority; the company more than doubled Max locations during the quarter, surpassing its prior target of just over 100, and now expects to finish fiscal 2027 with more than 700 Max locations. To fund the push, ServiceTitan is narrowing near-term expansion plans: it will focus on existing commercial trades (mechanical, electrical, plumbing, landscaping) and residential roofing rather than expanding into new commercial trades or broader residential exterior categories. The company also announced CRO Ross Biestman will leave his operating role after the Pantheon event, with Rikus Pretorius becoming CRO in the fourth quarter. Total quarterly revenue was $292.8 million, up 21% year over year.

## Finding 2
Fulltime AI (gofulltime.ai), a Montreal-based AI front desk for inbound-call businesses including home service companies, raised $1 million from Valsoft Corporation, announced from Montreal on August 18, 2026. The funding came through Valsoft's AI Ventures program. Fulltime positions the product as a full front desk replacement rather than a phone add-on: inbound calls, automated follow-up messages and outbound reengagement sit in one place, every call is answered in English and French, and missed calls are converted into booked jobs. The company's headline customer story is an HVAC company that replaced a traditional call center with Fulltime: base front desk cost fell by more than 50 percent, and calls and leads captured doubled. Founders are Vincent Vanier, William Matte and Thomas Fredrich (Head of Product), who said the team builds and monitors the system daily, tuning call handling and escalation logic. The page states no publication date; the funding date is given in the article text.

## Finding 3
Podium formally announced its AI Operating System for Home Services via PRNewswire on August 27, 2026, positioning it as the industry's first AI-first replacement for legacy field service management software rather than an AI add-on. The platform puts AI agents to work across field service management, communications and membership tools; it has been live since early 2026 and Podium says hundreds of contractors have already migrated from legacy FSM platforms. The named agents are: Larry, a voice agent that answers every call, qualifies the lead, checks availability and puts a confirmed appointment on the dispatch board; an AI Membership Coordinator that works the membership list and books seasonal visits without outbound calls; and Operator, an AI business manager that works across jobs, invoices and schedules. Cited contractor results include Air Design Heating and Cooling (600 maintenance members worked, 40% booking rate, $35K collected with no outreach calls), Magnolia Plumbing (invoice-to-payment time cut in half, 60 hours of admin freed per month), Mountain Valley Home Comfort (20,000 customers and 5,000 maintenance agreements migrated) and Carlsbad Heating and Cooling (four platforms consolidated into one, every call covered after hours without added staff). The system is available now for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and garage door contractors in the United States and Canada. Podium founder Eric Rea said: 'When you build the system around agents instead of workflows, the business runs differently.' This is a formal launch announcement from two weeks ago, not breaking news; filed with its stated date.
