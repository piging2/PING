## Angi killed auto-matched leads to chase quality
Angi's CEO took contractors' toughest questions head-on in a [To The Point Home Services interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hG2lwdAC7PQ) covered [September 4](https://aimgroup.com/2026/09/04/angi-ceo-kip-discusses-revamped-lead-fees-homeowner-matching/). Jeff Kip says the company eliminated auto-matched leads and cut more than $500 million in revenue to prioritize quality. Matching now runs on homeowner choice, where intent decides who gets the job.

The revamp also touched lead fees and the credit and refund process, the two complaints contractors raise loudest. Kip's repeated point is speed: the shops that answer in minutes win the work.
