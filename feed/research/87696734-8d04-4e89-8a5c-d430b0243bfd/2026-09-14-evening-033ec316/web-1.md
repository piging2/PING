## Finding 1
Mesa County updated its roofing advisory on September 13, 2026, warning that after recent hailstorms a growing number of out-of-town contractors are doing roof work across the Grand Valley without proper permits, inspections, or, inside Grand Junction city limits, a valid City contractor's license. The county says permits are required before any roofing work starts, a mid-roof inspection must happen when 10 to 40 percent of the roof is complete, and roofing contractors inside the City of Grand Junction must be licensed with the City. Skipping the mid-roof inspection can mean a portion of the roof has to be removed to allow inspection, delaying insurance claims and adding rework costs. The county advises homeowners to verify licensing, make sure the contractor pulls the permit, and have the contractor coordinate inspections with the local building department (Mesa County Building Department: mccomdev@mesacounty.us, 970-244-1631). This is not a named new business: it is the new signal that new, unvetted roofing contractors are entering the Western Slope market behind the hail damage, which matters because any legitimate new roofer filing in this window will be competing against and screened against storm-chasers.

## Finding 2
On September 9, 2026, Rescue 1 Plumbing announced an expansion into water efficiency services for residential and commercial properties in Grand Junction, Colorado. Founder Jack Thompson framed it as a commitment to local resource management and conservation amid prolonged drought conditions and rising municipal utility costs. The new offerings cover smart fixture installations, comprehensive water auditing, precision leak detection, and advanced irrigation control systems, including internet-connected systems with usage tracking, real-time leak detection, and mobile app monitoring, plus updated field-technician training on water-saving mechanisms and local plumbing code. The company says it is partnering with commercial enterprises across Mesa County to optimize facility-wide water management. Note: this is dated five days before the September 13-14 window and is an expansion of an existing regional plumbing company, not a new business filing, and it may already have been picked up in the last radar sweep. The story is filed here with its date because no fresher new-business telling surfaced and it is the most recent dated trade-business development on the Western Slope in the open record.
