## Finding 1
ServiceTitan is ending its Podium integration on September 15, 2026. Evidence grade: CONFIRMED. ServiceTitan co-founders Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan told customers in an email on Wednesday, August 12 that the company will end the Podium integration on September 15, offering discounts to switch to certified partners or suggesting they contact Podium to continue without an integration. The move affects nearly 1,000 contractors using the companies' integration, according to Podium CEO Eric Rea, who told customers in an email on Thursday, August 13 that ServiceTitan wanted a substantial revenue share and wanted Podium to agree to stop building anything competitive. ServiceTitan told Homepros in a statement it decided to sunset the integration after Podium's legacy agreement expired and Podium opted not to move forward with certification under its App Marketplace program. FieldCamp's ServiceTitan alternatives guide corroborates that in August 2026 ServiceTitan ended its Podium integration with roughly four weeks' notice, affecting an estimated 1,000 shared customers. Playbook note: shops pairing ServiceTitan as system of record with Podium for reviews, messaging and call handling must migrate or rebuild before September 15.

## Finding 2
CallRail expanded its home services ecosystem with a bidirectional integration between its AI voice assistant and Jobber. Evidence grade: CONFIRMED (company press release). CallRail describes the move as deepening its strategic partnership with Jobber, marking CallRail's first bidirectional integration between Voice Assist, described as CallRail's AI voice assistant with real-time lead qualification and intelligent transfer capabilities, and a field service management platform. Capabilities listed: live booking and real-time availability checks where Voice Assist can check Jobber calendar availability in real time and book a job appointment directly during the call; bidirectional data sync where caller data, call recordings, transcripts, and Voice Assist-collected intake fields automatically sync to Jobber client records; personalized returning caller recognition where Voice Assist can recognize returning Jobber clients, greet them by name, and skip redundant intake questions. The same announcement launched first-time integrations with JobNimbus, JobTread, and Workiz, which create leads on each platform from calls, texts, or form submissions and let Voice Assist pull customer information into live calls. CallRail says all four integrations are now available to its more than 10,000 home service customers. Playbook note: this is the documented AI-answering plus Jobber stack pairing to recommend over generic Zapier bridges when live booking matters.

## Finding 3
Smith.ai documents a direct integration with Housecall Pro for 24/7 call answering. Evidence grade: CONFIRMED (vendor integration page). The page states Housecall Pro is business management software for home services businesses like carpet cleaners, HVAC technicians, and plumbers, and says you won't miss a call when you're using Smith.ai receptionists, whether AI-first or human-first, to answer on behalf of your business. With the Housecall Pro integration, inbound callers are mapped to existing or new client records, so call summaries and appointment bookings can be automatically logged directly in the Housecall Pro account without lifting a finger. The page states Smith.ai is trusted by 5,000+ businesses. The page states no publication date. Playbook note: this is the standing vendor-supported AI-answering pairing for Housecall Pro shops.

## Finding 4
An independent 2026 guide details how AI receptionist layers connect to Jobber and Housecall Pro by plan tier. Evidence grade: STRONG INDICATION (single-author guide citing vendor pricing pages from June 2026). The guide states Jobber and Housecall Pro handle scheduling, invoicing, and payments but neither answers the phone or captures leads after hours, so the AI layer picks up the call, qualifies it, and writes a structured job into the FSM while the FSM stays the system of record. For Jobber: Core, Connect, and Grow all reach the Jobber App Marketplace and Zapier, so an AI agent can create jobs, log notes, and push intake without custom code; a guided custom API setup is a Plus-tier feature at $699 per month. For Housecall Pro: the MAX plan at $329 per month opens both Zapier and Housecall Pro's open API, letting an AI agent create jobs and fire follow-ups when a job status changes; on Basic and Essentials shops bridge through Zapier instead, which still covers job creation and status-based triggers. The page states no publication date. Playbook note: most Western Colorado shops can pair AI answering via Zapier or Make without needing top-tier API plans.
