## What a virtual receptionist really costs in 2026
One [pricing guide](https://www.virtustant.com/blog/virtual-receptionist-pricing) puts AI receptionists at $14 to $199 a month, live answering at $400 to $900 at realistic volume, and a dedicated remote at about $1,120 a month. The keeper is the break-even: at a $3.30 overage rate, roughly five and a half hours of talk time already costs as much as that dedicated seat, before booking, CRM updates, or follow-up get counted.
