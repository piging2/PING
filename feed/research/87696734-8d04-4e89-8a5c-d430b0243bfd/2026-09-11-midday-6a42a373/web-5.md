## Finding 2
Mesa County has posted an advisory telling Grand Valley property owners that permits and inspections are required for roof replacements following recent hailstorms. The county says it is seeing a growing number of out-of-town contractors starting roof work without proper permits, inspections, or, inside Grand Junction city limits, a valid city contractor's license. Homeowners are warned that skipping these requirements can delay insurance claims or force costly rework: permits must be obtained before roofing work starts, mid-roof inspections must take place when 10% to 40% of the roof is complete, and a finished roof that missed its inspection may need a portion removed for re-inspection. Before hiring, the county advises verifying the contractor's license (if in city limits), making sure they obtain the proper permit, and asking them to coordinate inspections with the local building department. Contact: Mesa County Building Department at mccomdev@mesacounty.us or 970-244-1631; City of Fruita permit requirements are handled by Fruita's building division. The page states no publication date.
