## Finding 1
Setproduct's blog guide 'The solo founder tech stack in 2026: Figma to Vercel' lays out what a complete custom build and hosting stack costs at three scales. The page itself states no publication date; the search index labeled it updated roughly four days before the September 17 reading day, so file it as recent but undated. Its guiding principle is to pick tools whose state an AI agent can read as plain text: design tokens as JSON, TypeScript types, a SQL schema file, environment files, all in the repo. The stack is Figma as the design source of truth with exported variables, Cursor as the agent pair programmer, Supabase as the whole backend (Postgres, auth, row-level security), and Vercel for deploys with preview URLs as the QA step. The cost ladder it prints: building at zero users costs about $0 a month; at 1,000 users about $60 to $90 a month; at 10,000 users about $250 or more a month. Line items at 1,000 users are Figma $16 per seat Professional, Cursor $20 a month Pro, Supabase $25 a month Pro, Vercel $20 a month Pro, Stripe at 2.9 percent plus $0.30 per transaction, Resend $0 to $20, PostHog $0 to $50, Cloudflare $0, plus about $12 a year for a domain. Vercel's specifics: Hobby is free, Pro starts at $20 per seat, and real cost grows through usage like image optimization, edge middleware invocations, and bandwidth, with $20 plus usage typically landing at $80 to $200 at 10,000 users. Two warnings carry over directly to any new site build: Supabase free-tier projects pause after seven days without activity, so the guide says to upgrade the moment a stranger can reach the signup form, and the service-role key that bypasses row-level security must never reach client code. It also advises putting marketing pages inside the Next.js app or using Framer, with Framer winning when copy needs to change without a deploy.
- https://www.setproduct.com/blog/solo-founder-tech-stack-2026 (undated)

## Finding 2
On September 11, 2026, QSR Web reported that The Big Greek Cafe, a fast-casual chain founded in 2009 by twin brothers Nikos and Simos Marmaras with locations across Maryland and Virginia, was revamping its tech infrastructure by selecting two digital restaurant platforms, Deliverect and Thanx, to unify ordering, direct delivery dispatch, third-party marketplace management, and customer loyalty. Under the new setup, Thanx powers the guest experience including the brand's mobile app and web ordering, with loyalty embedded in checkout and purchase history captured from the customer's first order so the brand can run targeted campaigns instead of blanket discounts. Behind the scenes, Deliverect's Commerce API supports the ordering backend across more than 550 point-of-sale systems, handling location-specific pricing, size-based modifiers, dayparting, and automatic prep-time adjustments during peak hours. For direct delivery without an in-house fleet, the chain deployed Deliverect Dispatch, which automatically assigns first-party website and app orders to third-party couriers based on custom routing rules, while Deliverect's order aggregation tools update pricing, limited-time offers, and out-of-stock items across DoorDash and Uber Eats simultaneously from one interface. Chief operating officer Niko Kontos said in the release, 'We were spending too much time fixing menus and working around problems instead of taking care of guests.' The stated pain points were menu fragmentation and system downtime as the chain pursues a national growth strategy.
- https://www.qsrweb.com/news/the-big-greek-cafe-partners-with-deliverect-thanx-to-streamline-digital-ordering/ (published 2026-09-11)

## Finding 3
On August 27, 2026, Builderall announced the launch of Builderall AI Hosting, a free service that takes a website generated by an AI tool like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Meta AI and turns it into a live working page in minutes. The release positions the service explicitly as an alternative to Netlify Drop, Vercel, and Cloudflare Pages, built for the small business owner rather than the developer. Users drag and drop the HTML or ZIP file their AI tool exported directly into the browser with no complicated account setup and no credit card, and the site gets a working URL on a secure Builderall subdomain. The free tier includes hosting for AI-generated static sites, the live Builderall subdomain, and a working contact form with email notifications; upgrading unlocks a custom domain, full email automation, a booking calendar, live chat and DM automation, and Leads Central, a dashboard showing every lead and the channel it came from. The launch cites 2026 Hostinger research finding that 63 percent of people using AI website and app builders have no coding background and that the average time to produce a working website draft has dropped to about 15 minutes. CEO Pedro Sostre III said, 'Netlify Drop, Vercel, and Cloudflare Pages are excellent at one job: putting a file online. None of them do anything after that. A small business owner who just spent fifteen minutes building a website with ChatGPT doesn't need a deploy pipeline. They need a contact form that works, a way to follow up with a lead, and a place to take a booking, without adding four more logins to get there.' Builderall says the product differs from AI app builders like Lovable, v0, and Bolt.new, which generate full products rather than marketing pages, and from Wix and Squarespace, which require rebuilding the site inside their own editor.

## Finding 4
On August 6, 2026, Toast (NYSE: TOST) announced an expanded integration with Google that enables AI-powered, agentic food ordering from Toast restaurants directly within Ask Maps, the conversational AI feature inside Google Maps. When a diner asks by voice or text for a restaurant recommendation or a specific food request, Ask Maps can now put a Toast restaurant's menu directly in front of them and carry the request all the way through to a completed order. Orders placed through Ask Maps flow through Toast's first-party ordering system, so operators grow order volume without adding third-party commission fees. To drive early adoption, Toast funded a promotional offer of $5 in Toast Cash toward a next order at any participating Toast restaurant for guests who order through Ask Maps, with qualifying orders at $30 or more paid online via express checkout and redemption in the Toast Local app; the promo was expected to begin in late August. The release also notes that Toast is a co-developer of the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) for Food, an open standard for how AI agents discover restaurants, order from them, and check out across surfaces like Search, Maps, and Gemini, so restaurants stay in control of their sales, menu, pricing, and guest relationships. Chief marketing officer and chief operating officer of enterprise Kelly Esten said, 'Now, guests can go from searching within Google Maps to ordering from a Toast restaurant in one seamless motion, surfacing our customers' menus at the exact moment the guest is deciding what to order.'
