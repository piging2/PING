## Finding 1
CONFIRMED (Quo's own documentation): Quo ships missed-call text-back as built-in auto-replies on every plan, with six trigger conditions: missed call with voicemail during business hours, missed call without voicemail during business hours, incoming text during business hours, and the same three after business hours. Quo sends only one auto-reply every three hours to the same contact, so repeat callers are not spammed. Shared numbers mean every team member is notified of incoming calls and texts, and Quo's Sona AI voice agent can answer calls during or after hours, answer common questions, take messages 24/7, log and transcribe calls with summaries, and send SMS during a live call (for example texting a caller a booking link so they can self-schedule). Quo's call views let a shop filter missed calls and calls handled by Sona to prioritize follow-up. The page itself states no publication date, though search results showed it was last updated roughly two weeks before September 17, 2026. This matters for PING Social because missed-call text-back is a core PING pitch and Quo already covers it natively, so the pitch to a shop on Quo is the layer on top: two-way follow-up, scheduling, and CRM-grade capture rather than the basic auto-reply.

Quo's Sona AI voice agent includes 1,000 free credits per month (about 10 AI-handled calls) on all plans, with paid credit tiers starting around $25 per month for 40 calls. TechRepublic's August 4, 2026 buyer's guide names Quo best for startups, citing shared inboxes, team threads, and integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Zapier, Make, Google Contacts, and Jobber. ## Finding 3
CONFIRMED (company press release dated September 10, 2026): Intermedia launched a native AI Receptionist, an AI voice agent that answers inbound calls with natural conversation, uses business-specific knowledge (FAQs, documents, web content) to answer questions, understands caller intent, transfers to configured destinations, and hands an AI-generated recap to the human who receives an escalated call. It covers after-hours and overflow calls, is configured through a no-code AI Builder, and works with Intermedia Unite and Intermedia Contact Center in the US, UK, and Canada. It is in open beta with general availability expected in October 2026.
