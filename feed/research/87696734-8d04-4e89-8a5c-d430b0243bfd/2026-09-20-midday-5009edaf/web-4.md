## Finding 1
EstimateKit released a free HVAC Labor Rate Calculator aimed at small shops running two- and three-truck operations, per ACHR News reporting published September 18, 2026. The calculator takes a shop's technician wages, labor burden, fixed overhead, billable hour efficiency, and target net margin, and returns the hourly billing rate required to hit that margin. It is free, requires no account, and runs in a browser on a phone or desktop. Founder Travis Paivarinta said every tool he looked at was either built and priced for shops running twenty-plus trucks or was cheap and did not do the job, and that his friends in two- and three-truck operations were doing this math on the back of an invoice or not doing it at all. The release frames underpriced labor as one of the most common and least visible margin problems in residential service: a shop can carry a healthy gross margin on equipment and still lose money on service calls because its labor rate was set years ago and never rebuilt as wages, insurance, and truck costs moved. No paid plan or pricing for EstimateKit itself was stated; the calculator itself is free.
