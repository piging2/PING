## Mesa County fights plan to reroute local mail through Denver
Mesa County's commissioners have asked USPS to reconsider a plan to reroute Grand Junction's mail processing through Denver. Per the county's [letter](https://www.mesacounty.us/news/commissioners/county-writes-usps-over-concerning-mail-system-changes), the detour adds 500 miles round trip to every piece of local mail, and mountain terrain plus frequent I-70 closures would compound the delays. Medication deliveries, mail-in voting, and the Clerk and Recorder's document recording and vehicle registrations would all slow.
