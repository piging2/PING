## Finding 1
Two longtime Grand Junction True Value hardware stores have gone fully independent and rebranded as Bob's Hometown Hardware. Mark and Jeannette Neal, co-owners of the Orchard Mesa store at 2686 U.S. Highway 50 and the Grand Junction store at 1838 N. 12th St., ended the stores' association with True Value and put up the new name in August; the rebrand was announced publicly at the 12th Street location on Aug. 21 when the new sign was installed. The move follows True Value's Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing and acquisition by Do It Best in fall 2024, which freed the stores from True Value's purchasing requirements and operational constraints. The stores are named for Bob Frederick, Jeannette Neal's father, who opened the Orchard Mesa store in 1983 and the 12th Street store in 1991. All employees were retained, and the stores keep offering the same in-store services, including key cutting (the article's lead photo shows an employee at the key machine). New for the independent stores: a 'Bob's Bucks' rewards program they fully control, and a new website (not yet operational at an Aug. 25 interview) that will allow online ordering and payment with in-store or curbside pickup. This is a rebrand of existing stores to full independence, not a new SOS filing; it is appearing in the Business Times' current September 15, 2026 edition, and the article page itself states no publication date.

## Finding 2
Rescue 1 Plumbing, a regional plumbing company operating in Grand Junction, Colorado, announced September 9, 2026 that it has expanded into water-efficiency services for residential and commercial properties. New offerings include smart fixture installations, comprehensive water auditing, precision leak detection, and advanced irrigation control systems. Founder Jack Thompson is quoted framing the expansion as a commitment to local resource management amid prolonged drought and rising municipal utility costs. The announcement came as a syndicated press release (PressAdvantage, carried on content-syndication domains), so treat the company-provided framing accordingly. This is a service-line expansion by an existing operator, not a new business filing or opening, but it is a dated, within-window trade-business development in Grand Junction worth the radar's attention.
