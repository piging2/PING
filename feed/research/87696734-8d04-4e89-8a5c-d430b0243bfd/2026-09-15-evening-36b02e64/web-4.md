## Finding 1
VoiceAIWrapper, a white-label AI voice agent platform for agencies, added a guided five-step AI Agent Builder on September 15, 2026. Agencies can build a ready-to-call voice agent on Vapi, Retell AI, or ElevenLabs from an industry template or by scanning the client's website, without writing prompts; a client portal setting lets agencies give selected clients access to build agents inside the agency-branded portal. Agents launch with three tools: appointment booking through Cal.com with live availability checks, call transfer to a human, and lead capture to a webhook. Platform claims from the release (evidence grade: CONFIRMED only as vendor-stated): one dashboard across five voice AI providers (Vapi, Retell AI, ElevenLabs Agents, Bolna, Ultravox); integrations with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Cal.com, Calendly, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Airtable, Twilio, Telnyx, Zadarma, Make.com, and n8n; billing through Stripe at agency-set prices with no revenue share taken. Pricing: Growth $79/month for five client accounts, Scale $249/month for 10, Pro $499/month for 20, with a 7-day trial requiring a card. Stack-relevance for the playbook: a credible turnkey white-label path for PING Social to resell voice agents to contractors without building the plumbing, though real-world pricing to end clients and call-quality are UNVERIFIED beyond the vendor's own copy.

## Finding 2
Intermedia Intelligent Communications launched AI Receptionist, a native AI voice agent built into its voice and contact-center platform, on September 10, 2026. The agent answers inbound calls conversationally, draws answers from a business knowledge base built from FAQs, documents, and web content, transfers callers to configured destinations with an AI-generated recap at handoff, and provides after-hours and overflow coverage. Setup is no-code: admins pick a voice, style, and name, add business details, and set transfer rules and escalation guardrails. It is compatible with Intermedia Unite and Intermedia Contact Center, and available to eligible customers in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. Status: Open Beta at launch, with General Availability expected in October 2026. Vendor claims (CONFIRMED as vendor-stated): more than 150,000 business customers and over 7,500 channel partners on the platform; a partner reseller model (CORE) that lets partners package the product as their own. Playbook angle: Intermedia is a telco-first native voice AI, so a prospect's existing phone provider may now be a competing or partnering voice-agent vendor; pricing was not disclosed.

## Finding 3
Yelp integrated OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 voice model into its two AI business tools, Yelp Host and Hatch, in a press release carrying a September 9, 2026 BusinessWire stamp (the rehost page states no publication date). GPT-Live-1 is OpenAI's latest voice model with a full-duplex architecture that handles interruptions, pauses, and overlapping speech; Yelp layers its own business data and language enhancements on top, including automatic detection of callers in nearly any language and tone detection (excitement, frustration, impatience). Hatch is the directly relevant playbook competitor: a Yelp company and AI communications platform for service businesses, founded 2018 and joined Yelp in 2026. Its voice AI diagnoses the issue, verifies service area and job details, and books appointments using real-time availability; the release says Hatch manages tens of millions of leads across voice, text, email, and web, representing billions of dollars of value attributed to customers annually (all vendor-stated, CONFIRMED only as claimed). Yelp Host, for restaurants, launched October 2025 and has handled more than 1 million calls. Hatch CEO Chris Bache's line from the release: the voice agent 'understands how service businesses operate, from technician availability and service areas to scheduling rules and emergency routing.' Playbook takeaway: the best-funded home-services voice AI just got a better voice; PING Social pitches should lead on workflow fit and owner control rather than voice quality.
