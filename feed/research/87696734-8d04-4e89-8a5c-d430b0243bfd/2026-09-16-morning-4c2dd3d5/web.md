## ServicePower adds AI image review to its field app
Technicians now get a pass, marginal, or fail verdict on job-site photos before they leave the site. [ServicePower](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/servicepower-expands-mobile-fsm-with-ai-powered-visual-intelligence-for-field-service-workers-302875907.html) folded Vision AI into its mobile field app, checking each image against the customer's own quality thresholds while the tech is still standing in front of the work, even with limited connectivity, and the worker keeps the final call. The pitch from chief product officer Reza Hemmati: "The hardest call in field service is the one a technician makes standing in front of the work, usually alone."
