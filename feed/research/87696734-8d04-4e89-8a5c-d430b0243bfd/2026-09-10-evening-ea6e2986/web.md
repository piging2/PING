## Phony.ai launches open-stack AI phone agents
[Phony.ai](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-10-phonyai-launches-provider-neutral-ai-phone-and-voice-agent-platform) [launched its AI phone-agent platform today](https://business.am-news.com/am-news/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-10-phonyai-launches-provider-neutral-ai-phone-and-voice-agent-platform) on an open-stack pitch: you pick the carrier, the speech-to-text, the language model, and the voice behind every agent. Home services is on its target list, one account can run agents for multiple clients, and talk time costs $0.12 a minute with telephony billed at carrier cost.

The consent controls answer the objection you heard this week: outbound calls only go out on an established basis, with do-not-call screening and quiet hours built in. Nate Nead's pitch line is that "a good opportunity doesn't disappear because nobody answered at 8:30 at night."
