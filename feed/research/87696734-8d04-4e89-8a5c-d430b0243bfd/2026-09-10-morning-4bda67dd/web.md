## Facebook now ranks watch time over likes
Business pages averaged 2 to 5 percent organic reach in 2025, and the 2026 shift redistributes what is left around deeper signals like watch time and real discussion ([Whizsky](https://www.whizsky.com/news/facebook-2026-reach-rules)). Facebook now weights time spent viewing, saves, profile visits after a view, and its own viewer surveys asking if a post was worth their time, while posts labeled too promotional get suppressed. For your passes that means a comment thread beats a burst of reactions, and [original Reels held past 60 percent watch time](https://gyre.pro/blog/facebook-algorithm-tips-on-how-to-increase-audience-reach) are the ones that earn a second wave of distribution.
