## Yelp puts OpenAI's newest voice model in its service-business AI
Yelp [announced September 9](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260909348516/en/Yelp-and-Hatch-Advance-Voice-AI-for-Restaurants-and-Service-Pros-with-OpenAIs-GPT-Live-1) that it has folded OpenAI's newest voice model, GPT-Live-1, into Hatch, its AI platform for service businesses. The upgrade brings full-duplex conversation to the voice agent. Callers can interrupt, change topics mid-sentence, or talk to someone in the background, and the AI adapts, reads tone, and answers in nearly any language.

Hatch says the platform manages tens of millions of leads across voice, text, email, and web. The telling line is the CEO's own: great voice AI takes more than a great voice model, which is the outcomes-first argument your own pitch already runs on.
