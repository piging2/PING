## Finding 1
Scope Technologies and Leap CRM announced a strategic integration that connects RoofScope measurement reports directly into Leap workflows for roofing contractors. Contractors can order RoofScope, RoofScopeX, and RoofScope+ reports within Leap based on workflow assignment and import complete RoofScope measurement data directly into Leap without manual re-entry. The companies position the integration as faster estimating with fewer measurement discrepancies, improved material ordering accuracy, stronger documentation for insurance workflows, and standardized estimating across multi-branch operations. Scope Technologies CEO Jerod Raisch said, "Contractors don't need another disconnected tool, they need a connected workflow," adding that measurement accuracy becomes transformational when it flows into estimating and job management. Leap CEO Patrick Fingles said the partnership brings best-in-class aerial measurement data directly into Leap's CRM, eliminating manual entry and giving customers an end-to-end workflow from measurement to signed contract in one place. The announcement page states no publication date and describes the integration as available now. Search results placed the announcement about 51 days before September 9, 2026.

## Finding 2
AccuLynx published its Spring 2026 product updates on March 31, 2026, last updated on June 16, 2026, with three changes to its estimation tool for roofing contractors. Smart Fields can now pull data from multiple estimates rather than just the primary one, enabling Good, Better, Best pricing presentations. Users can offer a true lump sum estimate by hiding or removing the tax line item from display. Templates can automatically calculate labor rates based on roof pitch. The same release added a Custom Fields Manager for contacts and jobs, appointment outcome tracking with an Appointments Report, and a redesigned lead form with duplicate detection and side-by-side calendar views. The release notes describe no new AI features in this update.
- https://acculynx.com/spring-2026-product-updates/ (published 2026-03-31)

## Finding 3
Trimble added AI capabilities to its MEP estimating software, described in a report dated July 9, 2026, covering an announcement from late June from its Westminster, Colorado headquarters. The update automates four tasks: identifying and setting drawing scale and sheet names across plan sets, recognizing, labeling and counting symbols such as receptacles, switches and light fixtures, calculating conduit lengths automatically including vertical rises and drops through auto-routing, and an AI Smart Assistant embedded in Accubid Anywhere that lets estimators query estimate data in natural language for historical pricing research and estimate version comparison. Trimble reports up to 60 percent time savings on the specific pre-takeoff and takeoff tasks affected, and average savings above 80 percent on the assistant's pricing and comparison queries. The company reports more than 4,000 contractors already using AI within its MEP estimating solutions and more than three million symbols detected automatically to date. The tools are framed as human-in-the-loop, with estimators validating outputs through their own quality control.
- https://highways.today/2026/07/09/trimble-ai-takeoff-tools/ (published 2026-07-09)
