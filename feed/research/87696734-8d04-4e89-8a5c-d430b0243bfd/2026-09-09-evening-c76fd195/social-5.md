## Finding 1
IDK Coffee, a coffee shop posting from the account @idk.coffeetsv (3K followers) on September 9, 2026, announced it is phasing out its classic '10th coffee free' punch-card reward in favor of a $6.50 loyalty credit usable store-wide. The owner-facing post says the change is driven by increased fuel, stock, wage, and payment processing costs, and it also removes discounts from food items. The caption frames the shop's growth from a 57 square meter space since 2023 and thanks customers for supporting the small business and its staff. The tactic is a real owner example of re-pricing loyalty so the reward still drives repeat visits while protecting margin: a flat credit redeemable on anything instead of a free coffee, announced transparently with the cost pressures named.
- https://www.instagram.com/p/DdFqGLup7Le/ (published 2026-09-09)

## Finding 2
Kassy Braganza, owner of Hey Lucky Cafe & Co., shared on Facebook on September 3, 2026 that her cafe now closes mostly at 5PM, gives away complimentary coffee, and makes more money than when it stayed open until 10PM. According to the post's caption, the change was not about selling more coffee but about being obsessed with knowing customers: gathering feedback, keeping a continuous conversation with the community, and prioritizing hospitality. The reel positions shorter hours plus free coffee plus deliberate customer knowledge as the combination that lifted revenue. This is an owner-attributed result rather than a vendor pitch, and it landed with her 5K-follower audience.
- https://www.facebook.com/reel/1103092418784616/ (published 2026-09-03)

## Finding 3
Dear Grace, Coffee Co., a drive-thru coffee business in Texas posting from @deargrace.coffeeco (25K followers), announced on September 9, 2026 a 'DOUBLE PUNCH THURSDAY' promotion running the next day, September 10, 2026. The post says pre-orders run 6 to 7am and walk-ins start at 7am, and customers should bring their loyalty card for an extra punch. The card itself, held up in the photo, reads 'Drive Thru More Drink More' and says 'Each visit is on us! Thank you for being apart of our loyalty program!' The post lists locations in Edcouch, Weslaco, and San Antonio, Texas. The tactic is a weekday traffic-builder: doubling the stamp rate on a slow morning to pull the same regulars in one extra time.
- https://www.instagram.com/p/DdD4g7xsnTg/ (published 2026-09-09)

## Finding 4
Starsi, the owner of Five Starz Maltby coffee shop in Maltby, posted a Facebook reel on September 8, 2026 laying out his repeat-visit playbook seven to eight months after opening. He is launching a loyalty card (holding one up in the video) and says a forthcoming website will carry a QR code offering a free drink on the tenth purchase. He also previews leaflets around the village and seasonal hot drinks for colder weather. The shop is closed on Tuesdays, he says customer feedback has exceeded his expectations, and the comment thread is uniformly supportive, with commenters saying they travel from neighboring towns like Dinnington and calling it 'the best coffee shop for miles.' This is an owner tying the physical stamp card, a simple website QR offer, and seasonal menu rotation into one retention push, with community comments serving as the social proof.
- https://www.facebook.com/reel/1676261877213253/ (published 2026-09-08)
