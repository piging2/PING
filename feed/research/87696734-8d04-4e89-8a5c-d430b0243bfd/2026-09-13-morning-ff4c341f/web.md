## Home services grew Q2 revenue through pricing discipline
[Jobber's Home Service Economic Report for Q2 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/home-service-delivers-steady-revenue-growth-in-q2-2026-proving-its-resilience-through-a-volatile-quarter-302856785.html), published August 20, found home service revenue growing through pricing discipline rather than volume. Every major segment was back to growth by June: Green at 7.5% and Construction at 9.1% year over year.

A tight labor market kept construction employment nearly flat, so shops grew by invoicing better, not hiring more. Digital payments rose 6% year over year, which the report ties to stronger cash flow on overdue invoices. The line worth borrowing is Jobber CEO Sam Pillar's: businesses priced their work well instead of chasing more jobs, and it paid off.
