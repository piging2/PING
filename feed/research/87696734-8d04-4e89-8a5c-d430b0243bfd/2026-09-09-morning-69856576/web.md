## Contractors' real reviews are vanishing in Google's 2026 crackdown
Your prospects' review counts can fall off a cliff overnight, and right now it may not be their fault. A March algorithm update [retroactively pulled reviews](https://thevalleymarketinggroup.com/blog/google-reviews-deleted-service-business-2026/) gathered through now-banned methods, and an April policy formalized the bans. The banned list was standard practice for years: on-site kiosks, incentives, review gating, and asking customers to name staff.

Profiles under 3 stars lose 97% of local searchers, and the rating feeds Local Services Ads ranking and cost per lead. What survives is a simple workflow: text or email 24 to 48 hours after the job, from the customer's own device, personalized, steady across every job instead of batched. That is a follow-up sequence you can automate, and a prospect whose reviews just disappeared is a prospect who will take the call.
