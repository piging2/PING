## Thumbtack Pro: costs, risks, and the worth-it test
The whole test is one line: put the bill beside the jobs. That is the verdict of PipelineOn's [Thumbtack Pro review](https://pipelineon.com/blog/thumbtack-pro-review/), updated two days ago (the company sells website visitor-identification software). Its worked example counts the full acquisition cost, not just the price per lead, and the page stresses these are example numbers, not averages.

The controls to set before a shop enables leads: lead-price and weekly budgets per service, what triggers a charge (ignoring a request does not avoid the fee), the current refund rules, and a pause point for a full schedule. Two shops can pay the same lead price and reach opposite conclusions, and raising the budget will not fix unanswered calls.
