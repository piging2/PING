## Finding 1
Mesa County is holding its Workforce Summit 2026 on Monday, September 21, 2026, from 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. at the Mesa County Workforce Center, aimed at employers dealing with hiring and workforce development. Tickets are $30 per person and include breakfast, snacks, and a workbook. The event centers on skills-first hiring: an interactive workshop covers skills mapping for priority roles, writing skills-first job descriptions, and skills-first screening and interviewing. The page states no publication date; the event itself is dated Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Finding 2
Jordan and Maddison Burge officially took ownership of Graff Dairy, the drive-thru ice cream shop on 29 Road in Grand Junction, on September 1, 2026. Previous owner Darin Carei put the business up for sale in March 2026; he still owns the real estate while the Burges bought the business itself. Their announced plans keep the nostalgic look, including the vintage cars, while adding coffee with earlier 7 a.m. openings, refreshers and other Red Bull-infused drinks, self-ordering kiosks, and new screens; customers can still order from employees, and the owners are soliciting menu ideas from the community via their Facebook page. The story was reported by the Grand Junction Business Times and summarized by 103.7 The River; the page states no publication date (its imagery is dated September 2026). The lead image shows the Graff Dairy storefront via Google Street.
- https://1037theriver.com/ixp/507/p/colorado-graff-dairy-new-ownership/ (undated)
