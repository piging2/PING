## Finding 1
T3 Services Group, an Austin-based group of residential HVAC, plumbing and electrical companies, announced on Sept. 17, 2026 that it has acquired Element Home Services. Element, founded in 2016, provides around-the-clock residential plumbing, HVAC and electrical repair, maintenance and replacement services through four branches: Jackson Hole, Wyoming; Durango, Colorado; St. George, Utah; and Prescott, Arizona. With the addition, T3 now includes eight partner companies across seven states. Element will keep its established brand and local leadership, with T3 providing resources across technology, marketing, recruiting, training and operations. T3 CEO Joe Finney said the partnership meaningfully expands T3's Mountain West presence; Element co-founder Jon Graham said T3's resources will create more opportunities for the team and bring the Element standard to more homeowners.

## Finding 2
Colorado is beginning its seasonal transition into fall, according to National Weather Service Boulder meteorologist Anya Aponte in a Sept. 15, 2026 report. She said temperatures are settling into the 70s and 80s with no 90-degree days in the foreseeable future, after six days above 90 already this month pushed September's average about five degrees above normal. The change will be sharpest at elevation: temperatures on Colorado's highest peaks could dip into the 30s over the next few days with possible dustings and a rain-snow mix on the 14ers. Southwestern Colorado faces the wettest turn, with the San Juan Mountains and surrounding areas given a good chance of heavy rainfall beginning Thursday night and continuing through at least Saturday. For Western Slope home-service demand, the signal is the start of heating season: colder nights arriving after an anomalously warm start to September.
