## Finding 1
ElevenLabs launched Reception, an AI agent that answers calls for small businesses, on Wednesday September 16, 2026. It works around the clock in more than 70 languages including English and Spanish, answers questions, books jobs, and schedules appointments straight into the business calendar, with call summaries, transcripts, and recordings available afterward. Pricing is a free 14-day trial, then Basic at $29 a month, Plus at $79 a month, and Premium at $199 a month, plus tax. Each plan includes a set U.S. phone number as an option to separate the AI and human lines, and businesses can set it to answer when staff cannot pick up or after closing. The article explicitly names plumbers as a use case: fielding calls while the plumber is on a job. Reception is built on ElevenAgents, ElevenLabs' conversational AI platform for voice, chat, and email agents. The launch follows ElevenLabs raising a $500 million Series D led by Sequoia Capital, bringing its valuation to $11 billion. Inc. notes the competitive field: Zoom launched its own AI receptionist in July 2026, and RingCentral, Yelp, Jobber, Goodcall, Smith.ai, and My AI Front Desk offer similar tools. The story was reported by Inc. one day before the reading day; the Inc. page itself states no publication date.

## Finding 2
Primare AI, a 2026-founded company headquartered in Austin, Texas, announced Primare P1 on September 17, 2026: a physical appliance installed at the client's premises that arrives staffed with eight virtual employees, including a 24-hour receptionist, a sales assistant, an executive officer, a business analyst, a marketing manager, a reputation manager, a market researcher, and a recruiter. The receptionist answers phone calls, texts, emails, web chat, and Instagram and Facebook direct messages around the clock using the business's own service descriptions, policies, and terminology. The pitch is ownership rather than rental: the client buys the hardware outright, data stays on the premises, open-weight models run locally so operating cost does not change with volume, and nothing is used for model training. Primare installs the appliance on site in one day and maintains it under an ongoing service contract that the client can end at any time while keeping the data; a higher-capacity configuration, P1 Max, is also available. P1 and P1 Max are available to order now in the United States, with installation typically scheduled within two to four weeks; configuration is set during a discovery call bookable at primare.ai/book or 1-888-299-1990. Primare serves small and mid-sized businesses in home services, legal, financial services, real estate, and automotive.

## Finding 3
WePro, a Houston-based field service management company, announced its AI-powered dispatch platform for home service businesses on September 18, 2026. The platform combines job scheduling and dispatch, live technician tracking, automated customer updates, and payments and invoicing tied to job completion in a single dashboard, distinguishing itself from basic calendar or CRM tools that leave routing, live status, and billing as separate manual steps. Its AI dispatching engine matches each job to a technician based on skill, availability, and location, and adjusts in real time as new jobs and delays come in. Spokesperson Sam Rottman framed manual scheduling as a hidden cost: the announcement cites an average of 15 wasted hours per week per dispatcher and up to 25 percent added to vehicle operating expenses from poorly planned routes. WePro is built for home service teams including cleaning, roofing, HVAC, and other field-based businesses, and interested companies can request a demo at wepro.ai. ## Finding 4
Angi (formerly Angie's List, NASDAQ: ANGI) announced September 16, 2026 that it is participating in OpenAI's Sponsored Agent pilot, making it one of the first brands to test sponsored AI agents inside ChatGPT. In the pilot, an Angi-branded sponsored agent may appear in relevant ChatGPT conversations about home services; homeowners can continue the conversation with the agent, share project details, and when the job needs a pro, enter Angi's service request flow to connect with skilled local pros. Unlike Angi's existing ChatGPT app, which homeowners choose to add, the sponsored agent appears as a clearly labeled sponsored experience inside conversations homeowners are already having. Co-founder Angie Hicks framed it as an AI-first strategy: homeowners go directly from discussing a home project in ChatGPT to connecting with a skilled local pro. This is a new lead-generation surface for home service businesses rather than a front-desk product: it changes where homeowners meet pros, through ChatGPT instead of search or ads.
