## Scaffold raises $15M to connect trade contractors' systems
D.R. Horton and Pulte Homes just bought into the thesis you sell, backing [Scaffold](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/15/3362272/0/en/scaffold-raises-15-million-to-connect-the-systems-that-run-residential-construction.html)'s $15M [seed round](https://www.finsmes.com/2026/09/scaffold-raises-15m-in-seed-funding.html) led by Navitas Capital. The Austin startup automates coordination between trade contractors and homebuilders, keeping one current record of every job across the portals contractors already use. Nobody has to learn new software or retrain a crew, and the company claims roughly 50% fewer dry runs and 89% fewer errors in the first 60 days.
