## AerialReports plugs roof measurements into its CRM
One measurement now runs the whole job. AerialReports [announced](https://lifestyle.operation-infinitejustice.org/story/336163/aerialreports-expands-contractor-os-to-connect-roof-measurements-crm-estimating-proposals-and-production-for-roofing-contractors/) September 18 that its expanded Contractor OS carries roof and exterior measurements through the full chain: leads, estimating, proposals, e-signatures, payments, scheduling, and production. The company calls it From Click to Close, and the payoff is one customer-and-property record instead of repeated data entry, plus an interactive 3D model and a no-credit-card trial.
