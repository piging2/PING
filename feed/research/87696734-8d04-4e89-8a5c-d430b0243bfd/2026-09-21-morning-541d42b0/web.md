## Google Business Profile lets you review and delete collected info
Google's assistant has been calling and texting shops to confirm hours and services, and those answers can land on the listing. The new tab lives under Edit profile → Collected info, where you can see each item's source and date and delete the outdated ones, per [Search Engine Watch](https://searchenginewatch.com/google-business-profile-now-lets-you-review-and-delete-collected-info/). Deleting the record does not fix the live field itself, so correct what customers see separately, as [Outrank's walkthrough](https://www.outrank.co.uk/latest-news/google-business-profiles-introduces-collected-info-what-businesses-need-to-know/) notes.
