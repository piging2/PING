## Finding 1
Pest Away Spraying of Delta, Colorado is a locally owned and operated full-service pest control company that says it has served Delta and Montrose Counties since 1983, covering Montrose, Olathe, Delta, Surface Creek, and the North Fork. Its one-page website is a bare template-style build with observable gaps: there is no online booking or quote request form anywhere on the page, the only ways to reach them are two phone numbers, (970) 874-4841 for the Delta area and (970) 249-3353 for the Montrose area, the contact email is an AOL address, pestawayspraying@aol.com, and the page carries no customer reviews or testimonials, only service lists for pest control, lawns, weeds, and tree spraying. A business operating since 1983 that still runs on phone-only contact and an AOL address matches Nolan's very-non-technical-owner revamp trigger closely, and it is a pest control company rather than a plumber, so it sits outside the September 12 plumber-website teardown.

## Finding 2

## Finding 3
Canyon Creek Electric of Montrose, Colorado describes itself as a commercial design-build, family-owned electrical contractor serving Montrose and Colorado's Western Slope since 2007, with over 25 years in the trade. The site shows clear age and neglect: an HTTPS request resolves to the plain-HTTP page with no redirect, so the business site loads over a non-secure connection in 2026, and the homepage's own photos and page assets carry 2020 timestamps with no sign of updates since. The 'Real Clients, Real Testimonials' wall includes a lukewarm review from 'Linda S.' of Montrose, who writes that the repair was a simple breaker reset, the electrician was there only five minutes, 'Charge was still $100,' and she rates customer service 4.0. There is no online booking or quote form visible on the page. A family-owned trade contractor whose site has no HTTPS, displays stale 2020 content, and showcases a complaint about a $100 house-call charge is a natural website-revamp conversation.
