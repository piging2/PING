## Finding 1
Rescue 1 Plumbing, a plumbing company serving Grand Junction, CO and surrounding areas, announced on September 9, 2026 that it is expanding into water-efficiency services for residential and commercial properties. The new offerings include smart fixture installations, comprehensive water auditing, precision leak detection, and advanced irrigation control systems, including internet-connected smart plumbing with automated usage tracking and real-time leak detection monitored via mobile apps. Company founder Jack Thompson said the expansion reflects a commitment to local water resource management amid drought conditions and rising municipal utility costs. The release notes the company is partnering with commercial enterprises throughout Mesa County and updating its technician training curriculum on water-saving mechanisms and local plumbing code. Contact: (970) 812-1715, info@rescueoneplumbing.com. Note: this is a company-issued press release (via Press Advantage), not independent reporting, so treat the claims as the company's own statements.

## Finding 2
