## Finding 1
PatchMaster, a national drywall repair and interior painting franchise, opened PatchMaster Grand Junction on August 31, 2026. The locally owned franchise is owned and operated by Michael Bilger, a construction veteran with more than 20 years of experience in architecture, construction, and project management (seven years in an architecture office, 14 years as a construction and project manager). Services cover drywall repair, ceiling repair, drywall installation, texture matching, and interior painting for homeowners and businesses. The stated service area spans Grand Junction and the Grand Valley plus Rifle, New Castle, Glenwood Springs, Carbondale, Aspen, Crested Butte, Gunnison, Montrose, Delta, Telluride, Ouray, and Moab, Utah. Bilger is an owner-operator involved in day-to-day operations, with community ties including BNI and the Redlands Lions Club. The story dates to August 31, 2026, about twelve days before the reading day, so it is filed as a recent opening rather than breaking news.

## Finding 2
Rescue 1 Plumbing, a regional plumbing provider serving Grand Junction and surrounding areas, announced on September 9, 2026 that it has expanded its operations with water-efficiency services for residential and commercial properties. New offerings include smart fixture installations, comprehensive water auditing, precision leak detection, and advanced irrigation control systems, including internet-connected leak detection with mobile-app monitoring. Founder Jack Thompson framed the expansion as a commitment to local resource management amid Colorado drought conditions and rising municipal utility costs. The company says the average area household can prevent the loss of thousands of gallons of water annually, and cites EPA benchmark data that water-efficient fixtures can cut total consumption by up to 30 percent. The company is also partnering with commercial enterprises throughout Mesa County on facility-wide water management. Filed as an expansion signal for an existing Western Slope trade business, dated September 9, 2026, within the last 72 hours of the reading day.
