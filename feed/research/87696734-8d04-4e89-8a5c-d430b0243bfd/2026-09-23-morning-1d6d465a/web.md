## 89% of small businesses say AI is paying off
Owners who run AI on scheduling and payroll together save an average of 7.7 hours and $343 a month, per [Homebase's 2026 Main Street AI Gap Report](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-22-new-homebase-data-89-of-small-businesses-say-ai-is-paying-off-saving-them-up-to-4100-a-year) of 750 small business owners, released Tuesday. The gap underneath is the opening: adoption runs 81% at $5 million in revenue but 54% under $500,000, and nearly two-thirds of the holdouts say they are still interested, with trust the thing holding them back.
