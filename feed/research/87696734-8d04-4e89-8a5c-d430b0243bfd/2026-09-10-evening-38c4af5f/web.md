## Google starts billing missed LSA calls October 1
A call nobody answers is about to land on the contractor's ad invoice. Google's new Local Services Ads policy counts any caller who stays on the line more than 20 seconds during business hours as a billable lead, even with no conversation ([Mediagistic](https://www.mediagistic.com/blog/glsa-lead-charge-policy-2026)).

The one carve-out is IVR, where the 20-second clock starts only after the caller presses a key. Missed calls also feed the responsiveness signal in Google's LSA ranking, so one unanswered ring can raise the bill and sink the ad at once.
