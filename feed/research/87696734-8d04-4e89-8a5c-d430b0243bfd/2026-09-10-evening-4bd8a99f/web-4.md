## Finding 1
Podium, the reviews and messaging company founded in 2014, formally announced its AI Operating System for Home Services, a platform that puts AI agents to work across field service management, communications, and membership tools. The platform has been live since early 2026 and Podium says hundreds of contractors have already moved off legacy FSM software onto it. The announcement is about two weeks old: the search listing showed it last updated roughly 14 days before the reading day, while the page itself states no explicit publication date, so it sits just outside the 7-day window but is new to this reader and was not covered by the paper's earlier ServiceTitan, Dispatchly, or Housecall Pro items. Three agents are named. Larry answers every call and books the job: he qualifies the lead, checks availability, and puts a confirmed appointment on the dispatch board without anyone on the team following up. The AI Membership Coordinator runs the membership program: it works through the membership list, books seasonal visits onto the calendar, and keeps the schedule full without anyone making a single outreach call. Operator is the AI business manager running the back half of the operation, working across jobs, invoices, and schedules until the work is done. Podium's release cites contractor results: Air Design Heating and Cooling worked through 600 maintenance members at a 40 percent booking rate and collected $35K in revenue without a single outreach call; Magnolia Plumbing cut invoice-to-payment time in half and freed up 60 hours of admin time per month; Mountain Valley Home Comfort migrated 20,000 customers and 5,000 maintenance agreements from its previous FSM and now runs daily reporting through Operator; Carlsbad Heating and Cooling consolidated four platforms into one and now has every call covered after hours without adding staff. CEO Eric Rea said: "I started Podium in 2014 and in 12 years I have never met a contractor who loves their FSM. They use it because they have to. When you build the system around agents instead of workflows, the business runs differently. The agents go do the work. For the first time, the software does the work instead of just holding the data." The platform is available now for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and garage door contractors in the United States and Canada, with contractors migrating from legacy FSM platforms said to go live in weeks rather than months with a dedicated onboarding team. For Nolan's playbook this is a direct competitor in both the AI answering and full-FSM categories, positioned against ServiceTitan-style incumbents.

## Finding 3
FieldServicePro, an AI-native business suite from EQUP Inc., announced its launch on August 20, 2026 from Boca Raton, Florida via an IssueWire press release. The suite combines field service management, CRM, sales pipelines, estimates, scheduling and dispatch, mobile job management, invoicing, recurring billing, payments, online booking, agreements with e-signature, a client portal, and email, SMS, and WhatsApp communication tools in one system aimed at the full lead-to-invoice lifecycle. It targets HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, pest control, landscaping, roofing, appliance repair, and facility maintenance businesses. The pricing move is the notable part for the playbook: flat-rate pricing instead of per-seat charges. The Starter plan is listed at $199 per month for up to 600 service visits per month and 10,000 marketing emails, and the Growth plan at $299 per month for up to 1,000 service visits and 25,000 marketing emails. Both include unlimited users, contacts, jobs, scheduling, dispatching, estimates, and invoicing, with annual billing options and a 15-day free trial. Founder Vivek Sharma said: "Service businesses do not need more disconnected tools. They need one reliable system that helps them manage the full job lifecycle, from the first lead to the final invoice." This is a pricing-disruptor entry for the playbook: per-seat pricing from Jobber, Housecall Pro, and ServiceTitan is a known friction point for growing shops, and a flat-rate AI-native alternative with stated prices gives Nolan a concrete comparison to bring into prospect conversations. This is filed in the past tense because it is about three weeks old.
