## Explaining the work beats speed and price in HVAC reviews
A [September 18 study](https://www.abnewswire.com/pressreleases/study-of-17241-hvac-reviews-finds-explaining-the-work-outranks-speed-and-price_836464.html) of 17,241 Google reviews across 95 HVAC shops found explaining the problem was the most praised technician behavior, named in 18.4% of positive reviews. Speed or emergency response trailed at 11.2%, punctuality at 9.7%. The usable line: 60.8% of reviews quoting a dollar figure were negative against a 3.5% baseline, which the report reads as anger at unexplained expense rather than expense itself.
