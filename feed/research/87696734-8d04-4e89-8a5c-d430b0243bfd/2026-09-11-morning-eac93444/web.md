## Waiting doubles the repair bill
In a June survey, [SupplyHouse](https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/home-services/2026/06/08/home-repair-delays-cost-more/90419248007/) found 93% of homeowners put off HVAC and plumbing repairs. The wait more than doubled the bill: $380 on average for calls within a week, $868 after six months. For the shops you pitch, the follow-up call plus a financing path is the product: it catches the small ticket before it becomes someone else's emergency.
