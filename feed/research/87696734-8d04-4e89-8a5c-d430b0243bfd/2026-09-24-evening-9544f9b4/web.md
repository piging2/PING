## Frontier labs plan their own AI safety standards body
As [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-google-and-anthropic-join-forces-to-set-ai-safety-standards/) reports, citing The Information, Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI aim to launch an AI standards body by the end of this year or early next year, after their push for a public-private partnership stalled. The body would back third-party pre-deployment testing, set incident-reporting rules, and qualify independent auditors, though critics warn the big labs could use it to box out open-source rivals.
