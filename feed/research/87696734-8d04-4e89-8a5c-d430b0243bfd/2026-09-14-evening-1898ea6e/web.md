## Texting registration takes at least four weeks
Per [Zoom's 10DLC compliance guide](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059336), brand approval takes 1-2 hours; the wait sits in campaign approval, 1-2 weeks on downstream carrier backlog, with number pools up to four weeks. Carriers have [blocked 100% of unregistered business texting outright](https://www.dailystory.com/blog/sms-text-compliance-9-tips-to-keep-your-business-out-of-trouble/) since February 2025, so the queue is not something a shop can skip. For a shop that wants texts live for the fall rush, the paperwork starts now.
