## ChatGPT ads opened at $60 per thousand views for big brands
OpenAI's ad business got off to a bumpy start: it charged some big brands steep flat rates, then moved to [auction-based pricing](https://www.wsj.com/business/media/inside-openais-ad-rush-41c92de2) after early testers pushed back. Rocket's chief marketing officer called the results underwhelming, and some advertisers paused their spending until the product steadies. With a self-serve platform courting smaller advertisers since May, the [sponsored-agent slots](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-pilots-sponsored-agents-in-chatgpt-with-wayfair-angi) Angi, Wayfair, Lowe's and others began piloting on September 16 could get cheaper and far more crowded.
