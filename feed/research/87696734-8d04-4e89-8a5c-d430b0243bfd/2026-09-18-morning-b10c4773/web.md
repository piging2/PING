## EPA reverses the R-410A installation deadline
One of the easiest urgency pitches in HVAC just expired: the EPA's May 2026 rule removed the January 1, 2026 installation deadline for pre-2025 residential R-410A equipment. Installed systems can be serviced and recharged indefinitely, so the honest pitch is age and repair history rather than a cutoff date, per a [San Diego shop's September 18 release](https://business.am-news.com/am-news/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-18-morey-plumbing-heating-and-cooling-inc-epa-reverses-refrigerant-installation-deadline-san-diego-hvac-company-explains-what-it-means-for-home-ac-systems). A shop still warning homeowners about the deadline is working from stale information, and stale information is a conversation opener.
