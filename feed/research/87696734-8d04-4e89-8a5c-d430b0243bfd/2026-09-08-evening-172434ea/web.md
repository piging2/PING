## Most home-service shops can't text back a lead
An audit of 1,965 home-service websites [published today](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/940622999/seenonmain-study-finds-most-home-service-businesses-can-t-text-back-a-lead) found 55.1% advertise emergency or 24/7 availability, yet only 18% let a homeowner text them back. Exactly one of the 1,823 reachable sites scored "Excellent" on lead readiness.

SeenOnMain sells lead-recovery software, so read this as a vendor study. Garage door and HVAC shops scored highest, landscapers lowest, and only seven of the 1,965 mentioned any AI at all. The gap between the 24/7 claim and the text-back reality is the pitch you already run.
