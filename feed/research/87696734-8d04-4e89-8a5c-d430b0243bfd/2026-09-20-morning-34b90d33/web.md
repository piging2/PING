## Your AI chats aren't privileged
In February 2026, federal judge Jed Rakoff ruled that a criminal defendant's conversations with Claude were not protected by attorney-client privilege and could be used against him. The core of the Heppner ruling, per [CTM Legal Group's breakdown](https://www.ctmlegalgroup.com/resources/is-my-ai-legal-conversation-private): a chatbot is not an attorney, and consumer AI privacy policies defeat any expectation of confidentiality. For your deployments, the practical read is to treat customer chat transcripts as [discoverable](https://www.thompsonhine.com/insights/your-ai-chats-arent-privileged-a-wake-up-call-for-legal-professionals/), like email or text, and settle retention and privacy-policy language at install time.
