## Local Services Ads are moving into Google Ads
Plumbing, HVAC, electrical and roofing sit in Google's first migration wave, which retires the standalone Local Services Ads dashboard; the notice lands by email 14 days out. Export spend, lead volume and cost-per-lead by service category before the switch, because [the reporting history does not transfer](http://www.qcherald.com/premium/stacker/stories/google-local-services-ads-transition-to-performance-max-what-to-save-before-your-account-migrates,188972) while manual max-cost-per-lead bidding collapses into one campaign-level target. That single target is the pinch for multi-trade shops, and [QuoteIQ's contractor walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GtdLfDiXyQ) carries the full pre-migration checklist.
