## Friday is National Tradesmen Day
IRWIN Tools started [National Tradesmen Day](http://nationaltoday.com/national-tradesmen-day/?fbclid=IwAR3ztmEC1nhPhZCK3YnqGmh035NojZuVwxRlan-8VDVqsCIAro9YpuiyZAA) in 2011 to honor skilled tradespeople, and this Friday, September 18, the industry gets a ready-made reason to say thanks. [Home Depot's Foundation](https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/trades-training-and-path-pro/celebrating-national-tradesmen-day-and-workforce-development) is marking it through Path to Pro, its program with 100 Black Men of America and the Home Builders Institute. The backdrop it cites: more than 400,000 open trade roles during September's Workforce Development Month.

The recognition costs nothing, and it lands on the exact people you want to know your name.
