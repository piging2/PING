## Finding 1
No new Grand Junction home-service business surfaced in the sweep dated September 13 or later, so the freshest genuine trade-business development is from just before that cutoff: Rescue 1 Plumbing, a Grand Junction plumbing company, announced on September 09, 2026 that it has expanded its operations into modern water-efficiency services for residential and commercial properties. Founder Jack Thompson said the expansion reflects a commitment to local resource management and environmental responsibility. The new offerings include smart fixture installations, comprehensive water auditing, precision leak detection, and advanced irrigation control systems, including internet-connected leak detection and usage tracking with mobile-app monitoring. The company is partnering with commercial enterprises throughout Mesa County on facility-wide water management and has updated its technician training curriculum for smart systems and local plumbing codes. Rescue 1 Plumbing provides residential and commercial plumbing services throughout Grand Junction and surrounding areas, specializing in system maintenance, emergency repairs, and sustainable plumbing upgrades; its listed contact is (970) 812-1715, info@rescueoneplumbing.com.

## Finding 2
Older but the only true grand opening in the sweep: on June 19, 2026, Honk-Key Lock and Safe, a full-service locksmith, safe, and vault company founded in Montrose, announced the grand opening of its new Grand Junction storefront at 230 E Lynwood St., Unit C, Grand Junction, CO 81503, extending its footprint into Mesa County and the surrounding Western Slope. Owner Austin Hohnke said the shop operates as a complete door-opening company, handling commercial doors, frames, hardware, and access control in-house alongside safe and vault services (safe openings typically $200 to $400 for residential units) and automotive key programming. The company is licensed and insured, holds a fire door inspector credential, performs NFPA 80-compliant fire door repairs, and serves Grand Junction, Mesa County, Montrose, and mountain communities including Aspen and Crested Butte. Its listed contact is 970-773-0047, info@honkkeylocksafe.com. This story is from June 19, 2026, so it predates the September 13 cutoff, but it is the only actual storefront opening the sweep found in the trade space.
