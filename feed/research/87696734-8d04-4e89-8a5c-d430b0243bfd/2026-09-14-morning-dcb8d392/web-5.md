## Finding 1
UpSmith is running a direct migration offer aimed at contractors caught by the ServiceTitan-Podium split. Its page says Podium comes off the ServiceTitan marketplace on September 15, 2026 and pitches UpSmith as a certified ServiceTitan integration that answers calls and texts and books jobs straight to the ServiceTitan dispatch board. For Podium customers the terms are: free through October 31, 2026, onboarding and setup on UpSmith, migration of contacts and opt-out lists handled for them, and the paid 12-month subscription starts November 1, 2026. The offer ends September 30, 2026. The page explicitly says it replaces the AI agents that stop reaching ServiceTitan on September 15, not reviews, payments or inbox, and that shops can keep Podium for what it still does. It also warns that if Podium is the shop's actual phone line, they will still need a phone provider such as Dialpad.

## Finding 2
ServiceTitan's own response to ending the Podium integration was to offer affected customers discounts to switch to certified App Marketplace partners, according to Homepros reporting on the August 12 customer email from co-founders Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan. The integration ends September 15, 2026 and affects nearly 1,000 contractors, per Podium CEO Eric Rea. ServiceTitan's position, via Mahdessian's LinkedIn post and a company statement, is that Podium was on a legacy partnership that had run its course and was offered certification on the same standards as others but opted not to certify. Certification, introduced last fall, requires partners to meet technical standards and either pay connection-based fees or share revenue. Podium's Rea says ServiceTitan wanted a substantial revenue cut and agreement to stop building anything competitive, then went non-responsive for about three months before sending a 30-day termination notice. Podium now bills itself as an AI Operating System for home services spanning call handling, dispatching and payments.

## Finding 3
For Nolan's FSM pricing playbook, Housecall Pro's current public pricing was re-verified August 31, 2026 as: Basic $79 per month monthly or $59 annually (1 user), Essentials $189 monthly or $149 annually (up to 5 users, then $100 per month per extra user), MAX $329 monthly or $299 annually (8 users, then $75 per month per extra user). Housecall Pro's own LLM info page, crawled 4 days ago, confirms the same three tiers and notes it also offers a Mechanical Package with different pricing for HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors, which maps to the trade-specific packages launched July 15, 2026. The trade packages bundle scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, online booking and reporting with a mechanical KPI dashboard, membership plans on jobs and estimates, AI-driven pricing benchmarks, price-book commissions, credit card surcharging and technician efficiency reporting.
