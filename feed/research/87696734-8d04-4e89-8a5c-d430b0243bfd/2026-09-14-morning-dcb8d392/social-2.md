Beat: social
Angle: In the last 24h of public contractor conversation (Facebook groups, Instagram, Threads), what are owners and techs saying about missed calls, AI answering objections, and lead response, in their own words. The paper already ran 'The objection catalog' and 'What owners actually say'; do not restate those threads. File only fresh posts and the exact language owners use.

## Finding 1
On September 13, 2026, Jeff Donabedian (@jeffdonabedian) posted a graphic titled in-image 'WHAT THEIR CUSTOMERS SAY · 04' quoting a homeowner verbatim: "There was no option to speak to a person, so I hung up without getting the job scheduled. I wrote a bad Google review." The caption says the homeowner called his plumber mid-remodel to schedule work, hit an AI that repeatedly failed to understand his street address and offered no path to a human, so he hung up, hired the next company he called, and left the bad review. The business owner only saw the review three weeks later. Donabedian's lesson, quoted verbatim: "The AI wasn’t the mistake. Nobody reading the call log was." He adds in a comment that the homeowner told the story three separate times over a few months in a public thread.

## Finding 2
On September 13, 2026, GoodcallAI Houston (@goodcallai.houston) posted a graphic headlined in-image 'THE OBJECTION I HEAR MOST' with the line, quoted verbatim: "You already have a robot. It's called voicemail — and it can't ask a single question." The caption argues voicemail is passive, cannot gather an address or distinguish emergencies, and positions its managed AI receptionist for Greater Houston home-service contractors as the replacement. On September 14, 2026, Gabe Mlynarek (@gabemlynarekbiz) posted a reel naming three businesses he would not recommend an AI receptionist to: 1) businesses that require an emotional connection with every single customer over the phone, 2) businesses already answering every call efficiently and economically, 3) businesses trying to trick customers into thinking the AI is a real person. His verdict, quoted from the post summary: the technology is "very close" but "we're not there yet."

## Finding 3
On September 13, 2026, PhoneStack (@tryphonestack) posted a graphic aimed at HVAC, plumbing and small-business owners that reads, quoted verbatim: "BEFORE YOU BUY ANYTHING Count your own calls for one week. Then talk to anybody, us included." The caption tells contractors to track five numbers over one week before talking to any vendor, including PhoneStack: total calls in, answered live, went to voicemail, rang out with no answer, and how many voicemails became a booked job. The point is to measure the actual voicemail-to-booked-job conversion rate before buying an AI receptionist.

## Finding 4
On September 13, 2026, Slice Of The Pie (@sliceofthepieco) posted a 2026 Home Services Benchmarks infographic built on a 100-dot visualization stating 27% of inbound home-service inquiries receive no reply at all, with headline text quoted: 'You paid for the lead. Nobody called it back.' The post adds that only 12% of contractors respond within five minutes, while 78% of customers hire whoever responds first regardless of price or reviews, closing with 'You're not competing on price. You're competing on speed.'
