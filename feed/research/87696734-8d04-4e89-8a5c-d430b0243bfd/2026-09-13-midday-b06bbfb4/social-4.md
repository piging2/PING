## Finding 2
Landscaper Keith Kalfas posted a 'Fall Cleanup Pricing Reveal' video on September 9, 2026 that puts his actual price book on camera instead of asking for a call. He states a $700 minimum for fall cleanups and shows the math: a $250 base price plus $60 per cubic yard of debris removed, walking through examples of two yards ($370) and three yards ($430), then explains which upsells, like ornamental tree trimming at $85 to $100 per tree with an orchard ladder, get a job to the $700 target. He even admits some customers push back on the $700 price and says he is considering lowering it for quicker jobs. The Instagram version drew 100 likes and 5 comments on an account with 39K followers; the Facebook version drew 120 likes and 19 comments on an account with 95K followers. The tactic is radical pricing transparency as content, which converts curiosity about a taboo topic (what jobs really cost) into saves, shares, and inbound estimates rather than relying on a discount.
