## Finding 1
CallRail, the AI-powered lead engagement platform, announced July 21, 2026 (the press-release page's own tracking data stamps it 2026-07-21) that it deepened its Jobber partnership and launched three new home-services integrations covering JobNimbus, JobTread, and Workiz. The Jobber tie-up is CallRail's first bidirectional field-service-management integration for Voice Assist, its AI voice assistant: Voice Assist can check a Jobber calendar in real time and book a job appointment directly during the call, caller data, call recordings, transcripts, and intake fields sync back into client records across all four platforms, and the assistant recognizes returning Jobber clients by name. CallRail said the integrations serve its more than 10,000 home-service contractor customers. Notably for the playbook: none of these four FSM platforms had a native CallRail bidirectional link before, so contractors on Jobber, JobNimbus, JobTread, or Workiz now get live call booking plus two-way CRM sync with the same lead-engagement tool.

## Finding 2
Housecall Pro launched its first industry-specific software packages for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical businesses on July 15, 2026 (DENVER, July 15, 2026, GLOBE NEWSWIRE), the company's first departure from a one-size-fits-all product. Built from insights across more than 100 million jobs and feedback from 200,000-plus pros, the three packages preconfigure industry onboarding, templates, and workflows around core scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, online booking, and reporting, plus new trade-specific features: a mechanical KPI dashboard, membership plans attached to jobs and estimates for recurring revenue, real-time AI-driven pricing benchmarks for price book items, price book commissions that set tech pay by line item, credit card surcharging, and technician efficiency reporting. Roland Ligtenberg, co-founder and Chief AI Officer, said the packages were shaped by millions of real jobs. The packages come in multiple pricing tiers (specific package prices not disclosed in the release).

## Finding 3
Workiz launched Genius Marketing on June 24, 2026 (the press-release page's own tracking data stamps it 2026-06-24), completing what it calls the industry's first 'Service Revenue Machine': automated, agentic demand generation embedded in its FSM operating system, which is trusted by over 120,000 professionals. The product adds a click-to-cash reporting layer that ties outreach campaigns to booked jobs and exact dollar revenue, an AI campaign engine that autonomously generates trade-specific outreach from real job history, customer tags, and last-service dates, automated estimate recovery aimed at the roughly 40% of estimates that typically go cold, and a centralized reputation feed that consolidates Google Business reviews with AI-drafted responses. Workiz claimed users see on average a 10% monthly revenue increase from Genius Marketing and up to 20% with the full machine; early adopter Gold Eagle Services, an Austin HVAC business, said its campaigns generated $400,000 over four months. CEO Didi Azaria said users no longer need to buy expensive leads or run complex ad campaigns.
