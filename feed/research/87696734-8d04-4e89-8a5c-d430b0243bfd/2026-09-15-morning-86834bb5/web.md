## ServiceTitan and Ferguson deepen pricing integration
Material costs are the quiet margin killer: 35% of commercial contractors cite labor and overhead as the top risk, 32% cite material prices, per an [undated ServiceTitan announcement](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/servicetitan-and-ferguson-partner). Three features are live: multi-account mapping for dual-trade shops, catalog mapping that ties Ferguson items to the Pricebook, and contract pricing that pulls negotiated Ferguson rates into estimates. Armstrong Plumbers' Bill Haupert says automatic updates are 'making estimating and pricing reviews simple and based on current, not historical or perceived, costs,' with in-app procurement and real-time availability to come.
