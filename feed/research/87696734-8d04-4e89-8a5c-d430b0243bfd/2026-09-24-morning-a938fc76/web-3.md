## Finding 1
Sela, a San Francisco-based voice AI platform for mortgage sales, has raised $21 million in total funding across Seed and Series A rounds, led by Costanoa with participation from Emergence Capital; the article surfaced in search results as updated within the last day. Founded in 2024 by Nate Becker and Vahe Tshitoyan, Sela builds voice AI agents that answer complex borrower questions, build rapport, and hand borrowers to a loan officer when human support is needed, measuring agents on business outcomes like borrowers reached and loans closed rather than minutes of AI usage. The company says its agents have held millions of conversations with consumers; in one A/B test with more than 10,000 prospective borrowers it reported a 9% increase in lead-to-lock rates versus the lender's existing process, and in another test with a top-five mortgage servicer its voice AI outperformed a competing voice AI solution by 41% on a lead-to-lock basis across more than 7,000 leads. The capital will go toward growing product, engineering, and go-to-market teams.
