## Finding 3
Grand Junction City Council voted 4-3 to remove the significant-tree preservation requirement from the city's development code, a change now part of Ordinance No. 5340, effective October 5, 2026. Developers will no longer have to preserve at least 30% of trees 15 inches or larger in diameter or replace removed ones. The change came from the city's Housing Affordability Code Task Force, which argued the old rules created new-development problems. Subdivision rules still require developers to consider mature trees and native vegetation, and subdivision plats must still identify significant natural features. The page states no publication date.
