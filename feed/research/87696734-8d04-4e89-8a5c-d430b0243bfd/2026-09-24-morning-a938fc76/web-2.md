## Finding 1
An independent comparison updated within the last week before this edition, with prices verified on each vendor's public pricing page on September 13, 2026, documents the current Jobber vs Housecall Pro price gap: Jobber Core costs $49/month month-to-month or $29/month billed annually, while Housecall Pro Basic is $79/month month-to-month or $59/month billed annually, a consistent $30 monthly gap, $360 over a year, before add-ons and fees. Both plans include one user and a 14-day trial with no credit card. At five users the race is nearly tied on annual billing, both list a $149/month equivalent (Jobber Connect vs Housecall Pro Essentials), while month-to-month Housecall Pro Essentials at $189 undercuts Jobber Connect at $199. The comparison notes the tradeoff behind the gap: Housecall Pro Basic includes a price book, job costing, consumer financing, and review management at entry level, whereas Jobber reserves job costing for its Grow tier and QuickBooks Online sync for Connect and above. Evidence grade: third-party research that names its verification date and method, but no hands-on product testing; the numbers are vendor-documented, the recommendations editorial.

## Finding 2
A 14-platform pricing comparison updated the day before this edition (marked verified current September 2026, pricing pulled from vendor websites in September 2026) exposes how Jobber's true cost stacks up for a typical 5-person crew: the $49/month Core plan needs Connect at $139/month to unlock QuickBooks Online integration and Grow at $199/month for two-way SMS, and layering the Marketing Suite ($99/month), AI Receptionist ($29/month), and Pipeline ($49/month) on a 5-user Grow plan at $299/month brings the real figure to $476/month, plus 2.9% + $0.30 per card transaction. The same roundup documents CompanyCam's 2026 restructure: the old 3-user minimum was eliminated but higher-tier prices rose, the Marketing Suite is now a paid add-on at $99/month, and SSO costs $250/month. It grades pricing transparency A through F: only 4 of 14 platforms publish full pricing without a sales call, and ServiceTitan remains fully quote-based with a per-technician model. Evidence grade: third-party research with a stated verification method and current-date verification marks; individual figures should be treated as independently reported until checked against the vendor's own pages.
