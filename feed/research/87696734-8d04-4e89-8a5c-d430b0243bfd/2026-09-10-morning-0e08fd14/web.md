## Housecall Pro hosts free contractor meetups Oct 1
Housecall Pro's inaugural [Trades Thursday](https://www.pmmag.com/articles/107699-housecall-pro-brings-home-service-pros-together-nationwide-for-inaugural-trades-thursday) includes a first look at the company's new AI-powered tools, and it is built for the exact shops you pitch. The October 1 events are free and open to any home service pro, whether or not they are a Housecall Pro customer. There are more than 15 regional hubs, Denver among them, plus local meetups led by its Superpros.

The tools reveal is playbook intel: their chief AI officer is putting the roadmap on stage this fall, so it is worth knowing what it does before it shows up in a pitch. Denver is the nearest hub to your Western Colorado market, a room full of your prospects and a natural question to ask in outreach.
