## Finding 1
NevTech, an AI automation agency in Indianapolis founded by Cody Neville, launched on September 21, 2026 a service that pairs AI-readable website builds with a bundled AI answering service. Every site ships engineered to be read by search engines and AI assistants, with facts like hours, services and service area labeled so they can be repeated correctly, and each site is submitted to Bing and the other indexes AI assistants draw on, not only Google. The included AI voice agent answers around the clock, books appointments and writes each call into the system the business already runs on, with configurations for dental practices, medical offices, and HVAC and home services contractors. The release frames the pitch around generative engine optimization (GEO): it cites a Pew Research Center study published July 22, 2025 tracking 68,879 Google searches by 900 U.S. adults, which found users clicked a search result in 8 percent of visits when an AI summary appeared versus 15 percent when no summary appeared, and only 1 percent of visits produced a click on a source cited inside the summary. It also cites Google Ads Keyword Planner data for the United States, compiled September 2026, recording monthly searches for "AI answering service" rising from 2,400 in September 2025 to 18,100 in August 2026, and searches for "AI receptionist" rising from 4,400 to a peak of 450,000 over the same period. Founder Cody Neville is quoted: "Design used to be the whole job. Now the job is being the answer." The service is available in Indianapolis and throughout Indiana, and remotely nationwide, with a 30-minute assessment at no cost. This is the company's own announcement via EINPresswire, so the search-volume figures are NevTech's compiled data, not independently verified.
- https://www.einpresswire.com/article/943775848/nevtech-launches-ai-web-design-and-ai-answering-service-for-indiana-businesses (published 2026-09-21)

## Finding 2

This is a third-party comparison, not vendor announcements, so each figure is reported-as-published rather than vendor-confirmed.

## Finding 3
CallSphere, an AI voice-agent vendor founded by Sagar Shankaran, has a live page (its search listing showed it as updated 2 days before the September 22 reading day; the page itself states no publication date) detailing its After-Hours / Home-Services vertical for HVAC contractors. The page claims seven specialist agents: AH-Triage severity classifier, AH-Emergency-Router warm-transfer to the on-call tech with E911 fallback, AH-Booking for non-emergency booking, AH-Quote for dispatch-fee and deposit capture, AH-Status-Lookup for job ETA and tech location, AH-Surge-Overflow for call spikes, and AH-QA-Closeout for post-call summaries. It claims native integrations: ServiceTitan via the official Tenant API (Tenant ID + App Key) creating jobs with correct Job Type, Business Unit, Campaign, Customer, Location and Priority with no Zapier middleware; Housecall Pro via OAuth; Jobber via API token; Workiz via API key; plus FieldEdge. Stated capabilities: severity triage on keywords like "no heat," "gas smell," "CO alarm," warm-transfer of emergencies to the on-call tech's mobile within 30 seconds with a structured briefing, 22-second median warm-transfer time, dispatch-fee quoting with card capture for no-shows, and SMS confirmations with tech name, ETA window and live GPS link where the FSM supports it. Stated pricing is $49 / $99 / $149 tiers with a 7-day no-card trial, plus a $19 per month trial tier described as covering most owner-operators, and a 22 percent affiliate offer. The page's ROI math for a 10-truck HVAC contractor claims $25,440 per month in recovered emergency tickets against a $99 per month plan. Everything here is the vendor's own marketing copy, so the integration and performance claims are CallSphere's assertions, not verified stack facts.
- https://callsphere.ai/blog/vw2a-hvac-emergency-dispatch-voice-agent-servicetitan-housecall-pro-2026 (undated)

## Finding 4
On June 24, 2026, Workiz announced the launch of Genius Marketing, which it positions as the final piece of its "Service Revenue Machine" alongside Genius Answering: a unified platform combining operations (call answering, scheduling, dispatching, invoicing) with agentic demand generation built directly into the field service management system. Genius Marketing's stated capabilities: a Click-to-Cash Reporting Layer tracking every automated outreach campaign to booked jobs and exact dollar revenue; an AI Campaign Engine that autonomously generates trade-specific outreach and follow-up sequences from real job history, customer tags and last service dates; Automated Estimate Recovery targeting the 40 percent of estimates that typically go cold, with multi-channel touchpoints until the job is secured; and a Centralized Reputation Feed consolidating Google Business reviews with AI-drafted responses. Workiz claims businesses see a 10 percent increase in monthly revenue just by activating Genius Marketing and up to 20 percent total revenue growth using the complete Service Revenue Machine, and cites early adopter Gold Eagle Services, an HVAC business in Austin, Texas, which it says generated $400,000 in revenue through Genius Marketing campaigns in four months, including $140,000 in booked jobs from two newsletters at a 32 percent open rate. Workiz describes itself as trusted by over 120,000 professionals across North America, completing over 6 million jobs annually. This finding is older than the reading window (June 24, not September) and is filed because the paper has not previously covered it; all figures are Workiz's own claims from its press release.
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/workiz-unveils-the-industrys-first-service-revenue-machine-completes-platform-with-launch-of-genius-marketing-302809288.html (published 2026-06-24)
