## 8 ways to ask for more Google reviews
For trades the trigger is job sign-off or invoice paid; the ask goes by text next morning with a direct link, plus one follow-up in three to seven days. [The guide](https://cloutly.com/blog/get-more-google-reviews/) lists eight ways to ask, from in-person and QR codes to email and video, and warns Google bans incentives, gating and on-premises pressure. Pulling a 4.7 average back from one one-star review takes 13 new five-stars.
