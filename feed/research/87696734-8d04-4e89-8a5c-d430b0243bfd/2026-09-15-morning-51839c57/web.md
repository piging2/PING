## Watsco to acquire plumbing distributor Granite Group
[Announced yesterday](https://www.inddist.com/mergers-acquisitions/news/22974410/hvac-distribution-giant-to-acquire-the-granite-group), the deal folds a third-generation family distributor into Watsco's $7.2 billion orbit: 82 branches, about $500 million in sales across seven Northeast states. Granite stays independent under CEO Bill Condron, terms were not disclosed, and closing waits on customary approvals. It is Watsco's 74th acquisition since 1989.

The part worth your attention is in [Watsco's own release](https://www.watsco.com/newsitem/watsco-to-acquire-the-granite-group-distributor-of-plumbing-and-hvac-products-revenues-of-500-million-throughout-the-northeast/): more than 70,000 contractors and techs now order through its digital platforms. The company calls that technology ecosystem a durable and widening competitive advantage in a $74 billion distribution market. The supply house is becoming a software vendor.
