## Finding 2
The Mesa County Board of County Commissioners has formally approved and sent a joint letter to Gov. Jared Polis, Senate President James Coleman, and House Speaker Julie McCluskie urging action on unfunded state mandates, an item that surfaced in search results within the last day (the county page itself states no publication date and says the commissioners 'announced today'). The letter names specific mandates with uncompensated costs, including SB23-166 and SB24-005 (Wildland-Urban Interface Code), HB21-1286 (Energy Benchmarking and Building Performance Standards), HB21-1250 (Demographic and Contact Reporting), and HB21-1236 (Colorado OIT Compliance). Commissioner Bobbie Daniel is quoted saying 'Unfunded mandates are hidden taxes. When the state requires new programs but doesn't fund them, local taxpayers end up footing the bill. That's not fair, and it's not sustainable.' The county says that under C.R.S. section 29-1-304.5 it is prepared, with other participating counties, to treat such mandates as optional if funding is not provided, and is inviting state leaders to discuss the true costs. The county's tagline for the effort is 'Fix It or Fund It.'
- https://www.mesacounty.us/news/commissioners/mesa-county-joins-bipartisan-coalition-calling-reform-unfunded-state-mandates (undated)
