## Finding 1
Snipps Heating & Air Conditioning, a family-run HVAC contractor based in Montrose at 113 Rose Lane, Suite C, Montrose, CO 81401, published a consumer advisory on September 21, 2026 urging Western Slope homeowners to verify that heating equipment was adjusted for elevation. The company says gas appliances rated at sea level must be derated above 2,000 feet under the National Fuel Gas Code at 4 percent per 1,000 feet, and that skipping the high-altitude kit brings incomplete combustion, elevated carbon monoxide, nuisance lockouts, and overheating. It recommends homeowners ask three questions of any furnace installed in the last several years: whether an altitude derate was performed, whether a high-altitude kit was installed, and whether a combustion test was documented at commissioning. The release says the company has served the region since 1996 and covers Montrose, Delta, Ouray, San Miguel, Gunnison, San Juan, and Hinsdale counties, including Montrose, Delta, Olathe, Cedaredge, Hotchkiss, Cimarron, Ouray, Ridgway, Telluride, Silverton, Gunnison, Crested Butte, and Lake City. Its stated services are heating repair and installation, furnaces, heat pumps, air conditioning, ductless mini-splits and retrofits, indoor air quality, maintenance plans, new construction, and property management support; it operates its own sheet metal fabrication shop, employs NATE-certified technicians, is an authorized Amana dealer, and provides 24-hour emergency HVAC service at (970) 240-8152. This is existing-business marketing activity from an established in-market trade contractor, not a new filing. ## Finding 2
Graff Dairy, the Western Colorado drive-thru on 29 Road in Grand Junction, has new ownership: Jordan and Maddison Burge officially took ownership on September 1, 2026. The previous owner, Darin Carei, put the business up for sale in March 2026 and retains ownership of the real estate; the Burges bought the business itself. According to the local press account (carried from a Grand Junction Business Times story), the new owners plan to keep the nostalgic look while introducing coffee, refreshers, and Red Bull-infused drinks, earlier 7 a.m. openings, self-ordering kiosks, and new order screens, with customers still able to order from employees. The shop is asking locals for menu feedback via its Facebook page. The page states no publication date (it appeared in search results roughly eight days before September 23, 2026); the ownership transfer date of September 1, 2026 is stated in the article itself. Graff Dairy is food retail, not a trade or home-service business, so it is not a new-business prospect, but it is the clearest local ownership change on record in this sweep window.
