## Finding 1
The ServiceTitan-Podium integration goes dark on 15 September 2026, two days from the reading day, ending a nine-year partnership covering roughly 1,000 shared customers. ServiceTitan co-founders Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan announced the cutoff in a 12 August email; Podium's listing was delisted on 14 August on both sides and now returns a 404. Each side tells the story differently: ServiceTitan says Podium declined to certify under its new App Marketplace program (published 24 June 2026), which adds a revenue-share requirement where none had existed for nine years; Podium CEO Eric Rea says ServiceTitan wanted a 'substantial cut of revenue' and demanded Podium 'stop building anything that competed with them,' went non-responsive for three months, then sent a 30-day termination notice, leaving Podium 'equally blindsided.' Affected contractors are being offered discounts to move to a certified communication partner or told to contact Podium about running unintegrated. Why it matters beyond the two companies: the dispute is really about competition, not paperwork, and waiting for a patched-up deal before 15 September is a poor plan. Playbook angle: any Western Slope contractor running ServiceTitan plus Podium for reviews and messaging is in a forced migration window right now, and switching vendors mid peak season is exactly the pain PING Social solves. Note: FieldCamp competes with ServiceTitan and discloses that; the factual timeline is sourced from both companies' on-record statements, which FieldCamp attributes rather than asserting.

## Finding 2
An audit of 1,965 home service business websites across plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, landscaping, house cleaning, pest control, and garage door repair, in 30 U.S. metros, found that 55.1% advertise emergency or 24/7 availability but only 18.0% actually offer text messaging and just 2.6% have any live chat. Another 76.9% make no explicit promise about response speed to a new inquiry, and only seven businesses in the whole sample, 0.38%, mentioned any AI assistant or chatbot on their site. Garage door repair and HVAC scored highest on lead readiness; landscaping scored lowest. Caveat for the playbook: the study was published 8 September 2026 by SeenOnMain, a lead-recovery software vendor selling the exact gap it measured, so treat the numbers as vendor research rather than independent. Even with that grain of salt, the '55% promise 24/7, 18% can text back' gap is a pitch-ready stat for Nolan's missed-call text-back conversations.
- https://lifestyle.houstonnewstoday.com/story/862083/seenonmain-study-finds-most-home-service-businesses-cant-text-back-a-lead/ (published 2026-09-08)

## Finding 3
Podium has launched its own full field service management product, built for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical, fronted by its AI employee 'Larry.' Podium's own FSM page, which states no publication date, describes a real-time visual dispatch board that Larry books by skill and location, a technician mobile app that works offline with full customer history, phone-based estimates with good-better-best price books and text-link signing, on-site and text-to-pay invoicing with automatic day 3/7/14 reminders, automatic maintenance-agreement renewals, and QuickBooks Online sync. Its FAQ is explicit: asked 'Does Podium's FSM replace my current scheduling software?', the answer is 'Yes. Podium's FSM replaces your scheduling software, dispatch tools, and any disconnected apps you're stitching together.' In short, the reviews-and-messaging vendor is now a direct ServiceTitan competitor, which is the subtext of the integration fight. Podium CEO Eric Rea posted on 21 August that he had seen a demo of the product and that Podium had crossed $100M in AI-agent ARR within 24 months. Playbook angle: Podium is both a cautionary tale about marketplace dependence and a new FSM entrant to track in competitive conversations.
