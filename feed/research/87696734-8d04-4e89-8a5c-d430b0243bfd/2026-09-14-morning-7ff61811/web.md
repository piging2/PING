## The number that matters is cost per booked job
A [new essay by Annakoren](https://medium.com/@annakoren/nobody-wins-the-lead-auction-89b55c3042d7) runs the lead-cost math most shops never bother with. A $50 shared lead goes to five pros, and at a one-in-five win rate and a thirty percent close rate, that works out to about $833 in lead spend for a single booked job. The platforms get paid five times on one homeowner's job, which is why, in the author's words, the system selects for reflexes rather than fit; the essay's second-half pitch for a commission marketplace is one writer's argument, but the math is a solid line to carry into any lead-cost conversation.
