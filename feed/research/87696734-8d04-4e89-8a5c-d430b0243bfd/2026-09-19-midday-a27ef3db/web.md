## A plain-language test for spotting AI grift
Toronto consultant [Tiffany DaSilva](https://kingnewspress.com/as-ottawa-pushes-ai-adoption-toronto-consultant-offers-small-business-owners-a-plain-language-test-for-spotting-ai-grift-26091849255/), who teaches AI marketing at Growclass and McMaster, gave small business owners a one-line filter for AI pitches on September 18: if you cannot understand what a vendor is telling you, stop listening, because 'if it's hard to understand, that's by design.' Canadian business AI use rose from 6.1% to 19.2% in two years, the surge she says is pulling in vendors owners cannot evaluate, and Ottawa wants 60% adoption by 2034. Her rule for owners: judge the explanation, not the jargon, and treat AI as an assistant rather than a replacement.
