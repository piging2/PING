## Emergencies lead the callback queue
On a heat-wave afternoon you cannot call back fifteen people at once, so the sequence is the strategy. A [working order](https://medium.com/@rick_21279/hvac-missed-calls-which-ones-should-you-call-back-first-8e686424f76f) for HVAC shops puts emergencies with safety at stake first, then replacement quotes, then maintenance-plan members, with tune-ups and price checks last. That turns your pitch from speed alone into triage, which is closer to what a busy shop actually needs.
