## Meta's Muse shakes the market
Meta's Muse is a virtual personal assistant that books appointments and trips, places orders, and sends emails and messages for its user. It has already passed ChatGPT to top Apple's App Store, per Sensor Tower data reported by [Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/meta-muse-paypal-shopify-stocks-selloff-efb8807d). Shopify plugged [Shop Pay checkout](https://WWW.ZACKS.COM/commentary/2994095/shopify-stock-jumps-on-meta-muse-deal-time-to-buy) into Muse on Monday and PayPal followed Tuesday, so the agent that books the appointment now closes the sale too.
