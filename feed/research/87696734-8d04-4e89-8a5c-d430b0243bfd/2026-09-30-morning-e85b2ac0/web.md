## AdaptClose launches AI sales coaching for home services
The expensive part of a home-service job isn't the lead, it's the kitchen-table conversation nobody measures.

It fits 5-to-30-rep shops in roofing, HVAC, solar and pest control, with AI roleplay for reps to practice the pitch.
