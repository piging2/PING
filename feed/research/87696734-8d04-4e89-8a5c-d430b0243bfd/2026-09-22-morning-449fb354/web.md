## 86% of solopreneurs try AI before hiring
Sixty-four percent of solopreneurs would rather look bigger and more capable than get more done without adding time, employees or costs, per [FreshBooks' report](https://lifestyle.longislandreport.org/story/846502/before-they-hire-86-of-solopreneurs-try-ai-first-freshbooks-survey-finds/). The September 15 "Era of the Solopreneur" report surveyed 500 solopreneurs and microbusiness owners, and 86% said they try AI before hiring for a task they cannot finish alone. That AI goes mostly to marketing, design and customer service, while 57% still give bookkeeping, invoicing and taxes ten-plus hours a week, the part of the business it reaches last.
