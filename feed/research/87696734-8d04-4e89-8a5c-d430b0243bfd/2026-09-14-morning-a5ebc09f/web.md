## QuoteIQ launches first native MCP connector for home service CRM
Contractors can tell ChatGPT, Claude or Grok to find a customer, check a balance, or book a job, and QuoteIQ does it. The [release](https://www.abnewswire.com/pressreleases/quoteiq-launches-the-first-native-mcp-connector-built-for-a-home-service-crm_835513.html) lists 134 tools across 21 areas, with changes previewed before they apply, an audit trail, and OAuth permissions tied to the subscriber's plan. Setup takes about two minutes, no API keys, included on every plan, which the [announcement](https://news.rhodeislandchronicle.com/story/796052/quoteiq-launches-the-first-native-mcp-connector-built-for-a-home-service-crm.html) says already serves more than 40,000 pros.
