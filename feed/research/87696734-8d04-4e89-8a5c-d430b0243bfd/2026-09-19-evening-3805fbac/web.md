## Colorado unemployment rises to 4% as payroll jobs decline
Colorado employers shed 1,400 payroll jobs in August, led by construction's loss of about 2,200, according to the state labor department's data [reported by ColoradoBiz](https://coloradobiz.com/colorado-unemployment-rate-rises-to-4-percent-payroll-jobs-decline/). The squeeze underneath is still supply-side: participation at its lowest since 1976, pay still climbing, and your prospects' hiring pain isn't easing on one soft payroll month.
