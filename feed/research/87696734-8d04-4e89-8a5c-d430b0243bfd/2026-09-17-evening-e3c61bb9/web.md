## Businesses use AI faster than they manage its risks
Six in 10 small and mid-market owners say employees already use public chatbots for work, while written rules lag far behind, according to [Nationwide](https://news.nationwide.com/nationwide-survey-businesses-are-using-ai-faster-than-they-are-managing-its-risks/). That gap is a practical opener for you: a one-page approved-tools list and a human check on customer-facing output sells before any automation does, as [Under30CEO's breakdown](https://www.under30ceo.com/small-business-cybersecurity-ai-policy-gap/) notes.
