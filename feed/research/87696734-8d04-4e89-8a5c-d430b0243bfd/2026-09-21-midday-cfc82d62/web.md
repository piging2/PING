## Small businesses using AI are hiring faster
Gusto compared adopters with peers who knew about AI but held off, and a year later the smallest adopters had pulled ahead by about 10% on headcount, via [SBE Council](https://sbecouncil.org/2026/09/21/small-business-using-ai-are-hiring-faster/). The new roles were not AI jobs but technicians, cooks, teachers and front-desk staff, hours freed from admin and scheduling put back into customer work.
