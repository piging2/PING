## Finding 1
Field service platform Zuper (Seattle, operating since 2020, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified) announced four new AI agents in a release carried with a September 10, 2026 wire date. AR Agent chases overdue invoices across voice, SMS and email until payment lands. Sales Coach Agent records in-person sales conversations with the homeowner's consent, scores them against the company's own sales playbook, and emails coaching to the rep and manager shortly after the visit; it is in beta with selected roofing and exteriors companies. Weather Agent posts a crew-ready weather digest to Zuper Chat or email before the day starts. Reputation Agent pulls new online reviews into Zuper Chat matched to the customer, job close date and assigned tech. Sales Coach, Weather and Reputation agents are available now; AR Agent arrives early in Q4. The agents are priced on usage-based credits as part of Zuper's AI plan, configured in plain language from Zuper's Agent Studio, and live inside the customer's jobs, invoices, crews and records rather than a separate dashboard. The release also describes Zuper Sense, a command center that flags stalling estimates and aging invoices, and quotes founder and CEO Anand Subbaraj: 'The time for experimentation is over. This is the outcome era.' Early customers report roughly thirty minutes saved per crew member per job and checklists completed fifty percent faster with voice via the existing Field Agent. Direct playbook relevance: AI receptionist for overflow and after-hours calls (the CSR Agent), AI collections, review-response matching to jobs, and voice-first field documentation are all now shipped as productized features by an FSM competitor, not just pitched as consulting add-ons.

## Finding 2
Reggora, an appraisal technology company serving lenders nationwide, launched HomeInspector.com on September 10, 2026, a free software platform for home inspectors on web, iOS and Android. The platform bundles customizable report writing, mobile inspection tools, interactive client and agent reports with repair request tools, online booking and scheduling, digital agreements with e-signature, integrated payments (collect before, during or after the inspection, with optional automatic report release on payment), and a custom website builder with online booking, all with no subscription, no per-report fees and no contract. The release positions it against inspection software and website subscriptions that can cost inspectors thousands of dollars per year. The free model is funded by optional paid property-data assignments for lenders; inspectors are not required to accept them and their reports are not shared with lenders. CEO Brian Zitin said 'giving the software away for free is exactly what our position in the transaction makes possible,' and General Manager Greg Montemurro said the team helps inspectors bring existing templates over with free setup. The platform is available now with free account creation and a free setup call. Playbook relevance: a full-stack free FSM-adjacent product in a home-service vertical, setting a new floor on what shops expect to pay for scheduling, booking, payments and websites.

## Finding 3
A field service software comparison published by 2Labs, with pricing and plan details rechecked on September 7, 2026, gives a current verified price snapshot for the three main FSM platforms. Jobber's solo annual-prepaid post-promotion prices were Core $29/month, Connect $99/month and Grow $149/month, with monthly-reference amounts of $49/$139/$199, plus promotional terms that vary by offer and renewal. Housecall Pro's annual prices were Basic $59/month (1 user), Essentials $149/month (5 users included) and MAX $299/month (8 users included), with monthly billing at $79/$189/$329, in USD excluding tax. ServiceTitan publishes no dollar amount; its pricing page is quote-based and per technician, so comparison requires a written proposal. The page notes Jobber offers a free trial with no credit card and a team-size selector, Housecall Pro offers a 14-day trial with MAX access (a caution: the trial plan may not be the plan the buyer keeps), and ServiceTitan offers a personalized demo path with no self-serve trial. It also warns that Jobber's promotional amounts should not be treated as renewal prices and that team size can change pricing. Playbook relevance: dated, vendor-verified public pricing for quoting FSM stack costs to prospects.

## Finding 4
A FieldCamp alternatives guide (updated within the last two weeks per the search page) documents the real per-seat cost breakpoints in Housecall Pro pricing: extra users past a plan's included seats cost $100/month each on Essentials (5 included) and $75/month each on MAX (8 included), with card processing fees from 2.59% on every plan (1% on bank payments). A seven-person team staying on Essentials pays $349/month, so the lower tier stops being cheaper than MAX past six users. The same guide puts Jobber's Grow plan at $299/month with $29 per additional user beyond included seats (a 12-person team pays $299 base plus $58, or $357/month before transaction fees), Service Fusion at flat $208/$325/$533 per month with unlimited users, and ServiceTitan as custom-priced, noting it analyzed 500+ verified reviews and characterizes ServiceTitan's real cost as roughly $8,000 to $15,000 per year with months-long onboarding. FieldCamp itself uses custom pricing and positions native AI dispatching, an AI Receptionist for 24/7 call answering, and automated payment reminders (teams report 3x faster invoice collection) as the differentiators. Playbook relevance: the per-seat breakpoints and payment-fee layer are exactly where small shops misprice their software costs, and the review-verified ServiceTitan cost band gives a grounded comparison point.
- https://fieldcamp.ai/alternatives/housecall-pro/ (undated)
