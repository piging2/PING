## Google adds local businesses to its aggregator and supplier search units
Google's DMA-era search framework gives directories a dedicated Aggregator Unit, fed through a Point of Interest feed, and direct providers a companion Supplier Unit, plumbers named explicitly among them. The change surfaced September 18 in Google's Search Central documentation, [flagged by Barry Schwartz](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-local-business-query-units-42115.html) and [unpacked by NetContentSEO](https://netcontentseo.net/article/google-quietly-expands-aggregator-and-supplier-search-units-to-local-businesses-863). It is EEA-only, and it is one more surface between the homeowner and the shop.
