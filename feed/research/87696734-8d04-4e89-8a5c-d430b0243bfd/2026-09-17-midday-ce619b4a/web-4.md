## Finding 1
Wisetack's own home-services merchant page states a flat 3.9% transaction fee for the contractor, with an added variable fee when the customer selects an extended 0% APR plan of 6, 12, or 24 months (marked with a footnote dagger). The page states no publication date. Coverage runs $500 to $65,000 per job with terms from 3 months to 10 years, though a footnote limits some merchants to 3 to 60 months; consumer APRs range from 0% to 35.9% based on amount requested and creditworthiness, with no prepaid finance charges or participation fees, and state interest rate caps may apply. The worked example on the page: a $5,500 purchase costs $253.54 per month for 24 months at 9.9% APR, or $1,833.33 per month for 3 months at 0% APR. The four-step workflow: (1) the contractor sends a prequalification link (optional) with the estimate and the customer sees the maximum approved amount, (2) at price agreement the contractor sends an application link and the customer applies and locks in terms, (3) the contractor completes the work and the customer confirms it is done, (4) funds arrive by bank transfer within 1 to 3 business days depending on the bank. On approval friction, Wisetack's evaluator document (filename-dated April 2026, no publication date stated inside it) says it runs only a soft credit check when a customer applies, so customers can see options and choose terms with no credit impact; the application is fully digital and completed in seconds; and once it is complete, both customer and merchant know instantly whether the customer is approved and what the options are. That document also says Wisetack pays merchants one business day after a job is complete, integrates directly with Jobber and Housecall Pro so financing can be offered from inside existing estimates and invoices, runs an average call response time of 34 seconds with an NPS of 78, and ships a free marketing toolkit of printed materials, copy blurbs, and digital graphics. Wisetack's own marketing claims on the merchant page: jobs financed through Wisetack are 4.5 times bigger than jobs paid for another way, 87% of businesses won a job because they offered Wisetack monthly payments, and 88% of customers who financed were happy and relieved it was offered. Note for the playbook: Wisetack's site states coverage up to $65,000 while the BBB Septic example capped eligible projects at $25,000, so the ceiling varies by merchant and lending partner. None of this material appeared in the BBB Septic page the paper already used.

## Finding 2
Housecall Pro's own help center (the page states no publication date) publishes the full processing-fee card for Housecall Pro Payments. Standard cards swiped, tapped, or chipped with a card reader or Tap to Pay on Mobile cost 2.59% per transaction, with Tap to Pay carrying an additional $5 per active device per month. Standard cards entered online by the customer through a digital invoice cost 2.99%. Cards entered manually by staff, scanned in the mobile app, or saved as a card on file cost 3.49%. American Express and commercial, corporate, and business cards cost 3.49% through any method. ACH/bank transfer costs 1% per transaction. PayPal and Venmo paid via an invoice cost 2.99%. PayPal Pay Later and Klarna buy-now-pay-later each cost 4.99%. Consumer financing starts at 3.9%. Mobile check deposit is free. Fees are deducted before the deposit, so the net amount after the fee is what lands in the bank. Standard payout is 2 business days, with instant funding through Instapay at an additional 1%; ACH standard payout is 1 to 2 business days. Activation takes a few minutes: connect the business bank account and verify business details under My Money > Set up payments. Payment methods activate automatically, and the shop controls which methods appear on all invoices or per invoice. An independent comparison updated August 15, 2026, with rates re-verified against the vendors' published pages on July 27, 2026, corroborates Housecall Pro's card rates as 'as low as 2.59%' with 1% bank payments.

## Finding 3
For Jobber Payments, vendor-published rates verified against getjobber.com on July 27, 2026 (per the Augmented Trades comparison updated August 15, 2026) are 2.9% plus 30 cents per card transaction, 1% for ACH, and 2.7% plus 30 cents for Tap to Pay. Separately, SoftwareSuggest's Jobber page, whose pricing information it says was last updated September 13, 2026, quotes Jobber's own pricing page as showing digital payments 'as low as 2.7% +30c'. Processing fees sit on top of the subscription: a Jobber plan is still required, e.g. Connect at $199/month no-commitment for 5 users per the comparison's July 2026 verification. The comparison notes payment processing is where software quietly costs money and cites the $4,000 water-heater-job math where fractions of a percent are real dollars. It discloses affiliate relationships with both Jobber and Housecall Pro and advises checking the numbers against a live demo.

## Finding 4
A current 8-processor comparison for contractors, surfaced in this week's crawl (the page states no publication date), frames the real monthly math for a small shop. It characterizes Jobber at high-2% plus fixed for cards with around 1% ACH transfers, and Housecall Pro at high-2% plus fixed with around 1% ACH payments; both require the CRM subscription and both charge extra for instant payouts, roughly 1% extra for Jobber and an instant-deposit fee on top for Housecall Pro. Its rule of thumb: a small plumbing shop running $80,000 per month through cards at roughly 3% pays about $2,400 in processing, and shifting half of the larger invoices to low-cost ACH keeps hundreds in the pocket each month. It also notes contractors pay more than retail because of bigger average tickets and more card-not-present transactions (phone deposits, emailed links, saved cards), and recommends cards for deposits and small service calls while pushing ACH for progress draws and finals on large invoices.
