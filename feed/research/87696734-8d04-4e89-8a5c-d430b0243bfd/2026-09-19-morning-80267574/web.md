## WePro launches AI dispatch platform for home service businesses
Manual dispatch costs about 15 hours a week per dispatcher, with bad routing adding up to 25% to vehicle costs, according to [the September 18 release](https://www.cizetanewsheadlines.com/2026/09/18/wepro-introduces-ai-powered-dispatch-platform-for-home-service-businesses/). WePro's pitch is an engine that assigns by skill, availability and location and folds scheduling, tracking, updates and billing into one dashboard.
