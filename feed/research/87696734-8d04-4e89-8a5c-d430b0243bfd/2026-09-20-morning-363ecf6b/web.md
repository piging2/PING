## Grand Junction rec center opens with free weekend
The [city lists](https://www.gjcity.org/1230/Grand-Junction-Community-Recreation-Cent) free public use 5 to 8 pm Friday, September 25 and 9 am to 5 pm Saturday, September 26 at 2836 Recreation Way. A 4 pm ribbon cutting opens the weekend. Per [the opening guide](https://kubcthecanyon.com/ixp/507/p/colorado-grand-junction-recreation-center-opens/), Saturday pool entry runs in three first-come sessions and regular hours start Monday, September 28 at 5:30 am.
