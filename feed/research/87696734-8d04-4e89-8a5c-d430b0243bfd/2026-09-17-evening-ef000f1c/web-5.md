## Finding 1
QuoteIQ, an all-in-one field service CRM for home and field service professionals, announced September 14, 2026 in Savannah, Georgia the launch of QuoteIQ MCP, described as the first native Model Context Protocol connector built directly into a home service CRM. The connector links a contractor's QuoteIQ account to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or other MCP-compatible AI assistants so everyday-language conversation can prepare estimates and invoices, check unpaid balances, look up customers and account histories, view schedules, book jobs, and monitor inventory. AI-prepared changes are previewed before being applied and require approval inside QuoteIQ; requests are recorded in an audit trail, changes can be undone, and the connector uses secure OAuth with per-plan permissions. Setup takes about two minutes with no API keys or development work, and the connector is included across all QuoteIQ subscription plans. Grade: STRONG INDICATION for the launch and feature facts, since the material is the vendor's own press release carried by ABNewswire with no independent outlet coverage yet; the vendor's marketing claims inside it (first-of-its-kind status, 40,000-plus users, the $40 million acquisition rejection) are WEAK INDICATION as self-reported figures. The playbook note for Nolan: this is the competitive shape to watch, because conversational AI estimating and invoicing that sits on top of a contractor's existing CRM is exactly the kind of practical automation PING Social recommends, and a prospect already on QuoteIQ would not need a separate quoting bot. ## Finding 2
Review Rover, a Google review automation platform built for home service businesses, was announced June 17, 2026 (dated on the release) as a system that fires an automated SMS-and-email review sequence the moment a job is marked complete or an invoice is paid, with no manual action from the owner or crew. The default cadence is an SMS within minutes of job close, an email at 24 hours, and a second email at 72 hours, and a five-touch sequence combining three SMS messages and two emails was in beta at the time. Its Smart Prompt feature shows each customer a short service-specific starter draft (an HVAC tune-up prompt differs from a roof repair prompt), which customers edit and post from their own Google account, plus negative feedback routed privately to the owner instead of to Google. It connects to Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks Online, Stripe, and 8,000-plus tools via Zapier, and is priced at $97 per month for the first Google Business Profile with each additional location at $50 per month. Grade: STRONG INDICATION for the tool's existence and feature set (vendor release with founder details); the vendor's performance claims (20 to 30 percent conversion of contacted customers, roughly three times higher conversion on the five-touch beta sequence, median 312 percent lift in monthly review velocity) are WEAK INDICATION as self-reported numbers. Note on age: this piece is three months old, not a September launch, and no September review-automation launch surfaced, so it files as the still-current playbook option for automated review requests rather than as September news.
