## Finding 1
FieldEdge announced on September 15, 2026 a direct integration with Free2Grow, an AI answering platform for home service businesses, from Atlanta, GA. The integration is built on a custom API developed in partnership with FieldEdge, and Free2Grow is the first AI answering solution to integrate with the platform since FieldEdge opened its system to third-party partners. FieldEdge users can now have inbound calls answered automatically 24/7 with appointments booked directly into the system. Free2Grow's AI also responds to leads from Google Local Services Ads, Facebook, Angi, Yelp, and other channels through its Speed to Lead feature. The companies plan to expand the integration in the coming months with additional automations and deeper data synchronization. Free2Grow already integrates with ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, and HouseCall Pro. A small shop on FieldEdge could buy this and use it with no migration: calls get answered and booked while staff stay on the job.

## Finding 2
Quotr, an AI construction estimating platform based in San Francisco, introduced a new per-seat pricing structure for Quotr Software, its self-serve AI takeoff and estimating product. The page states no publication date; it surfaced as updated roughly four days before September 18, 2026. Three plans replace the previous single team plan. Quotr Lite costs $79 per seat per month and covers core AI takeoff: a basic AI agent, on-screen takeoff tools, AI symbol detection, area detection, and a custom database. Quotr Plus costs $299 per seat per month and adds advanced AI, the full Quotr database, guided onboarding, project sharing, 2,000 square feet of monthly done-for-you takeoff service credits from Quotr's estimating desk, and a 10 percent discount on Quotr procurement services. Quotr Enterprise is custom priced with priority support, a dedicated customer success manager, on-site onboarding, and customized workflows. Existing customers keep their current plan and price with no increase, and get the new features at no extra cost. Plans are month-to-month, and a contractor can start on Lite and move to Plus at any time. This is a pricing change a small shop can actually act on: a single estimator gets AI takeoff at $79 a month.
- https://quotr.ai/blog/new-pricing/ (undated)

## Finding 3
A builts.ai case study, surfaced as published roughly two days before September 18, 2026 (the page itself states no publication date), describes how a 24-tech residential HVAC contractor in the Toronto area raised first-call bookings by 28 percent by pairing dispatch automation with voice AI, without leaving ServiceTitan. Baseline during early summer: a 46 percent first-call booking rate for inbound calls, an 18 percent missed-call rate on hot days, and an average speed to answer of 1 minute 45 seconds during peaks. The automation classifies each service request, checks the ServiceTitan calendar, and books the best-fit slot using skill tags, 30-minute proximity windows, schedule protection with a 15-minute travel buffer, and AM/PM customer windows, writing directly to ServiceTitan with a timestamped note in the job activity log. Voice AI answers in seconds during peak hours and after hours, handling new service requests, tune-ups, warranty lookups, appointment changes, and basic troubleshooting, with warm transfers including a transcript summary to CSRs for complex installs, price negotiations, and escalations. Four weeks pre-go-live versus four weeks post-go-live: first-call booking rate went from 46 percent to 59 percent, missed-call rate dropped from 18 percent to 3 percent during peaks, average speed to answer for overflow calls fell to about 8 seconds, same-day dispatch rose from 22 percent to 35 percent, manual rescheduling calls dropped 62 percent, and about 12 hours per week of CSR time shifted to installs and follow-up. The vendor notes the same playbook works with Housecall Pro or Jobber. A small shop could buy this layered on the field-service software it already runs, with the whole rollout taking one week in the case study.
- https://builts.ai/blog/hvac-dispatch-automation-raised-first-call-bookings-28-percent/ (undated)
