## Finding 2
American Ratings Corporation, the creator of Diamond Certified Resource, announced it is bringing its research-based rating system from the San Francisco Bay Area to Denver, Arapahoe, Boulder and Jefferson Counties in Colorado. The expansion begins with roofing contractors that pass Diamond Certified's 12-step rating process and score Highest in Quality and Helpful Expertise. Colorado roofing companies can apply for Diamond Certified until September 23. A limited number of Founder positions are available in each county on a first-applied basis, with five years of free top placement, a rate lock and a money-back guarantee. CEO Greg Louie said the company is focusing on roofing contractors because it is a region where homeowners deal with a lot of hail and storm damage.
