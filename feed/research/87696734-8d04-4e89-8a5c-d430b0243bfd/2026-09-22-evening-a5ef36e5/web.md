## Home Service Scorecard ties tech pay to company profit
Home Service Engine, a fractional ServiceTitan back office for residential contractors, launched [Home Service Scorecard](https://wkow.marketminute.com/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-22-home-service-engine-releases-home-service-scorecard-linking-technician-pay-to-company-profit) today. The shop's annual profit goal becomes a daily earnings target for every tech, CSR and salesperson, who check a leaderboard and what the day is worth in performance pay. Founder Danny Peavey, on the problem it solves: "Most contractors have a profit goal their team has never seen."
