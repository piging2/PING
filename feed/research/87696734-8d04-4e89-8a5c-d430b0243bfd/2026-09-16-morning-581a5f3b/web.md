## Rad Coffee co-founder launches DeskPanther, built with AI tools
After running three Rad Coffee shops on seven or eight separate apps, Jade Valore built [DeskPanther](https://dailycoffeenews.com/2026/09/15/rad-coffee-co-founder-unleashes-deskpanther-operations-software/) herself with AI-assisted development tools and launched it publicly on September 8. One web app now covers inventory counts, repairs, cash drops, and shift notes at $39 per location per month, with no POS integration by design. She puts it bluntly: 'DeskPanther is nothing like what's out there, because if it was, I would be using that. It's not, and that's why I created it.'
