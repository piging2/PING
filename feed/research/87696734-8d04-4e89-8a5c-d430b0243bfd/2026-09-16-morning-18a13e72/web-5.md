## Finding 1
An independent August 2026 pricing comparison of five AI receptionist services gives concrete contractor-relevant packaging. Voksha starts at $49/month (Pro for solo practitioners), $99/month Premium, $899+/month Enterprise, flat monthly with 24/7 answering and HIPAA compliance. Smith.ai starts at $95/month for AI Receptionist (includes prepaid calls, $2.40 per call overage; a 150-call month lands around $350 to $400) and $292.50/month for the live virtual receptionist tier with North America-based human fallback, plus 7,000+ integrations including ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro. Goodcall starts at $59/month (detailed tiers: Starter $79/month for 100 unique customers, Growth $129/month for 250, Scale $249/month for 500, $0.50 per unique customer overage, 30% off annual) and prices per unique customer rather than per minute or per call, which favors businesses with high repeat-caller volume; note it cannot port an existing business number and most integrations run through Zapier. My AI Front Desk is $79/month on annual billing ($99/month standard, 200 minutes on Starter) with unlimited calls on most plans. Rosie AI starts at $49/month. The comparison warns that Smith.ai per-call overages create budget uncertainty for seasonal or spiky call patterns, and that Goodcall's Zapier-based sync can introduce data latency versus native FSM integrations.

## Finding 2
A September 2026 independent comparison of missed-call text-back tools ranks HighLevel best overall for local service businesses at $97/month Starter ($297/month Pro), the only tool at that price passing all five of the author's Missed Call Recovery Test points: native auto-text from the business number, two-way SMS, CRM contact creation, automated follow-up sequences, and booking. CallRail does missed-call text-back natively at $50/month with a 14-day free trial, paired with best-in-class call tracking and attribution, but it is not a CRM (no pipeline, booking, or email automation) and follow-up automation is limited. Keap starts at $299/month with missed-call text-back not clearly native. Jobber ($49/month entry) and Housecall Pro ($59/month entry) do not include missed-call text-back natively; two-way SMS sits on higher tiers and Jobber's AI Receptionist is an add-on. Key playbook caveats from the piece: SMS is usage-based on top of plan prices (HighLevel bills through LC Phone), A2P 10DLC carrier registration is required before sending at scale, and a tool that sends one canned reply without follow-up automation is a notification, not a recovery system.
- https://jaysonlinereviews.com/best-crm-with-missed-call-text-back/ (undated)

## Finding 3
Botphonic's 2026 stress test ran seven AI receptionists (Botphonic, Goodcall, Numa, Smith.ai, AnswerForce, Signpost, Hatch) through peak Monday-morning call surges, an 11 PM HVAC emergency, dispatch conflicts, and missed-call follow-up against Jobber and ServiceTitan environments. Only Botphonic and Goodcall maintained zero dropped-call rates in the peak surge (Goodcall handled 14 simultaneous calls); only Botphonic and Smith.ai handled the after-hours emergency without routing to voicemail, though Smith.ai required manual dispatch-board entry afterward. Typical AI receptionist pricing for home services runs $150 to $500/month depending on call volume and integration tier; Smith.ai charges per conversation, predictable at low volume but expensive past 200+ calls/month. The test's key buying distinction: native FSM integration (Botphonic real-time sync, Goodcall native ServiceTitan) versus Zapier-based integrations (Smith.ai, AnswerForce) with 1 to 3 minute sync delays that matter for real-time dispatch conflict handling. It also notes Jobber launched its own native AI Receptionist in August 2025, available on its Plus plan or as a Grow plan add-on, matching caller IDs to client profiles and booking directly, worth evaluating for Jobber shops alongside dedicated tools. ROI framing cited: average revenue lost per missed home-services call is $1,200 (Housecall Pro analysis), and businesses responding to after-hours leads within 5 minutes convert 8x better (Invoca).
- https://botphonic.ai/best-ai-receptionist-software-for-home-service-companies/ (undated)

## Finding 4
Current published pricing for review-generation software contractors buy: NiceJob starts at $45/month for Lite (Reviews) and $75/month for Standard (Reviews), covering 1 to 2,500 customers with Google and Facebook review invites, SMS review invites, automated invite campaigns, and review replies; website bundles (Lite or Standard + Convert Website) run the same $45/$75 per month plus $100/month for the Convert site with a $345 setup fee. The same pricing page lists competitor starting prices for the playbook: ReviewBuzz from $199/month, ReviewGain from $49/month, Grab Your Reviews from $23.17/month, Reviews Manager from $39.99/month, and ReviewRev from $10/month per user. NiceJob offers a free trial but no free plan, and integrates with Jobber, QuickBooks, HubSpot, and Zapier.
