## Finding 1
WestCO Roofing (rendered WestCo Roofing on its business page) is re-introducing itself on Colorado's Western Slope. Founder Deborah Tuttle posted on September 10, 2026 (post created September 11, 2026 UTC) that she is starting over to build the roofing business after previously relying on referrals, now door-knocking to generate work. She reported 15 houses knocked in three days with 3 claims filed and a goal of 100 doors a day, while balancing sales work with existing client management and motherhood duties. The photo shows Tuttle in a car wearing a pink tank top with the WESTCO ROOFING sunset-over-mountains logo. The post drew 170 likes and 31 comments, all supportive, with commenters offering referrals and requesting business cards. A separate WestCo Roofing business-page post on September 11, 2026 shows the crew at work on a residential roofing job in Fruita, Colorado, with three workers on the roof of a light-colored two-story house. A blue WestCo Roofing yard sign in the front yard lists phone number 970-596-9779 and a QR code, and the caption offers inspections. This is a trade self-announcement within the last 48 hours and is distinct from the Honk-Key Lock opening already covered.
