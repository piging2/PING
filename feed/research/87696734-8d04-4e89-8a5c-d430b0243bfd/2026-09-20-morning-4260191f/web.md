## ChatGPT and Claude can now run your Meta ads
Meta now lets you point ChatGPT or Claude at your Facebook and Instagram ads through its Ads AI Connectors, and the setup is a normal Meta Business login with no code and no API keys. Once connected, the AI can pull reporting, audit performance, and draft or edit campaigns, but everything it creates lands paused, so nothing spends until a human clicks go. Check Business Settings, then Integrations, before you connect anything, and confirm exactly what each agent can touch, write access most of all, as [this week's marketing roundup](https://www.b2the7.com/news-blog/marketing-trends-september-21-2026) lays out.
