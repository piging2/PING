## MAITVIER builds AI growth framework for U.S. small businesses
MAITVIER LLC [announced](https://www.abnewswire.com/pressreleases/maitvier-develops-ai-growth-framework-for-us-small-businesses_838188.html) the American SME Growth Accelerator, a four-part framework pairing a growth-assessment platform and an AI-based marketing intelligence engine with a workforce academy and support programs. It is still in development, and the premise is familiar: small businesses face the same customer-acquisition and pricing dilemmas as large ones, but rarely have the analysts to work through them.
