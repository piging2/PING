## Mesa County puts $80M 29 Road interchange on November ballot
The measure would let the county issue bonds repaid from existing sales tax revenue, with no tax increase, per the [county announcement](https://www.mesacounty.us/news/commissioners/board-county-commissioners-approves-ballot-measure-29-road-interchange). If voters approve, final design starts on the I-70 interchange and widening 29 Road to four lanes between Patterson and I-70, with new bike and pedestrian paths.
