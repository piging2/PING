## Finding 1
Mesa County will undertake a road resurfacing project starting Sept. 30 in the Loma and Redlands areas, operating Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., with completion anticipated by mid-October. Project locations are 12 Road from US 6 to O Road in Loma, E Road from 23 Road to Vista Grande Road in the Redlands, and S. Broadway from CO 340 to 20 Road in the Redlands. The scope is milling 2 inches of existing asphalt and applying a 2-inch hot mix asphalt overlay. Travel impacts are one-lane alternating traffic with traffic control measures in place and minor delays expected. The county lists a project hotline at 970-456-1481 and email resurfacingproject@gmail.com. The page states no publication date.

## Finding 2
The Colorado Department of Transportation, in partnership with United Companies, will begin resurfacing operations on US 6 east of Fruita from Mile Point 20 to MP 26 on Sept. 13. The page also describes it as a resurfacing project on US 6 between Fruita and Grand Junction beginning Sept. 13, 2026, consisting of asphalt resurfacing, guardrail repairs, Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) improvements, and pavement marking, scheduled for completion in November. Motorists can expect nighttime single-lane closures with flagging operations and alternating one-way traffic Sunday through Friday from 7 p.m. to 7 a.m., plus shoulder closures and reduced speeds in the work zone. The page notes construction schedules are weather-dependent and subject to change. The page states no publication date.

## Finding 3
Intermountain Health's new Matchett Park Clinic opens Sept. 28, 2026 inside the new Grand Junction Community Recreation Center (CRC) at 2836 Recreation Way. The clinic will offer physical therapy and rehabilitation including aquatic therapy, plus primary care focused on wellness exams, sports physicals and annual vaccines, and orthopedic-related rehabilitation services for recovery after surgery, injury rehabilitation, pain management, and strength and mobility improvement. Intermountain Health St. Mary's Regional Hospital is described as the official health and wellness partner of the CRC. Clinicians will be available for media tours at 1 p.m. Monday, Sept. 21 at Intermountain Health Matchett Park Clinic, Grand Junction Recreation Center, 2836 Recreation Way, Suite A. The page includes an image labeled CRCAquaticTherapyAdults-9 showing aquatic therapy.

## Finding 4
Alluvial, a new furniture store, is now open at 421 Colorado Avenue in downtown Grand Junction, next door to Roots. The store offers furniture brands not previously available in Grand Junction, along with home decor, art and custom-order furniture, operating as a showroom for living-room, bedroom and dining furniture, chairs, bookshelves and local art. Morgan Woodward is the primary owner with Jenna Smith holding a smaller ownership stake. The article states Alluvial opened on September 12 and was already full of merchandise when visited. The page states no publication date.
