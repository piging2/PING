## Brookfield to buy plumbing maker Reliance for $2.8B
On September 15, Brookfield agreed to buy all of Reliance Worldwide, the market leader in push-to-connect plumbing fittings, for US$3.38 a share in cash. The roughly US$2.8 billion deal was unanimously recommended by the board and is expected to close in Q1 2027. The tell is Brookfield's own thesis: replacement work in aging housing stock throws off resilient cashflows, and a new generation of tradespeople is adopting the fittings.
