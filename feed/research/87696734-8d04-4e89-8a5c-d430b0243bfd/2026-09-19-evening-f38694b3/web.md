## ChatGPT users sue OpenAI over contractors reading their chats
Hundreds of contractors paid over $50 an hour read real ChatGPT conversations under OpenAI's Project Lily, [first reported by 404 Media on September 14](https://www.hypefresh.com/openai-is-hiring-hundreds-of-contractors-to-secretly-read-chatgpt-conversations/). They score full exchanges on a one-to-seven scale, sometimes seeing memory summaries with past topics and rough location, past a privacy filter the company admits can miss identifiers ([report](https://www.webpronews.com/openai-contractors-pore-over-chatgpt-secrets-for-50-an-hour/)). That practice became a proposed class action in California federal court on Wednesday, with the "improve the model for everyone" toggle on by default for free, Plus and Pro accounts, and off only for Enterprise, Business and Edu.
