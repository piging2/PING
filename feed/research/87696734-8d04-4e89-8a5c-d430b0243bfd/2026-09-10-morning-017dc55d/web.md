## LSA history does not survive the move to Google Ads
Google is moving Local Services Ads into Google Ads between August and December, and the email saying an account is ready means it is go time. [Anna Anderson](https://www.roofingcontractor.com/articles/102664-google-local-services-ads-change-what-roofers-need-to-know), CEO of A-Squared Marketing, told Roofing Contractor there is no pause and leads will live in Google Ads, not the LSA dashboard.

Account details carry over while history does not, so save what you want for benchmarking now. The quiet edge is clean listings: licensing, insurance, ownership and contact details matching across Google Ads and your Business Profile, where tighter connections punish small mismatches.
