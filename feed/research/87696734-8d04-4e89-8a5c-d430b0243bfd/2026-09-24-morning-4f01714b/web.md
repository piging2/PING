## Labor shortages plague $230B kitchen and bath sector
Nearly half of kitchen and bath firms say staffing gaps have kept them from taking on new work, according to [NKBA's 2026 workforce study](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/nkba-2026-skilled-labor-study/) of more than 500 professionals in the U.S. and Canada.
