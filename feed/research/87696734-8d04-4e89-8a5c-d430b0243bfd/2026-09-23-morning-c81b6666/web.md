## Missed calls cost plumbing $8.2B a year, study claims
[Waverly Research's new analysis](https://www.abnewswire.com/pressreleases/clogged-toilet-repair-delays-leave-homeowners-waiting-as-industry-faces-82-billion-loss_837244.html) of emergency plumbing demand found that 91% of emergency jobs go to whichever shop answers the phone first, regardless of price or reviews. The industry still misses 58% of its incoming calls, worth $800 to $2,000 each: 40% of those calls land after hours, when tickets run 40 to 60% higher.
