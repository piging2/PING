## Auburn HVAC shop adds electrical, renames itself
Two career HVAC technicians opened Air Pro Solutions in Auburn, Washington in 2024. The shop [announced today](https://business.am-news.com/am-news/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-26-auburn-contractor-air-pro-solutions-adds-electrical-services-and-becomes-air-pro-solutions-heating-cooling-and-electrical) ([also carried by the Evening Leader](https://business.theeveningleader.com/theeveningleader/article/marketersmedia-2026-9-26-auburn-contractor-air-pro-solutions-adds-electrical-services-and-becomes-air-pro-solutions-heating-cooling-and-electrical)) it is adding residential electrical work and renaming itself Air Pro Solutions Heating, Cooling & Electrical. The logic is the shared ticket: furnaces, heat pumps and mini-splits all run on dedicated circuits, so one crew takes a system upgrade from estimate to final connection instead of handing part of the job to a second contractor.
