## Google Business Profile brings back post view counts
The free posts contractors publish on their Google profiles are measurable again: per the [September Small Business Bulletin](https://netcontentseo.net/article/google-business-profile-brings-back-post-level-view-counts-after-three-years-774), Google is restoring per-post view counts, a move Search Engine Journal's [roundup](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-redesign-business-profile-post-views-seo-pulse/589151/) confirms is underway (that page states no publication date). Each post card shows combined Search and Maps views for the previous 18 months, the metric Google retired on February 20, 2023, and it is not in the API yet. Views only, no clicks: enough to audit which updates customers actually saw, not what they did after.
