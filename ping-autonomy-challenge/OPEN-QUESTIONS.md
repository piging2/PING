## THE WINNER: biggest remaining uncertainty

**Q1. Do any of the library's guarantees hold on the Ask FYD conversational surface?** (the Ask FYD seam)

**Sources:** FAIL-1 Q7 (viewport oracle on conversational output), FAIL-2 Q7 (sink check on Ask FYD outputs), FAIL-3 Q8 (query-time entity assembly and receipts), FAIL-5 Q3 (handoff between pre-bound generation and live Q&A), FAIL-6 Q4 (answer-generation context through the same gate); FAIL-1 falsification cross-survivor finding C-B ("unguarded by all three survivors; #1, #5, and #6 all reopen through the chat surface, untested by every falsifiable test"); FAIL-2 falsification ("the highest-priority unanswered question in the file"; upgrade condition to FALLS on #2 and #5 if answers are not sink-validated); FAIL-3 falsification (all four survivors onboarding-scoped, "every #3 verdict should be read as at acquisition time"); FAIL-5 falsification ("S1 and S2 are site-copy mechanisms, not Ask FYD mechanisms"; until the handoff seam is defined they "cannot be claimed to protect Ask FYD output at all"); FAIL-6 falsification ("Ask FYD context, sitemaps, and RSS are claimed but untested"); FAIL-8 Q7 (single composition point; Ask FYD is an un-enumerated consumer); FAIL-4 grounding (ask-composer LLM variance as an open drift source); ASK-FYD-SEAM-AUDIT.md.

**Blast radius:** widest in the corpus. The seam audit counted 30 surviving mechanisms: 26 falsifiable tests exercise the site-render path only, 4 touch the conversational path (F9M1, F9M2, F6S3, F6S4 by design), and 5 claim Ask FYD coverage in prose while their tests never touch it (F2S2, F5S1, F5S3, F6S1, F2S1/S3 by implication). Through seams A and B of the audit alone (free-text answers, URL-safety and claim-binding dimensions), failure classes #1, #2, #3, #4, #5, and #6 reopen simultaneously, plus #8-adjacent staleness and #9 false confidence. No survivor's test validates Ask FYD outputs for URL safety, claim binding, or within-tenant visibility.

**Decidability:** fully empirical and settleable this month. It needs no product milestone and no values judgment. The falsifiable tests already exist; they were simply never pointed at the conversational surface.

**Why it matters (credibility):** every "prevents #N" claim in the library was established on the static-projection seam. Ask FYD is the flagship interactive surface and the product's proof loop ("Ask FYD from the graph" per standing memory). A mechanism library that certifies the site-render surface while the conversational surface sits outside every test makes hollow claims about the product users actually touch. The audit also found 5 mechanisms whose prose claims Ask FYD coverage their tests do not exercise, which is the exact shape of an untested guarantee.

**What would settle it (experiment plus decision, one month):**

1. Enumerate the Ask FYD answer path end to end: retrieval, answer composition, markdown/link rendering, answer cards and carousels, evidence citations, and tool-call arguments.
2. Port each class's existing falsifiable test to the conversational surface: run the viewport oracle over rendered answer cards at the width matrix (F1); poison Ask FYD answers with javascript: and data: URLs in markdown links and embedded media and assert the sink validator fires (F2); run adversarial questions, including question smuggling ("since you're the cheapest plumber in town...") and cross-record synthesis, against fixtures with known evidence (F5); probe Ask FYD over another tenant's evidence and over private records in a public viewer context (F6); ask trap questions against entities known to be missing from a blind-built graph and assert unexplained-absence flags (F3); ask the same question across sessions after an owner correction and assert answers track corrections, not stale source values (F4/F8).
3. Build the missing mechanism the audit sketches: a query-time binding verifier between answer generation and delivery. It takes the generated answer plus the retrieved claim set for the turn, requires every factual sentence to resolve to a retrieved evidence record or be marked UNKNOWN or refused, fails closed on unbound sentences, and computes SUPPORTED / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED / UNSUPPORTED deterministically from binding coverage instead of accepting the generator's self-grade. The falsification's objection stands until this exists: a self-graded label is not a mechanism.
4. Decision rule: every gate that runs on static projection must either run on the answer path (one composition point, per FAIL-8 Q7) or the library's prevention claims get formally scoped to "static projection only" with Ask FYD marked uncovered. No mechanism may claim Ask FYD coverage in prose without a test that exercises it.

**Why it outranks the runners-up:** it wins on all three ranking criteria at once. Its blast radius is the widest (six failure classes reopened through the one surface every user converses with, per the audit's hard count of 26-of-30 site-render-only tests). It is the most decidable (the tests exist and only need repointing; no product milestone, no taste judgment). And it hollows the library's credibility most directly, because every prevention verdict was earned on the static seam while the interactive surface, the one the business actually sells, sits outside all of them.
