# PING Autonomy Grand Challenge: Mechanism Library (draft)

**Date:** 2026-09-23. **Mode:** research and synthesis only. No implementation code and no abstract autonomy philosophy.
**Gate:** a mechanism survives only if it honestly prevents at least 2 of the 9 failure classes after falsification.

**Failure classes:** #1 mobile-overflow, #2 unsafe-url, #3 graph-blindness, #4 fixture-drift, #5 claim-binding, #6 visibility-error, #7 clone-sitespecs, #8 correction-loss, #9 conflict-collapse.

## Method

Thirty-two lane survivors from the 9 FAIL reports were crossed against 8 present falsification files (FAIL-1, FAIL-2, FAIL-3, FAIL-5, FAIL-6, FAIL-7, FAIL-8, FAIL-9) plus 3 merged-family falsifications (typed seam, oracle, correction). There is no FAIL-4-falsification.md: every verdict resting on FAIL-4 material is marked PENDING below.

For each survivor: every class claim marked FALLS, NOT PREVENTED, NO LONGER PREVENTED, or "does not survive" was dropped; survivors left with fewer than 2 honest classes were moved to the rejected section; duplicate mechanisms across lanes were merged into single entries; every kept entry carries the strengthened falsifiable test its falsification demanded. Verdict scale: WITHSTANDS / WEAKENED / FALLS / PENDING. No survivor in the corpus WITHSTANDS as originally specified; the honest library is narrowed survivors plus strengthened tests.

Two additional sources shaped this draft: SURVIVOR-ROSTER.md (independent audit: 30 survivors clear the gate on their own claims, 2 excluded), COVERAGE-MATRIX.md (merge proposals and weak-claim flags), ASK-FYD-SEAM-AUDIT.md (seam coverage), CUT-LOG.md (55 deduplicated cuts), DELETE-OUR-NEED.md (the wave-1 gate: exactly one wave-1 family survived, the LANE-14 handoff contract), harvest-index/MECHANISM-INDEX.md (wave-1 IDs and pinned SHAs), and INVARIANT-7-ADJUDICATION.md (admits the re-scoped LANE-18 invariant 7 as a new survivor).

**Scope convention used throughout:** every verdict below applies to the static site projection unless a test explicitly exercises another seam. The Ask FYD conversational seam is the standing exception; see Open Questions.

## M1. Typed projection seam (fail-closed constructors, verifier, sink re-validation, fail-closed defaults)

## M2. Independent empirical verification (oracle, receipts, pinned references, variance coupling, golden corrections)

**Merged from:** F1-S1, F3-S1 (folded: fixture-pinning clause; standalone excluded, see Rejected), F3-S2, F3-S4 (folded: pinned-independent-ruler clause), F7-S1, F8-S3.

**How it works.** After generation, independent checkers evaluate the output against declared expectations, with no shared trust in the generator's self-assessment, and violations block promotion. Five check shapes run as one family: (1) measured render properties in a browser harness (viewport overflow assertion, determinism byte-compare, correction-presence assertion); (2) pinned input/output fixtures per input shape with a registry that fails the gate on unregistered accepted shapes; (3) extraction receipts (candidates in vs entities out, each unexplained drop alerting, receipts diffed across regens); (4) a pinned reference parser of genuinely different implementation lineage cross-checked against production on sampled inputs; (5) input-output variance coupling (d_in over input graphs, d_out over output specs; collapse and drift both fail the gate) computed by an independent verifier; (6) a golden correction set asserted against rendered effective output as a regen gate. The merged falsification's composition verdicts are adopted: repair-or-block is struck (auto-repair is self-falsifying drift; only block-and-escalate, with any repair re-entering as a declared attributed input); the five verifiers' independence must be constructed (lineage assertions, positive controls, a verifier differential corpus the generator cannot see), not asserted; expectation updates (fixture edits, reason-code minting, threshold changes, golden-set edits) are themselves gated against a reference the update cannot move; the family is a bug-catcher, not a Goodhart-proof guarantee.

**Prevented classes, one crisp reason each:**

- **#1 mobile-overflow:** the viewport assertion measures the rendered artifact itself, so emergent compositional overflow the constructors cannot see fails the build. (WEAKENED: static load-time, same-engine only; font-swap, lazy-load, interaction-triggered, fixed off-canvas, 100vw-scrollbar, and WebKit-only shapes are outside the tested envelope.)
- **#3 graph-blindness:** receipt accounting makes silently dropped candidates loud; quarantine routing (UNRECOGNIZED_SHAPE / UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT) keeps refused shapes loud; the pinned reference parser detects silent behavior change in the production extractor. (WEAKENED: conditional on the F3-S3 routing and an independent fixture oracle; receipts catch seen-but-dropped cases, not traversal-level blindness.)
- **#4 fixture-drift:** the determinism byte-compare and receipt diffs across regens convert silent behavior change into named failures. (WEAKENED: F8-S3's #4 claim struck; the merged claim is the drift half, F1-S1's determinism plus F3-S1's drift-from-pinned plus F3-S2's count-drift plus F3-S4's narrow reference detector.)
- **#7 clone-sitespecs:** the variance-coupling gate fails byte-structure clones of distinct businesses; evidence-density and bar discipline keep the d_in metric honest. (WEAKENED, crude only; F1-S1's divergence assertion was struck as untestable; threshold gaming and median-pair convergence are outside the tested envelope.)
- **#8 correction-loss:** the golden suite pins golden-covered corrections against rendered output as a regen gate and doubles as an object-roster check (dangled anchors catch dropped entities). (WEAKENED: coverage-bounded; F3-S2's #8 claim was NOT PREVENTED as tested; the honest core is golden-covered fields plus owner-store integrity.)

**Falsifiable test (the single strongest merged-oracle test, from MERGED-oracle-falsification).** Input: nine poisoned runs plus one clean control. Run 2 (masking): the same overflow fixed with overflow-x:hidden; must FAIL and the failure must name the masking. Run 4 (pinned blindness): fixture pins the refusal bug; a repair fixing the traversal must not be reverted to satisfy the fixture; reverting the repair to keep the fixture green FAILS. Run 5 (laundering): mint a new reason code covering the dropped candidates from run 3; the explained-but-unrecognized volume budget must fire an alert; silence FAILS. Run 7 (repair determinism): run twice with repair enabled across differing font-timing environments; byte-compare final shipped DOM; every repair must appear in the provenance log as a declared attributed input change; any unlogged or environment-dependent repair FAILS and the repair branch is struck. Run 8 (retraction plus coverage): retract one golden correction in the decision store; the suite must FAIL naming the retraction and linking the store record; separately, record a real correction on a field with no golden twin, corrupt that field's read path, run regen and the suite: the suite must FAIL via the roster check; green while a real correction is lost FAILS. ## M3. Owner decisions as durable typed records (decision store, overlay, three-way regen, correction tasks)

**Merged from:** F5-S3, F8-S1, F8-S2, F9-M4.

**How it works.** An owner judgment is a typed, identity-anchored, durable record (correct-fact / confirm-fact / hide-fact, carrying stable target identity, the owner value, a snapshot of the source value it was made against, actor, timestamp, and digest-bound approval), separate from the source layer, in a dedicated decision store the regeneration pipeline may read but never writes to. Every regeneration recomposes the effective value by re-applying the owner overlay over the new source (three-way merge over base source / new source / owner overlay; owner wins ties on owner-asserted fields). When a new source observation contradicts a correction, a typed event fires (SOURCE_CHANGED_AGAINST_OWNER_ASSERTION), the owner's value stays effective, both values are preserved as a first-class conflict, and resolution routes to an owner action (keep mine / accept source / inspect) via an owner correction task. Dangling pointers (deleted evidence, revoked assertion) fail closed: the claim is omitted and flagged, never rendered as orphaned text. The merged correction falsification's composition verdicts are adopted: name the minting authority and its authentication before any authenticity claim; the effective-value authority question (re-apply-on-read vs re-apply-on-regen idempotency) and the orphan-semantics contradiction (fail-closed vs honored-orphan) must be resolved to single rules; owner-vs-owner needs a typed event and an adjudication rule; the timeless always-preserve policy is falsified on the time axis, so a conflict lifetime policy with a default and a clock plus correction freshness or re-confirmation are required before the durability claims are stated without scope qualifiers.

**Prevented classes, one crisp reason each:**

- **#8 correction-loss:** a re-dump or regen cannot silently overwrite an owner correction: the overlay is separate from the source layer, re-applied at read or regen, and source contradiction raises a named event instead of clobbering the judgment. (WEAKENED: holds in the instant, single-owner, stable-identity, in-band regime; degrades under identity reorganization, second actor, and time.)
- **#9 conflict-collapse:** source-vs-owner disagreement becomes a typed event with both values visible and a named resolution path, instead of a silent merge. (WEAKENED: source-vs-owner axis at regen time only; owner-vs-owner has no vocabulary; unresolved-over-time decays toward the cut "owner-wins-always" without a lifetime policy.)
- **#6 visibility-error:** hide-facts across re-dump stay honored: a hidden service does not resurrect when the source re-emits it. (WEAKENED: tested form only; the record-vs-field visibility joint stays open.)
- **#5 claim-binding:** corrections carry verifiable provenance (actor label, timestamp, digest-bound approval against a stated base); a rendered corrected sentence binds to the OWNER_ASSERTED record, not to free text. (WEAKENED: holds only where the minting authority is authentic; the actor label is demo-grade and the authority is unnamed.)

Dropped class claims: #4 (pruned by the merged correction falsification: the golden suite's #4 claim was struck, and #4 is not this family's claim).

**Falsifiable test (the hostile-lifetime gauntlet, from MERGED-correction-falsification).** Input: a business object with phone field F, base source F = A; Actor Alpha (verified owner identity in the harness) records correct-fact F = C_alpha against base A through the real override endpoint with digest-bound approval; Actor Beta (a distinct second claimant identity) records correct-fact F = C_beta against base A through the same endpoint; new source fixture re-emits the object merged into a renamed object with a new id absorbing the old id, and the source now says F = B, the business's genuine new number (ground truth known to the harness, not the system); a second field G carries a correction recorded 5 simulated years ago that the new source contradicts with a currently-true value. Procedure: run regeneration (three-way merge), serve the site fresh with no code changes; run N = 10 further regen cycles with the source continuing to say B, no owner resolution; inspect after the first and last runs: the effective value of F on the rendered page, the decision-store bytes (must be unmutated by regen), the event log, the owner task queue, the owner-facing surface (not just the log), the effective value of G. Expected observable outcome: a named OWNER_VS_OWNER_CONFLICT event exists (no silent last-write-wins between C_alpha and C_beta); the effective value is deterministic across serves with the conflict disclosed, not one claimant's value presented as settled; a named re-anchor event lists the new id as a candidate successor of the dead anchor; the merged object does not render with no owner decision attached; the orphaned patch on the dead id does not count as "honored" while its successor escapes; SOURCE_CHANGED_AGAINST_OWNER_ASSERTION names A and B and both values remain visible; after N silent regens every conflict is still surfaced on the owner-facing surface (not aged into a log), the lifetime policy names its default and clock, the task queue has exactly one open task per conflict (dedup key excludes timestamps); field G: the staleness must surface (re-confirmation prompt, freshness flag) or a named policy default must have fired; the decision store is byte-identical across all regens. What proves the mechanism DOESN'T work: effective F equals C_alpha or C_beta with no OWNER_VS_OWNER_CONFLICT event anywhere (silent overwrite on the third axis); the merged object renders F with no owner decision while the old patch sits "honored" on the dead id (fail-open re-identification); any conflict is findable only in an archived log after N regens (typed event decayed into the cut version); any regen step mutates the decision store (the layer separation is decorative); field G renders the 5-year-stale correction as settled fact with no freshness signal and no fired policy (staleness preservation, the mirror image of #8, defended by the family's full protection apparatus).

**Sources:** FAIL-5, FAIL-8, FAIL-9; merged correction falsification attacks (a) minting authority, (b) identity instability, (c) correction-vs-correction, (d) staleness, (e) overlay accumulation; wave-1 corroboration: LANE-14 handoff contract (the status vocabulary and CONFLICTING carry rule FAIL-9 cites; note the lane lists seven states while FAIL-9 calls it a six-state contract, unresolved in the sources).

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** The mechanical core survives: typed identity-anchored records, a decision store the source pipeline cannot write to, overlay re-application, typed conflict events, no silent overwrite. Against its designed enemy (the refresh that clobbers judgment) it holds, in the instant, with one owner and stable identities. It does not fall outright; attacks (a), (b), (c) are scope findings. Attack (d) is the exception: on the time axis the abstraction is falsified, not merely weakened, because its timeless always-preserve policy is itself the staleness failure. The verdict is WEAKENED pending two structural repairs: named minting authority with authentication and scoped correction jurisdiction; and time in the abstraction (conflict lifetime with default and clock, correction freshness or re-confirmation, patch lifecycle states).

## M4. Conflict as first-class evidence (storage, answer grammar, policy, quarantine)

**Merged from:** F9-M1, F9-M2, F9-M3, F5-S1's CONFLICTING clause, F3-S3 (unknown-shape and UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT quarantine).

**How it works.** Conflicts are stored as evidence, not merged away: contradictory observations become CONFLICT records with both sources, both values, and a resolution state; no merge ever deletes the losing observation (suppression is not deletion; post-resolution supersede records replace deletion). Certainty is attenuated at the answer seam: a conflicted value cannot be rendered or answered with the grammar of settled fact; the disclosure precedes the declarative value, and machine-readable outputs carry the same marker. An auditable source-priority policy (owner-confirmed > website > google-listing, policy authorship and legitimacy metadata, version-flipped notifications to owners, safety precedence over priority) picks the presented value while the loser stays visible and retrievable in the evidence graph. Unknown shapes and contradictory blocks at acquisition route to quarantine (UNRECOGNIZED_SHAPE / UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT) with a gap event rather than being dropped or merged. The falsifications' verdicts are adopted: M3's #6 prevention claim is cut (it is post-render detection, not prevention; kept only as detection); conflict identity over time and the normalization rule must be stated before the run, with a formatting-drift fixture proving zero false conflicts and a refetch-stability fixture proving no time-axis duplicates; retention sweeps must preserve conflicted observations; disclosure prominence and machine-readable outputs are required, not optional.

**Prevented classes, one crisp reason each:**

- **#9 conflict-collapse:** the disagreement is preserved as a record with both provenances instead of being silently merged, and the loser stays retrievable after the policy picks a winner. (WEAKENED: storage scope holds; projection and answer seams are partial; false-confidence can return through unbound answers outside the conflict grammar.)
- **#5 claim-binding:** a conflicted claim cannot bind as settled fact: the verifier refuses to present a contested value with settled-fact grammar, and policy decisions are cited as evidence. (WEAKENED: storage-level and presentation-level only; the F5-S1 CONFLICTING clause was proven only at the render-model boundary.)
- **#8 correction-loss:** corrections enter as stronger evidence with priority over source observations, and the preservation record survives the conflict; retraction deletes nothing, it appends a supersede record. (Stands at the storage level; falsification noted the deletion hole and was closed by supersede semantics.)
- **#3 graph-blindness (quarantine clause only):** contradictory blocks and unknown top-level shapes are quarantined loud with a gap event instead of being merged or dropped, so a conflict is never mistaken for a non-entity. (WEAKENED: conditional on classifier-boundary pinning, normalization before deduplication, a noise budget, and a drain SLA.)

Dropped class claims: #6 prevention from F9-M3 (detection is not prevention; retained as post-render detection).

**Falsifiable test (merged from the three source falsifications).** Input: a fixture business with website hours 8-5 vs google-listing 9-5; a formatting-drift fixture ("St." vs "Street", "8a-5p" vs "08:00-17:00") that must not produce a false conflict; a refetch fixture repeating the identical contradiction across cycles; a conflicted phone field with owner correction 7-4 under policy [owner-confirmed > website > google-listing]. Procedure: run acquisition twice; query Ask FYD for hours against the conflicted and an agreeing fixture and record the answer text; regenerate the SiteSpec; inspect the evidence graph for the loser observation after the policy pick; run the retention sweep and re-check conflicted observations. Expected observable outcome: the graph holds both observations plus a CONFLICT record UNRESOLVED; the formatting fixture yields zero CONFLICT records; the refetch fixture yields exactly one CONFLICT record (no time-axis duplicates); the Ask FYD answer against the conflicted fixture names both sources and values (or the policy winner plus a disclosed disagreement) while the agreeing fixture is stated plainly; the rendered value is the policy winner (7-4), the audit events name the rule and the loser remains retrievable; the retention sweep preserves every conflicted observation. What proves the mechanism DOESN'T work: any silent merge destroying the loser; any Ask FYD answer stating one value unqualified under conflict; any false conflict from pure formatting drift; any duplicate CONFLICT from refetch noise; any policy decision with no audit event or no retrievable loser; any conflicted observation deleted by retention; any disclosure that a machine consumer could skip (plain-text note without a machine-readable marker).

**Sources:** FAIL-9, FAIL-5 (S1 CONFLICTING clause), FAIL-3 (S3 quarantine); LANE-14 handoff contract (wave-1 corroboration FAIL-9 cites for the #9 discipline; state-count discrepancy unresolved).

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Storage-level conflict preservation holds; answer-seam attenuation holds for the conflicted slice only; the priority policy's #6 prevention claim was cut to detection; the quarantine clause is conditional on pinning, normalization, noise budget, and drain SLA that are currently unspecified. The unanswered normalization-ownership question (FAIL-9 CUT-LOG FLAG 2: naming a normalization authority contradicts the No-ClaimAuthority cut, while accepting false conflicts contradicts M1) is the sharpest contradiction in the corpus.

## M5. Hermetic projection, replay tripwire, content-derived identity

**Merged from:** F1-S3, F4-S1, F4-S2, F4-S4.

**How it works.** Projection is a pure function of declared inputs: no wall-clock reads, no RNG, no ambient I/O, no undeclared config in the render path (declared-input purity). The declared inputs include corrections, owner overrides, the pinned pipeline version, the seeded LLM copy with model id, and every environment-shaped constant. Projection output is byte-compared across runs; any divergence between runs with identical declared inputs blocks publish and names the divergent layer (replay-mismatch tripwire). Object identity is derived from content hashes, never minted from UUIDs or timestamps, so the same logical object re-derives to the same identity across runs (content-derived identity). Legitimate nondeterminism (build timestamps, nonces) is recorded once as a declared input (record-once), not recomputed. The F4 falsification is absent, so F4-S1/S2/S4 are carried at claimed scope pending falsification; F1-S3's falsification (WEAKENED) is already adopted: hermetic execution closure, digest-pinned code and dependencies, cross-machine tests, undeclared-input mutation fixtures, and anchor-rot reporting.

**Prevented classes, one crisp reason each:**

- **#4 fixture-drift:** purity makes silent drift impossible by construction: with identical declared inputs the output is byte-identical, and any divergence is a named, publish-blocking event, not a quiet change. (PENDING for F4-sourced claims; WEAKENED for F1-S3: undeclared-input mutation and cross-machine coverage required.)
- **#8 correction-loss:** corrections are declared inputs the projection function cannot silently drop; an applied/unapplied per-correction report exposes anchor rot instead of hiding it. (PENDING/WEAKENED as above; re-anchor under identity change is the known gap.)
- **#7 clone-sitespecs (F4-S1, provisional):** content-derived identity gives site identity a stable anchor that replay can check. (PENDING.)

## M6. Evidence decay with action-time re-verification and forward taint

**Admitted by adjudication:** re-scoped LANE-18 invariant 7 (INVARIANT-7-ADJUDICATION.md, 2026-09-23). As originally specified for the outreach coordinator it prevents zero of the 9 and stays dead there; re-scoped to the FYD pipeline it fills a gap no lane survivor covers.

**How it works.** Every evidence record about the external world carries an observed-at timestamp and a per-claim-class freshness window (a policy, versioned, not a number on the record). Confidence decays as the window passes: a claim whose evidence is past its window is marked STALE; stale is not false, it is un-renderable without re-checking. Before any outbound action (rendering a claim to a public site or asserting it in an Ask FYD answer), any claim on STALE evidence must be re-verified against a live source within its window; if re-verification cannot be performed, the claim fails closed (omitted or qualified), never rendered as settled fact. When a claim is falsified (a new source crawl contradicts it, or an owner correction falsifies it), the system traces forward through the derivation graph to every artifact built from that claim (render models, cached projections, serialized client graphs, digest lines, analytics rollups) and marks them TAINTED, which schedules their regeneration or re-composition. This is the evidence-graph and worker-layer statement of the same discipline F5-S3 (dangling pointers fail closed) and F9-M4 (conflict and correction task lifecycle) implement at the render seam: S3 covers revoked evidence, this covers stale evidence; F8's overlay covers consumers that pass the read seam, this covers consumers that do not.

**Prevented classes, one crisp reason each:**

- **#5 claim-binding:** a claim bound to old-but-still-resolving evidence passes F5-S1's existence check and F5-S3's dangling check, so without this mechanism it renders as settled fact with a real evidence pointer behind it. Decay plus action-time re-verification closes the staleness-shaped hole in the binding chain: support must be fresh, not merely present.
- **#8 correction-loss:** when an owner correction falsifies a source claim, F8's pull-based overlay only protects consumers that pass the single read seam; STEP-06 proved such consumers are not guaranteed. Forward taint pushes the falsification to every derived artifact, so the correction cannot die silently inside a cached projection or an analytics rollup that never re-composes.

**Falsifiable test (from the adjudication).** Input: a fixture business with a phone claim bound to a DIRECT_EVIDENCE observation recorded 400 days ago (past the phone class freshness window of 180 days); a cached render model and a digest line derived from that observation; then an owner correction changing the phone, entered through the real override path. Procedure: (1) attempt to render the site and answer "what is your phone number?" via Ask FYD without any re-acquisition; (2) record the owner correction and inspect every derived artifact for taint marks and scheduled regeneration, including the cached render model and the digest line. Expected observable outcome: (1) the phone claim does not render as settled fact on stale evidence alone; it is omitted or qualified as stale pending re-verification, and the event log names the STALE classification; (2) after the correction, the cached render model and the digest line both carry TAINTED marks tied to the falsified claim, with regeneration scheduled; the digest line is not silently left carrying the old number. What proves the mechanism DOESN'T work: (1) the 400-day-old phone renders as settled fact with its evidence pointer, because S1/S3 both pass it: decay not implemented; (2) the owner correction is effective at the live read seam (F8-S1 passes) while the cached render model and digest line still carry the falsified number with no taint mark: forward taint not implemented. The deliberate design: in scenario (2) the F8-S1 and F8-S4 tests both pass, which is exactly why this test is not redundant.

**Sources:** INVARIANT-7-ADJUDICATION.md; FAIL-5 falsification critique C4 (names the missing re-confirmation policy), FAIL-9 open question 2 (staleness bound as open question), FAIL-8 falsification ("Time is the adversary the tests forgot"), DELETE-OUR-NEED.md section (e) (the gate's kill of the original specification stands).

**Verdict: PENDING.** Admitted by adjudication as a legitimate generalization, not gate-gaming: its falsifiable tests fire in scenarios where every existing test passes while the failure occurs. No falsification pass has attacked it yet. Recorded design debt: a principled taint cutoff (one poisoned claim must not require rebuilding the world), freshness windows as versioned policy, re-verification authority (same minting-authority discipline as owner assertions), and specified graph-outage behavior.
