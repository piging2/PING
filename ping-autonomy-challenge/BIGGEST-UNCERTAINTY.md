# THE BIGGEST REMAINING UNCERTAINTY

**Date:** 2026-09-23. **Mode:** research only, no implementation, no new claims invented.
**Context:** this section is the final report's uncertainty section for the PING Autonomy Grand Challenge. It is drafted from the corpus that exists: OPEN-QUESTIONS.md, the cross-cutting findings of all 9 FAIL-N falsification files plus the 4 MERGED-*-falsification files, ARCHAEOLOGY-SUPPLEMENT.md, CLASS-INTERACTIONS.md (unresolved tensions U1-U10), and ASK-FYD-SEAM-AUDIT.md.

**Ranking rule used below:** an uncertainty is ranked by three criteria. Blast radius (how many failure classes and survivor families it touches). Decidability (whether a concrete experiment can close it this month, without a product milestone or a values judgment). Cost of being wrong (what breaks in the shipped product if we guess and miss). The winner is the one that wins on the largest share of the three.

## 1. CANDIDATE EVALUATIONS

Each candidate is stated precisely, then grounded in the falsification evidence, then given its settling shape and the cost of being wrong.

### (a) One shared projection-seam mechanism for evidence binding, visibility, URL safety, and provenance, and whether it covers Ask FYD's free-text seam

**The uncertainty, precisely:** whether a single mechanism at the projection seam can jointly enforce evidence binding, visibility classification, URL safety, and provenance without becoming an overbroad competing authority (a gate that certifies what it cannot see), and whether any such mechanism covers Ask FYD's free-text seam, where claims are composed at query time from unbounded retrieval rather than bound at build time from a closed claim set.

**Evidence for its importance:** MERGED-typed-seam-falsification Attack A (the type-minting authority problem): every version of the merged abstraction assumes a trusted constructor, gate, or verifier and leaves the trust root incomplete (deserialization bypass, license forgery on the write path, unspecified record-minting authority, OWNER_CORRECTION minting by the planner, self-graded geometry contracts). FAIL-6 falsification C1: S1 says the leak is "impossible at the gate" while S4's headline mutation assumes a bypass can be written; the resolution is structural (make the raw graph type unnameable outside the gate module) or the gate is a convention wearing a mechanism's name. FAIL-5 falsification's shared weak joints J1-J3: the representation gap (the verifier only sees what the generator declares), label trust (manufacture crosses as a mislabeled GENERATED_PRESENTATION or DERIVED slot), and unspecified write authority on evidence and owner-assertion records. FAIL-5 section 4: S1 and S2 are site-copy mechanisms, not Ask FYD mechanisms; S3's "every Ask FYD factual answer carries VALUE plus CLASSIFICATION plus EVIDENCE_REF" is asserted, not constructed, and contradicts free-text generation unless answers are composed from pre-bound fragments (in which case they are not free text). CLASS-INTERACTIONS merge-family summary: the nine reports converged independently on the typed-seam shape; all versions are WEAKENED; the availability twin (U6, exception pressure reopening #2) is the missing half.

**What would settle it:** two tracks. Track 1, minting: name the minting authorities explicitly and implement the structural resolution (raw types unnameable outside the gate; CI static gate on `as` casts targeting provenance types; an audited construction-site inventory covering deserialization, hydration, cache restore, fixture loading, and admin write paths). Track 2, the conversational seam: define the handoff seam the FAIL-5 falsification sketches (retrieval returns viewer-filtered claims; each answer sentence carries bindings to retrieved claims or is marked UNKNOWN/refused; the SUPPORTED/PARTIALLY SUPPORTED/UNSUPPORTED label is computed deterministically by the verifier, never self-graded by the LLM). Decision rule: every gate that runs on static projection must either run on the answer path or the library's prevention claims are formally scoped to static projection only.

**Cost of being wrong:** the merged seam becomes a competing authority with no authority: it certifies binding shape while unbound or mislabeled content crosses (J2), unlisted bypasses bypass it (FAIL-1 B5), and the Ask FYD prose claims are false. Fail-open where the product is most exposed.

### (b) The Ask FYD conversational seam, unguarded by every survivor family

**The uncertainty, precisely:** whether any of the 30 surviving mechanisms' prevention guarantees hold on the Ask FYD conversational surface, where answers are composed live at query time from retrieval rather than projected at build time from declared inputs, and where no survivor's falsifiable test has ever pointed.

**Evidence for its importance:** ASK-FYD-SEAM-AUDIT.md's hard count: of the 30 surviving mechanisms, 26 falsifiable tests exercise the site-render path only, 4 touch the conversational path (F9M1, F9M2, F6S3, F6S4 by design), and 5 claim Ask FYD coverage in prose while their tests never touch it (F2S2, F5S1, F5S3, F6S1, F2S1/S3 by implication). FAIL-1 falsification cross-survivor finding C-B: "#1, #5, and #6 all reopen through the chat surface, untested by every falsifiable test in the file"; Survivor 1 gates static projection, Survivor 2 gates static slots, Survivor 3 covers static projection purity. FAIL-2 falsification: Q7 (does the sink check run on Ask FYD outputs?) is "the highest-priority unanswered question in the file," with an explicit upgrade condition: if Ask FYD outputs are not sink-validated, Survivor 2's verdict upgrades to FALLS on #2 and #5. FAIL-5 section 4: two new attacks exist only at this seam (question smuggling, "since you're the cheapest plumber in town..."; cross-record synthesis presented as derived), and the triage labels (SUPPORTED/PARTIALLY SUPPORTED/UNSUPPORTED) are unbound meta-claims produced by the LLM whose honesty is in question: "a self-graded label is not a mechanism." FAIL-3 falsification Q8: all four survivors are onboarding-scoped; query-time entity assembly is untested, so "every #3 verdict should be read as 'at acquisition time.'" FAIL-6 falsification: S1's test channel list omits tool-call arguments even though the mechanism claims Ask FYD context; S4 has no Ask FYD mutation. FAIL-8 Q7: Ask FYD is an un-enumerated consumer (the single-composition-point invariant is unproven). Through seams A and B of the audit alone, failure classes #1, #2, #3, #4, #5, and #6 reopen simultaneously, plus #8-adjacent staleness and #9 false confidence.

**What would settle it:** fully empirical, no product milestone, no values judgment. Enumerate the Ask FYD answer path end to end (retrieval, answer composition, markdown/link rendering, answer cards and carousels, evidence citations, tool-call arguments). Port each class's existing falsifiable test to the conversational surface: the viewport oracle over rendered answer cards at the width matrix (F1); javascript: and data: URL poisoning in answer markdown links and embedded media, asserting the sink validator fires (F2); adversarial questions including question smuggling and cross-record synthesis against fixtures with known evidence (F5); Ask FYD over another tenant's evidence and over private records in a public viewer context (F6); trap questions against entities known to be missing from a blind-built graph, asserting unexplained-absence flags (F3); the same question across sessions after an owner correction, asserting answers track corrections rather than stale source values (F4/F8). Build the missing mechanism the audit sketches: a query-time binding verifier between answer generation and delivery, with triage labels computed deterministically from sentence bindings. Decision rule per OPEN-QUESTIONS.md: every gate runs on the answer path, or prevention claims are formally scoped to static projection only with Ask FYD marked uncovered; no mechanism may claim Ask FYD coverage in prose without a test that exercises it.

**Cost of being wrong:** every "prevents #N" claim in the library was established on the static-projection seam. Ask FYD is the flagship interactive surface and the product's proof loop ("Ask FYD from the graph"). A library that certifies the site-render surface while six failure classes reopen through the conversational surface makes hollow claims about the product users actually touch. The 5 prose-claim-but-untested mechanisms are exactly the shape of untested guarantees the challenge exists to kill.

### (c) Conflict significance and normalization (the FAIL-9 forbidden-authority paradox)

**The uncertainty, precisely:** whether conflict detection can avoid both horns of the fork. No normalization: "8-5", "8:00 AM - 5:00 PM", "8a-5p" as CONFLICT records, exploding into dozens of conflicts per field, depleting M2's credibility budget until users ignore every hedge (the boy-who-cried-wolf finding), flooding M4's task queue, and DoSing F3-S3's quarantine. A significance filter: a semantic authority that decides which disagreements count as disagreements, which is FAIL-9's failure (silent resolution) arriving through a rulebook with the family's vocabulary printed on it. And the cut list forbids semantic comparison as a new authority, while "non-identical values" across representations is itself a semantic judgment, so the normalization owner is unnamed by construction.

**Evidence for its importance:** MERGED-conflict-falsification Attack (a): the fork is at the abstraction level, not at any member; "no filter means noise that trains everyone to ignore the CONFLICTING class; a filter means a silent merge engine." Attack (b): M3's distinctiveness over silent last-write-wins reduces to "the pick is traceable by an engineer"; silent to owners and visitors is unaddressed, and policy-version flips re-pick thousands of conflicts with a perfect audit trail (silent at scale, documented). FAIL-9 falsification cross-mechanism finding 2: the cut list's rationale contradicts Q1 (detection semantics: exact-string vs semantic normalization, owner unnamed). CLASS-INTERACTIONS U4: "either name a normalization authority (contradicting the cut) or accept the explosion (contradicting M1)"; U3: no hedge budget, no per-field attenuation budget, no feedback arc from M2's attenuation rate back to M1's detection criterion.

**What would settle it:** a decision, not an experiment. Nolan (or the product authority) names the normalization owner, the significance policy (versioned, auditable, owner-visible, with the same authorship legitimacy requirements as M3's priority policy), the per-field attenuation budget, and the explosion-rate guardrails. An experiment can measure explosion rates and hedge fatigue, but no experiment can legitimize who gets to author the filter. The decision's residual is a values call about how much uncertainty the product will show.

**Cost of being wrong:** either hedged-everything product (M2 attenuation becomes wallpaper, the CONFLICTING class trains everyone to ignore it, M4's queue is a write-only log in the owner-absent majority regime) or a silent merger with an auditable costume (the filter suppresses genuine disagreements and nobody sees them; F3-S3 quarantines fields out of the graph on serialization drift).

### (d) Oracle independence and expectation rot

**The uncertainty, precisely:** whether the oracles that check the mechanisms (viewport oracle, mutation suites, golden correction sets, fixture pins, receipt counters) keep their independence and their bite over time, under cost pressure (sampling reopens the class on unsampled inputs) and under labor pressure (stale pins fail loudly on legitimate changes, training maintainers to silence the gate; silent fail-closed creates the exception pressure that reopens #2).

**Evidence for its importance:** MERGED-oracle-falsification: per-member breakage under cost pressure (F1-S1 breaks under sampling pressure, its own Q2; F7-S1 weakened; F3-S2 weakened; F3-S1 survives). The same pattern the OPEN-QUESTIONS.md runner-up 2 names across five reports: FAIL-2 Q6 (neutralization telemetry; silent fail-closed's second-order effect), FAIL-3 Q2 ("a quarantine nobody drains becomes a second /dev/null with better labeling"), FAIL-4 Q6 ("what stops a rubber-stamp update from laundering real drift?"), FAIL-8 Q6 (golden-set maintenance is S3's rot vector), FAIL-6 Q5 (mutation-set coverage as channels grow). CLASS-INTERACTIONS U9: "an ignored drift guard is no guard"; U10: quarantine as throughput tax with no budget and no owner.

**What would settle it:** emit the telemetry, name the owner of each golden set and quarantine, set drain/rotation SLAs, wire the roster check, record every sampling justification with its named residual risk. The answer is known; this is an execution gap, not a knowledge gap. It threatens whether mechanisms keep working, not whether they work.

**Cost of being wrong:** mechanisms work on day one and die quietly. Silent over-blocking of legitimate contact paths (tel: with extensions, IDN domains, mailto: with query params) generates the business pressure for broad exceptions ("skip validation for owner-entered values"), and that exception reopens #2. Guard rot is the slow version of falsification.

### (e) Correction identity stability across entity merge/split

**The uncertainty, precisely:** whether an owner correction anchored to (object id, field path) keeps its intent when the entity merges, splits, deletes and resurrects, or the field path changes, and whether any mechanism reports the orphaned correction with candidate successors instead of honoring it on a dead identity while the successor renders uncorrected.

**Evidence for its importance:** MERGED-correction-falsification Attack (b): entity merge/split, deletion plus resurrection, and tenant migration defeat the family's anchor universality; no re-anchoring protocol exists and two contradictory orphan semantics coexist (D4). The concrete merge case is fail-open in effect: the hide patch on the dead id stays "honored" (satisfying F8-S1's test disjunction) while the merged successor renders visible with no owner decision; the test passes and the owner's intent dies. FAIL-8 falsification cross-survivor observation 5: S1's disjunction ("stays honored or raises a named state") needs a third branch: honored on a live identity, or a named re-anchor event with candidate successors, or a named target-missing state; honoring an orphaned patch while a successor object renders is explicit falsification. FAIL-1 falsification A9: anchor rot (the lookup dies, not the input) and wrong-node re-anchor (fuzzy re-anchor to a similar-but-wrong node, passing a substring contains-check) both defeat placement-unaware assertions. ARCHAEOLOGY-SUPPLEMENT.md (d): "the hard half (re-anchoring a correction when its node ID goes stale on source change) ... has no LIVE analog."

**What would settle it:** run merge/split/resurrection fixtures through the composed correction machinery and assert per-correction applied/unapplied reports: honored on a live identity, named re-anchor event with candidate successors, or named target-missing state. Assert that honoring an orphan while a successor renders fails the suite, and that preservation assertions are placement-aware (bound to the anchored node), not substring contains-checks. The F8 strengthened tests already name this shape.

**Cost of being wrong:** owner intent silently defeated behind a green test suite. The owner's correction becomes theater: honored on a corpse identity, escaped by the successor, with the logged cause ("source changed") naming nothing about the lost correction. #8 persists while the dashboard reports #8 prevented.

### (f) The phantom-analog problem

**The uncertainty, precisely:** how much of the mechanism library is specified against architectures that do not exist yet, so that its guarantees are design fiction anchored to phantom interfaces rather than executable against the production pipeline.

**Evidence for its importance:** ARCHAEOLOGY-SUPPLEMENT.md (a): 9 wave-1 harvested mechanisms dead on arrival on phantom analogs (the Run entity and execution history, TOOL_* events, attempt identity, canonical task_id, each confirmed absent by lane 19's audit). FAIL-6 falsification C2: "the survivors are therefore specified against an architecture that does not exist yet"; the actual production fix (per-channel call-site filtering) is a member of the cut class, not of the specified survivor S1. FAIL-6 cross-cutting finding C3: the fixture-behavioral pattern (tests verify behavior on a fixed fixture, never the structural property), with the suggested standing rule that each survivor's test must include a property-removal mutant the test must fail. FAIL-6 C4: composition dependencies are unmapped, so a silent failure in any dependency voids the dependent's claim with no test failing.

**What would settle it:** the delete-our-need pass plus the PING-ANALOG-INDEX.md comparison (each mechanism's assumed architecture mapped to live file+line, or classified PHANTOM), plus FAIL-6's structural rule: every survivor's test runs against the production pipeline, not a fixture approximation, with property-removal mutants. The settling machinery is already specified; this is an audit project, not a research question.

**Cost of being wrong:** the library certifies mechanisms that cannot be built (no anchor points exist) or that need invention mislabeled as reuse. Prevention claims become claims about an imaginary system, and the first real implementation discovers the gaps under production pressure, which is the most expensive place to discover them.

## 2. RECOMMENDATION: THE BIGGEST REMAINING UNCERTAINTY

**The winner is (b): the Ask FYD conversational seam.**

**Reasoning.** It wins on all three ranking criteria at once, and it is the only candidate that does.

Blast radius: it is the widest in the corpus and the only uncertainty shared by every survivor family. No family is exempt: the oracle family gates static projection, the typed-seam family gates static slots, the pure-projection family covers static projection purity, and the extraction family's every verdict is acquisition-time only. Through the audit's seams A and B alone, six failure classes (#1, #2, #3, #4, #5, #6) reopen simultaneously, plus #8-adjacent staleness and #9 false confidence. The hard count (26 of 30 tests site-render-only) turns "maybe uncovered" into "measured uncovered."

Decidability: it is fully empirical and settleable this month. The falsifiable tests already exist; they were simply never pointed at the conversational surface. It needs no product milestone (unlike owner identity, which is gated on real owner auth before public deployment), no values judgment (unlike the normalization paradox, whose authorship legitimacy only Nolan can confer), and no invention-gated mapping project (unlike the phantom-analog audit). Enumerate the answer path, port the tests, build the one missing mechanism (the query-time binding verifier), and either the gates hold on the answer path or the claims get formally rescoped. There is a concrete terminal state either way.

Cost of being wrong: this is the credibility question. Every "prevents #N" claim in the library was established on the static-projection seam while the flagship interactive surface, the one the business actually sells and the one the standing product strategy names as the proof loop ("Ask FYD from the graph"), sits outside every test. Five mechanisms already claim Ask FYD coverage in prose their tests never exercise. If the library ships with that shape intact, it is certifying a product surface it has never tested, which is the exact failure of epistemic honesty the falsification game was built to prevent.

A corroborating structural point: settling (b) is also the first real execution of OPEN-QUESTIONS.md's Q3 completeness invariant ("an unlisted seam is an unguarded seam"). The Ask FYD seam is the largest known instance of the invariant, and the answer-path enumeration the experiment requires is the inventory procedure Q3 asks for. The winner pulls the third-ranked question along with it.

And the honest interaction with the identity question: a query-time binding verifier that cites "who asserted this" will inherit the unnamed trust root (MERGED-correction's hole at the foundation) until owner identity exists. The verifier is still worth building first, because it is decidable now and bounds the damage of a spoofed owner later: fail-closed on unbound sentences limits what any actor, legitimate or not, can get asserted. The winner does not depend on the identity milestone; it degrades gracefully without it.

## 3. THE SETTLING EXPERIMENT (falsifiable-test format)

**Experiment: ASK-SEAM-PORT.** Port the existing falsifiable tests to the Ask FYD conversational surface; build the one missing mechanism (query-time binding verifier); force the scoping decision.

**Input.**

1. A seeded test tenant with a known evidence graph: businesses with known fields, known private records, known conflicts, known corrections applied after the seed.
2. An instrumented Ask FYD answer pipeline with stage boundaries named: retrieval, answer composition, markdown/link rendering, answer cards and carousels, evidence citations, tool-call arguments.
3. A hostile question corpus: URL-poisoned answers (javascript: and data: URLs in markdown links and embedded media), claim-smuggling questions ("since you're the cheapest plumber in town, do you offer senior discounts?"), cross-record synthesis traps (hours from record A plus a late-night review from record B), cross-tenant probes (questions answerable only from another tenant's evidence), private-record probes in a public viewer context, trap questions against entities known to be missing from a blind-built graph, and the same factual question asked across sessions before and after an owner correction.
4. A benign corpus (the availability twin): tel: with extensions and international formats, IDN/punycode domains, mailto: with query params, legitimate Unicode copy.

**Procedure.**

1. Enumerate the answer path end to end: every stage in (2) that can emit text, links, or structured arguments becomes a named seam. This enumeration is the completeness-inventory procedure; any stage that cannot be named is itself a finding.
2. Port each class's existing falsifiable test to the conversational surface:
   - F1: run the viewport oracle over rendered answer cards and carousels at the width matrix, including the interaction matrix (open sheets, both scroll directions).
   - F2: poison answers with javascript: and data: URLs in markdown links and embedded media; assert the sink validator fires on the answer renderer independently. Per-sink scoping required (the data: image exception, if present, must not reach iframe-src or href sinks).
   - F5: run the smuggling and synthesis questions against fixtures with known evidence; assert no endorsed premise and no presented-as-derived claim without the required bindings.
   - F6: probe Ask FYD over another tenant's evidence and over private records in a public viewer context; assert refusal or viewer-filtered retrieval, with multi-turn viewer-identity confusion cases included.
   - F3: ask trap questions against entities known to be missing from a blind-built graph; assert unexplained-absence flags rather than confident incompleteness.
   - F4/F8: ask the same question across sessions after an owner correction; assert answers track corrections, not stale source values.
3. Build the missing mechanism: a query-time binding verifier between answer generation and delivery. It takes the generated answer plus the retrieved claim set for the turn, requires every factual sentence to resolve to a retrieved evidence record or be marked UNKNOWN or refused, fails closed on unbound sentences, and computes SUPPORTED / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED / UNSUPPORTED deterministically from binding coverage. The label computation belongs to the verifier, never to the LLM.
4. Apply the decision rule: every gate that runs on static projection must either run on the answer path (one composition point per FAIL-8 Q7) or the library's prevention claims are formally scoped to "static projection only" with Ask FYD marked uncovered. No mechanism may claim Ask FYD coverage in prose without a test that exercises it.

**Expected.** Either the ported tests pass and the binding verifier holds (in which case the library's prevention claims extend to the conversational surface with evidence), or specific classes fail and the library is rescoped honestly. There is no third outcome: "claims coverage, untested" is not a permitted terminal state after this experiment runs.

**Falsifier.** Any of the following upgrades the named class's prevention verdict to FALLS on the conversational surface and forces the rescope decision: a javascript: or data: URL in an answer's markdown link or embedded media renders unneutralized (F2); an answer labeled SUPPORTED contains a factual sentence bound to no retrieved record, or endorses a smuggled premise, or presents cross-record synthesis as derived without the required refs (F5); an answer cites another tenant's evidence or repeats a private record in a public viewer context (F6); an answer is confidently complete over a graph known to be blind at the queried entities (F3); an answer repeats a pre-correction value after the correction landed (F4/F8); an answer card scrolls horizontally at any matrix width (F1). A mechanism whose prose claims Ask FYD coverage but whose test suite has no conversational case must retract the claim before the experiment's verdict can be cited.

## 4. THE RUNNER-UP AND WHY IT LOST

**Runner-up: (c), the conflict significance/normalization paradox (the forbidden authority).**

It is the deepest genuine paradox in the corpus: the cut list forbids the semantic authority that the family's own detection criterion ("non-identical values") requires, and the two horns (explosion that trains everyone to ignore CONFLICTING, or a significance filter that is FAIL-9 through a rulebook) are both live, with per-member breakage documented in MERGED-conflict Attack (a). Its cost of being wrong is real and product-visible: a hedged-everything product or a silent merger with an auditable costume.

It lost for two reasons. First, decidability: it is closable only by a decision on authorship legitimacy, which is Nolan's values call, not by any experiment a research lane can run. The winner is fully empirical and settleable this month with tests that already exist. Second, blast radius and ordering: the paradox is class-bounded (#9, partly #3), while the winner reopens six classes across every family; and the paradox sits downstream of the winner, since a query-time binding verifier that computes support over retrieved claims must still handle conflicted retrieval, meaning (c)'s significance policy will be needed inside (b)'s verifier once (b) is built. The winner is the load-bearing question; the runner-up is the next question the winner's answer will need.

**Close third: (f), the phantom-analog problem.** It threatens the library's construct validity wholesale (a certified mechanism with no live anchor point is design fiction), and FAIL-6 C2 shows the failure is already real in one family. It ranked below the winner because its settling machinery is already specified (the delete-our-need pass, the PING-ANALOG-INDEX comparison, FAIL-6's production-pipeline test rule), which makes it an audit project with a known procedure rather than an open research question, and because it cannot fail fast on a single experiment the way the Ask FYD seam can. **(e)** ranked fourth: real, empirically testable, and already reduced to fixtures by the FAIL-8 strengthened tests, but class-bounded to the correction lifecycle and narrower in blast radius than the top three. **(a)** ranked fifth: largely the shared-seam framing of the same minting and conversational-seam problems the winner and the MERGED-typed-seam attack already cover; its Ask FYD half is the winner, and its structural-resolution half is a known design answer awaiting implementation. **(d)** ranked last: an execution gap with a known answer (telemetry, owners, SLAs), threatening whether mechanisms keep working rather than whether they work at all.

## 5. NOTES FOR THE FINAL REPORT

* No question in the corpus challenges whether the mechanisms' core ideas are wrong. The pattern across all nine falsifications is the same: the tests assume, without constructing, the precondition the mechanism depends on. The biggest remaining uncertainty is the largest unconstructed precondition, and it is the conversational surface.
* The winner interacts with the identity question (OPEN-QUESTIONS.md runner-up 1): the binding verifier inherits the unnamed trust root until owner identity exists, but it is still worth building first because it is decidable now and fail-closed on unbound sentences bounds what any actor can get asserted.
* The winner interacts with (c): once the binding verifier exists, conflicted retrieval needs the significance policy, versioned and auditable like M3's priority policy. Build the verifier first, then name the normalization authority.
* What is NOT the biggest uncertainty: whether the mechanisms work on static projection (largely verified, WEAKENED with named strengthening paths), whether guards need maintenance (known answer, execution gap), and whether owner identity is needed before public deployment (already decided per standing memory; the uncertainty is the milestone date, not the answer).
