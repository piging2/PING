## Missing links for the product chain

Chain: GENERATED EXPERIENCE -> ASK FYD -> EVIDENCE -> OWNER CORRECTION -> CONVERSATIONAL CUSTOMIZATION -> REGENERATION

1. **Ask -> Owner-correction loop breaks at the UI.** `composeAskFyd` drafts digest-bound `site_patch` proposals and `answerAskFyd` surfaces them in the API 200 body (`proposal`, `sitePatchCard`), but the served visitor widget (`src/fyd/components/ask-fyd-widget.tsx`) has ZERO proposal/patch rendering or approval (grep: no matches for "proposal"/"patch"). For anonymous viewers proposals are null by design (no grants), but there is no owner ask surface at all. Result: a visitor or owner who asks "change X" gets an answer, never an actionable, approvable change. The data path exists; the last meter of UI does not.
2. **Two Ask context compilers, one served.** `src/fyd/ask/context-compiler.ts` v2 (`compileAskContext`, G1-G12 hardened: byte budgets, exclusion ledger, relevance gate, epistemic rank enforcement, secret stripping, capability filtering) is NOT in the served Ask path (zero references from ask-pipeline.ts). The served route uses the older `buildAskFydContext` (context-builder.ts). The v2 compiler's only consumer is the decision-fabric MC-dispatch branch. Converge on one; the v2 compiler is the stronger machinery and the served route is the stronger integration.
3. **Conversational customization covers presentation + visibility only.** The Lane D vocabulary (9 kinds) handles reorder/feature/hide/show; design-system directives are refused by frozen law; MEDIA directives are absent entirely (zero matches in owner-command-compiler.ts); truth correction lives in a separate fact_correction proposal path, not in the conversational flow. An owner cannot conversationally say "use this photo", "change the headline font", or "that's wrong, the hours are X" through one vocabulary. Three separate doors where the product needs one.
4. **Concierge unwired.** `src/fyd/ask/concierge.ts` (deterministic, no-LLM, 7 canonical questions, cleanest grade vocabulary SUPPORTED/DERIVED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN, pure function) is referenced only by invariant tests, never by the served pipeline. It is the strongest honest-answer module in the tree and the served route does not use it.
5. **DEHPP chrome-split not on HEAD.** The customer/demo separation that removes harness artifacts from the public page (fyd-dehpp) sits on a branch; HEAD still serves the pre-split route shape. Nolan's standing screenshot complaint maps exactly to this gap.
