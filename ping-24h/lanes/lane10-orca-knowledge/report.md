## 3. INTEGRITY FINDINGS

**Tag preservation across the boundary.** The compiler preserves: OWNER_ASSERTED (`USER_OVERRIDE`), DIRECTLY_OBSERVED (`DIRECT_FACT`), `website_statement`, `DEMO_SYNTHETIC`, `relationship_fact`, `owner_authorship`, `GENERATED_COPY`, `INFERENCE`, and `UNKNOWN`. It cannot preserve `CONFLICTED` or `STALE`: neither classification exists in `classificationFromProvenance` and neither has an input. Note the reached-loop report's proposed patch test #1 names "KNOWN/UNKNOWN/CONFLICTED/STALE/INFERRED/OWNER-ASSERTED/DIRECTLY OBSERVED tags": against the actual compiler, CONFLICTED and STALE are unrepresentable, and KNOWN is not a distinct tag (the positive vocabulary is the classification strings). The attack-9 regression as specified ("retract an overlay op, recompile, assert the claim surfaces STALE/UNKNOWN") will surface UNKNOWN, not STALE; the patch lane should align the test with the compiler's real vocabulary or extend it.

**Every material assertion retains a route to evidence?** In the packet, yes by construction: per-object `evidenceRefIds`, field-level `field:` ids, `rel:` ids, a registry with per-item classification, and digests binding graph, siteSpec, searchIndex, and packet. The chain breaks in four places: (1) the compiler is unwired, so no material assertion flows through it today; (2) `verifyAnswerClaims`, the registry-membership plus B3 epistemic-rank enforcer, has zero production callers; (3) the dispatch executor receives the raw prompt, so post-patch the packet binds the artifact and work order but not necessarily the executor's reasoning (prompt routing is a follow-on decision for the patch lane); (4) conflict and staleness assertions cannot be represented at all.

**Three vocabularies, no canonical mapping.** The compiler's evidence classification (`USER_OVERRIDE`/`DIRECT_FACT`/`website_statement`/...) vs the Ask serving path's FactClass vocabulary (`DIRECT_FACT`/`DERIVED_FACT`/`INFERENCE`/`GENERATED_COPY`/`USER_OVERRIDE`) vs the MC projection vocabulary (`PROVEN`/`OBSERVED`/`DERIVED`/`UNKNOWN`/`STALE`/`CONFLICTING`/`PROTOTYPE`/`UNWIRED`) are three separate schemes with three owners and no mapping between them. Worse, `classificationFromProvenance` exists twice with different mappings: `context-compiler.ts:180` maps `directory` to DIRECT_FACT and owner kinds to USER_OVERRIDE; `ask-composer.ts` maps `owner` to `owner_authorship` and has no `directory` case. Two implementations of the same capability (provenance to epistemic class) with no single owner. Per the constitutional rule of one, these should converge; the compiler's version is the stronger candidate (G6-extended, handles more kinds).

**No second truth store needed.** The investigation confirms the mission constraint is satisfiable without one: the governed read seam (`PingObjectReader` → `fyd-tenant-graph.ts`) already composes the live tenant graph from fixture plus journal overlays with digest verification at both layers, and the packet compiler is a pure function over it. Models consume compiled evidence; nothing here requires a new retrieval layer.

## 5. SMALLEST GAP (one item)

**Owner:** FYD Ask lane (owns both files involved).

**Gap:** `compileAskContext()` cannot express CONFLICTED and takes no conflict declarations as input, even though the complete, locked conflict model already exists in the same lane.

**Change:** add optional `conflicts?: AskFieldConflict[]` to `CompilerInput`; in the evidence registry, mint conflict observation refs in the exact format the serving path already parses (`<objectId>#<field>#conflict-<index>`, `field-conflicts.ts:104`), classified CONFLICTED; suppress the disputed field value in the packet object, matching the locked FYD-Q1 projection policy ("Contact information is being verified." copy stays on the serving path). No new store, no new graph, no journal schema change, no new owner.

**Why this one:** of the five ABSENT questions, four (happening, work-running, effects, outcomes) need new event inputs, which is a larger design decision about what a dispatch-time packet should carry. Conflicts are the single ABSENT with a complete, locked, same-lane donor implementation already shaping production copy, with a ref format the serving path already parses. It is the smallest delta with the largest integrity payoff: it closes "what conflicts?" from ABSENT to REACHED for the packet and repairs the vocabulary hole where the compiler cannot carry the epistemic state the Ask path treats as most dangerous. Natural follow-on (not the smallest gap): converge the two `classificationFromProvenance` implementations under one owner.
