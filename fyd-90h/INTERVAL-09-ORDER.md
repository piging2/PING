Nolan's notes attached to the order:

- "No-signal outcome with the window honestly left open" is better than
  manufacturing a success metric. That epistemic discipline is exactly what
  we want to protect.
- Once enough loops are observable, ORCA becomes progressively less dependent
  on model judgment: the model discovers, evidence accumulates, software
  crystallizes the stable portions, the model remains for genuinely novel or
  ambiguous decisions.
- The evidence changes the priority: do not let Interval 08 security cleanup,
  Cloudflare, R2, portability, or the public-social research pull FYD back
  into infrastructure expansion. Cloudflare is net-new, object storage is
  net-new, placement reconciliation is net-new, and there are three competing
  event shapes. Inventory and fence; don't let convergence eat the product.
- The earlier "FYD — FOCUS AFTER INTERVAL 08" directive is superseded where it
  conflicts with this order (notably: public-history purge was HOLD there, is
  AUTHORIZED CONDITIONALLY here).
