LICENSE: MIT (Microsoft Corporation) | CLASS: ADAPT
--- PROBLEM ---
A coordinator that re-resolves routing at every step can dispatch the same work twice, route retries to a changed runtime, or lose the link between what was authorized and what actually ran. Recovery cannot distinguish the original execution context from a later one.
--- MECHANISM ---
AgentExecutionBinding is a write-once record created before dispatch and authoritative for routing, recovery, cancellation, settlement, and finalization. ID: orka-outcome-unknown-fail-closed
SOURCE: orka-agents/orka @ cb73ac8019117c4e86d04fcfd3237ac65f56662a
LICENSE: MIT (Microsoft Corporation) | CLASS: ADAPT
--- PROBLEM ---
When a worker or coordinator fails at or after a side-effect boundary (a prompt was possibly accepted by the runtime), retrying risks double execution, while declaring failure risks hiding real work that happened. Most frameworks collapse this ambiguity into either silent retry or empty success.
--- MECHANISM ---
The execution state machine distinguishes pre-submission failures (safe to requeue: Pending, Reserved, Submitting) from post-boundary failures, which settle to a terminal OutcomeUnknown that must never be treated as generic retryable failure and must be a unique durable value, never an empty or omitted string. Recovery never replays a possibly-accepted prompt: source message is 'outcome is unknown and was not replayed'. Observed at commit cb73ac8019117c4e86d04fcfd3237ac65f56662a in api/v1alpha1/task_runtime_types.go, lines 66 and 85, internal/controller/acp_recovery.go, line 101.
--- INVARIANT ---
Ambiguity is a terminal, explicitly named state, never an absence of state. A prompt that may have been accepted is never re-submitted by recovery.
--- FAILURE_MODEL ---
Pre-boundary failures are recoverable by resume. Post-boundary failures are terminal and honest: the record says the outcome is unknown and says it was not replayed, so no silent duplicate work and no false success.
--- SECURITY_MODEL ---
The no-replay rule prevents a crash-looping coordinator from being weaponized into repeated side effects (repeated tool calls, repeated publishes).
--- SCALING_MODEL ---
No retry amplification at the boundary. Recovery work is a bounded classification pass over stale attempts at leadership change, not unbounded re-execution.
--- CRX_ANALOG ---
Give CRX run attempts an explicit terminal OutcomeUnknown with a unique durable marker. This is exactly CRX's typed-failure rule: never silently convert failure or ambiguity into empty success.
--- SEMANTIC_DELTA ---
CRX already types failures; the delta is making ambiguity its own terminal value with a non-empty marker, and enforcing that recovery never replays across a side-effect boundary it cannot prove uncrossed.

ID: orka-single-control-authority
SOURCE: orka-agents/orka @ cb73ac8019117c4e86d04fcfd3237ac65f56662a
LICENSE: MIT (Microsoft Corporation) | CLASS: ADAPT
--- PROBLEM ---
Adding a second store for control-adjacent data (outbox tables, session databases, payload stores) silently creates a second control authority: the two stores disagree after partial failure, and recovery must reconcile competing truths.
--- MECHANISM ---
Outbox and payload stores are explicitly committed behind the primary control fences: SQLite transactions carry the Kubernetes controller epoch and session fences, and the architecture documents that the payload store is never a second control authority. Execution snapshots are AES-256-GCM encrypted with a controller-managed 32-byte key; losing the key fails startup closed rather than minting a new one and orphaning snapshots. Observed at commit cb73ac8019117c4e86d04fcfd3237ac65f56662a in internal/store/task_data_transaction.go, cmd/agent_execution_snapshot_secret.go, website/docs/concepts/architecture.md.
--- INVARIANT ---
There is exactly one control authority. Every auxiliary store commits under its fences or it does not commit.
--- FAILURE_MODEL ---
A failed auxiliary write fails the fenced transaction; there is no state where the auxiliary store disagrees with the authority, because it cannot commit alone.
--- SECURITY_MODEL ---
Encrypted snapshots with fail-closed key management mean a lost key is a loud startup failure, not silent data orphaning or re-encryption under a new key.
--- SCALING_MODEL ---
Auxiliary stores scale as read models of the authority; they never need independent consensus.
--- CRX_ANALOG ---
Cite as the standing guardrail against second-universe proposals: adapt new state into CRX's existing event journal under its fences; never bolt on a parallel durable store that can disagree with it.
--- SEMANTIC_DELTA ---
Names the anti-pattern explicitly and shows the alternative: auxiliary stores as fenced projections of one authority.
