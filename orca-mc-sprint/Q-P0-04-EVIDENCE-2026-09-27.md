## Design note (for follow-up, not a P0 FAIL)
The journal gateway's POST /events does not verify the caller's journal identity;
the WRONG_JOURNAL check is client-side only (server.ts, dump.py). A direct POST
to the gateway bypasses the marker check. This is by design ("identity comes from
the journal's own assertion"), but means the gateway must not be exposed to
untrusted clients. Flagged for gateway hardening review, not a Q-P0-04 failure.
