## THE ACCEPTANCE GATE (all must genuinely pass before this era activates)

1. FYD understands a real business.
2. An owner correction propagates and survives regeneration.
3. ORCA generates legitimate work autonomously.
4. Mission Control truthfully reconstructs missions.
5. HOLD/UNKNOWN is correct.
6. Tenant boundaries hold.
7. Restart/replay work.
8. The website is genuinely good.
9. The owner can safely act from that understanding.

At that point, stop treating the next frontier as "finish the system." The system
has crossed a threshold. The next frontier is: TURN THE WORKING MACHINE INTO A
LEARNING BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM. The first 100 hours prove correctness and
autonomy. The next era proves usefulness, generality, economics, compounding
learning, and scale without loss of control.

Permanent cycle from here: REAL BUSINESS -> UNDERSTAND -> CORRECT -> OPERATE ->
OBSERVE OUTCOME -> LEARN -> GENERALIZE -> REDUCE HUMAN WORK -> NEXT BUSINESS ->
REPEAT.

## POST-CONVERGENCE LAW (the directive after the current file)

THE SYSTEM NOW HAS TO EARN ITS EXISTENCE IN THE REAL WORLD. Do not respond to
successful infrastructure by inventing more infrastructure. Freeze architectural
expansion unless observed customer reality forces it. Move the swarm from
machinery construction to business understanding, owner correction, continuous
reconciliation, customer action, procedure discovery, outcome measurement and
generalized learning. Introduce businesses as adversarial worlds, not templates.
Every owner correction is high-value evidence. Every unsupported answer is a
product defect. Every repeated manual procedure is an automation candidate, not
automatic permission to automate. Every external effect remains
capability-governed. Every learned rule must preserve tenant isolation,
provenance and purpose. Every autonomy expansion is earned per capability from
measured outcomes, never inherited from an agent or model. Mission Control
compresses reality and human attention. ORCA discovers and coordinates the next
legitimate improvement. Jev measures where intelligence is trustworthy. TenantOS
disappears beneath correct isolation. Hermes executes. FYD remains what the
customer experiences. PING remains what makes the experience trustworthy. Measure
correction burden, maintenance eliminated, time-to-trust, customer outcomes,
support burden, unit economics and human interventions — not agent activity. Grow
from two adversarial businesses to ten, then to fifty only when generic machinery
survives. Let real usage attack every assumption. Convert recurring failures into
regression knowledge. Convert repeated correct model judgment into cheaper
deterministic machinery where appropriate. Convert repeated owner behavior into
proposed procedures. Convert verified procedures into narrowly authorized
capabilities. Convert outcomes into identity-minimized system learning. Never let
learning silently become authority. Never let scale silently become
customer-specific branching. Never let revenue pressure weaken evidence, privacy
or owner control. The post-100 objective is no longer to prove that PING can run
itself. It is to prove that a trustworthy autonomous system can understand a
business, remain current as reality changes, reduce the owner's work, safely
operate bounded parts of that business, learn from the outcomes, and make the
next business substantially easier without requiring Nolan to invent the next
task.

## 1. Architectural scarcity rule (once gate passes)

For the next phase: NO NEW ORCHESTRATION PRIMITIVE WITHOUT A NEW REAL-BUSINESS
FAILURE. NO NEW AUTHORITY WITHOUT A NEW AUTHORITY REQUIREMENT. NO NEW EVENT TYPE
JUST BECAUSE IT IS CONVENIENT. NO NEW TENANTOS SUBSYSTEM. NO NEW MISSION CONTROL
FEATURE WITHOUT AN OPERATOR QUESTION IT ANSWERS. NO NEW JEV DECISION CLASS
WITHOUT A CONSUMER AND AN OUTCOME LABEL. NO NEW FYD ABSTRACTION WITHOUT TWO OR
MORE businesses demonstrating the need. ORCA's existing loop (observe a
discrepancy, gather evidence, generate a bounded mission, execute, falsify,
verify, close, select the next frontier) already provides the intended autonomous
machinery. Force it to earn its keep. Center of gravity moves:
ENGINEERING -> PRODUCT -> CUSTOMERS -> OUTCOMES -> LEARNING.

## 2. The next milestone: 10 real businesses, not 10,000 agents

From 2 adversarial fixtures to 10 genuinely different business worlds — not ten
cosmetically different plumbing companies. Adversaries that break different
assumptions: (1) home-service with emergencies, (2) appointment-based
professional service, (3) restaurant/hospitality, (4) multiple locations,
(5) person-centric consultancy, (6) project/portfolio business, (7) sparse web
presence, (8) contradictory/stale web presence, (9) substantial media/gallery
content, (10) complicated services/products/people relationships. Objective:
discover which assumptions we thought were universal were actually Happy Place
assumptions. Every new business is an adversarial world. No customer-specific
branches.

## 3. The BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING EXAM

For every business, before generating anything, FYD takes an exam. An independent
falsifier gets the source corpus and asks: What does this business do? What
doesn't it do? Where does it operate? Who is associated with it? Which facts
conflict? Which are stale? Which claims are strongly/weakly supported? What would
be dangerous to claim? What services matter most? What relationships exist? What
is unknown? What changed recently? Compare FYD's canonical business
representation against evidence. This produces a Business Understanding Error
Corpus. Classify failures by root cause: ACQUISITION LOSS, PARSING LOSS,
OBSERVATION LOSS, ENTITY/OBJECT LOSS, RELATIONSHIP LOSS, RESOLUTION LOSS,
EVIDENCE LOSS, TEMPORAL LOSS, CONFLICT LOSS, OWNER-INTENT LOSS, PROJECTION LOSS,
ANSWERING LOSS. This gives ORCA a permanent source of legitimate product work.

## 4. FYD stops being principally a website generator

Critical product transition. The website becomes one projection of THE BUSINESS
MODEL FYD MAINTAINS. From the same authorized business understanding: Website,
Ask FYD, Owner workspace, Business profile, Service catalog, Contact/lead
experience, FAQs, structured metadata, social/profile information,
proposal/estimate context, customer conversation context, internal operating
context — all derive from the same state. But do not integrate every platform
immediately. First prove: ONE OWNER CORRECTION CAUSES EVERY CURRENTLY AUTHORIZED
PROJECTION TO BECOME CONSISTENT WITHOUT BESPOKE EDITING. That is the moat.

## 5. The CONTINUOUS BUSINESS RECONCILIATION LOOP

The original system learns a business once. The next system keeps knowing it.
For each authorized source maintain: LAST OBSERVED, CURRENT OBSERVATION, CHANGE,
MATERIALITY, CONFIDENCE/EVIDENCE, CONFLICT, OWNER ASSERTION, PROJECTION IMPACT,
ACTION REQUIRED. Example: business website changes hours -> FYD observes ->
compares against owner state -> if unambiguous and policy permits: knowledge
updates -> affected projections regenerate -> Ask changes -> owner sees concise
change receipt. If conflicting: CONFLICT -> HOLD KNOWLEDGE PROMOTION ->
owner/evidence reconciliation. If consequential: proposal -> approval. This is
where FYD starts feeling alive.

## 6. TEMPORAL TRUTH as a serious system property

The next hard problem is not "what is true?" but: what was true when, according
to whom, observed when, superseded by what, and what should currently be
projected? Every meaningful assertion needs: VALID FROM, VALID UNTIL, OBSERVED
AT, RECORDED AT, SUPERSEDED AT (where applicable). Then FYD can answer: "You used
to advertise 24-hour emergency service, but your current site no longer does.
Your owner instruction still says emergency calls are accepted. Which should
customers see?" Vastly more valuable than scraping another page.

## 7. OWNER CORRECTION is the highest-value training signal

An owner correction tells FYD what evidence it misunderstood, which source was
wrong, which distinction mattered, which object was misresolved, which
presentation was inappropriate, which question it should have asked, or which
owner preference dominates. Do not merely persist the corrected value. Persist
the reason structure when safely available: BEFORE -> OWNER CORRECTION ->
EVIDENCE CONTEXT -> WHY PREVIOUS STATE LOST -> AFFECTED PROJECTIONS -> OUTCOME.
Learn from the class of correction WITHOUT turning identifiable customer
information into shared training data. This is the outcome-learning direction
PING has been aiming at.

## 8. ORCA graduates from defect coordinator to improvement coordinator

Post-100 ORCA searches four categories: CORRECTNESS GAP (false/broken/unsafe),
CUSTOMER FRICTION (unnecessary human effort), PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY (evidence
suggests a generic improvement), ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY (evidence suggests
measurable business value). But ORCA must never turn "opportunity" directly into
mutation. It becomes: OBSERVATION -> HYPOTHESIS -> REVERSIBLE EXPERIMENT ->
OUTCOME -> KEEP/REVERT/LEARN. ORCA becomes an autonomous product-development
coordinator.

## 9. Mission Control becomes the business/system flight recorder

Do not turn MC into Jira. Add only views answering consequential questions. The
transition is from "what is the computer doing?" toward "what is FYD doing for
this business?" MC should eventually answer: WHAT CHANGED IN THE BUSINESS? WHAT
DID FYD LEARN? WHAT DID FYD CHANGE? WHY? WHAT IS CONFLICTED? WHAT IS STALE? WHAT
IS WAITING FOR ME? WHAT CUSTOMER ACTIVITY MATTERED? WHAT DID FYD DO
AUTONOMOUSLY? WHAT VALUE DID THAT CREATE? WHAT DID IT COST? WHAT WENT WRONG?
WHAT IS ORCA IMPROVING NOW? The existing acceptance requirement (explain
authority, capability, external-effect uncertainty, evidence, verification, replay
without logs or SQL) remains underneath.

## 10. Jev: the Decision Reliability Map

Stop asking "does Jev work?" Build per decision class: N, ACCURACY, CALIBRATION,
HIGH-CONFIDENCE ERROR RATE, ABSTENTION RATE, COST, LATENCY, DISAGREEMENT WITH
FALSIFIER, OUTCOME REGRET, CONSEQUENCE CLASS, CURRENT AUTOMATION LEVEL. Autonomy
expands per decision class, never globally. TASK_CLASS may become automatic
quickly; OWNER_INTERRUPT_REQUIRED may stay advisory; FACT_CONFLICT_RESOLUTION
may remain shadow; PUBLICATION_APPROVAL may remain completely outside Jev
authority. The system earns autonomy locally. It never receives "AI autonomy
level 4."

## 11. The AUTONOMY LADDER (per capability, not per agent)

A0 OBSERVE (can see). A1 RECOMMEND (can propose). A2 PREPARE (can prepare exact
reversible work). A3 EXECUTE REVERSIBLE LOW-RISK (policy-authorized). A4 EXECUTE
+ VERIFY + RECOVER (no human involvement in ordinary operation). A5 CONTINUOUSLY
OPTIMIZE WITHIN BOUNDS (may generate and run experiments inside policy). The
ladder belongs to capabilities, not agents — an agent never becomes "A5." This
prevents trust earned doing one thing from silently transferring to everything
else.

## 12. The OUTCOME GRAPH

Connect: BUSINESS STATE -> OPPORTUNITY/PROBLEM -> MISSION -> ACTION -> EXTERNAL
EFFECT -> OBSERVATION -> CUSTOMER RESPONSE -> BUSINESS OUTCOME -> LEARNING. But
preserve uncertainty: the outcome graph represents OBSERVED ASSOCIATION without
inventing CAUSED BY.

## 13. Marketing becomes BUSINESS GROWTH EXPERIMENTATION

No "social media bot." Every experiment begins with a business hypothesis, then:
hypothesis -> audience -> authorized content -> channel -> action identity ->
receipt -> engagement -> visit -> conversation -> qualified opportunity ->
customer/outcome where observable. Attribution remains UNKNOWN when it cannot be
established. Posting-frequency optimization is permanently rejected as a goal.

## 14. The first real owner

Once the gates are actually green: a real owner, no architecture demo. "FYD has
already learned your business. Tell me what's wrong." Then watch without
rescuing: what they click, correct, what surprises/frightens them, what they
ignore, what terminology fails, what they want changed, what they ask FYD, what
they assume it can do, what they won't trust it to do, what they'd pay for, what
would make them stop. Every hesitation is potential product evidence.

## 15-17. The product KPIs

CORRECTION BURDEN: initial material errors/business, unnecessary questions,
corrections required, repeat corrections of same semantic class, corrections that
fail to propagate, corrections lost after regeneration, false conflict prompts,
correct autonomous reconciliations, time from URL to owner-ready understanding.
Mature FYD requires fewer corrections because evidence processing improves, not
because it guesses more. BUSINESS MAINTENANCE ELIMINATED: repetitive digital
maintenance that disappeared (hours updated once, service updates propagate,
stale claims detected, contact changes propagate, lead context arrives
structured). TIME TO TRUST: URL -> "yes, that's my business"; first correction ->
seen everywhere; first autonomous change -> accepted; first customer interaction
handled correctly; first action completed without owner intervention.

## 18-21. BUILD -> UNDERSTAND -> MAINTAIN -> OPERATE

Advance one stage at a time; earn OPERATE procedure by procedure. First operating
procedures are boring and verifiable: qualify an inquiry, prepare an estimate
request, collect missing project information, answer evidence-backed service
questions, route an inquiry, prepare appointment options, detect stale business
information, prepare a site update, organize a new project/photo, prepare
follow-up, summarize customer context. PROCEDURE MINER: ORCA observes repetitive
owner behavior and produces a PROCEDURE CANDIDATE (observed repetitions, inputs,
decision points, required evidence, external effects, capabilities, failure
modes, verification, owner authority, estimated value); owner/policy authorizes a
bounded version. Never automate immediately. This creates the second flywheel:
business activity -> observed procedure -> bounded automation -> outcome ->
improved procedure. UNDERSTANDING + OPERATION.

## 22. Keep PING underneath the product

Never expose Event Authority, canonical hashing, Jev, ORCA, TenantOS, worktrees,
replay internals, capability machinery to normal owners. FYD says "I found
conflicting hours," not "Observation O-817 conflicts with canonical assertion
A-52." The machinery exists so the user doesn't have to understand it.

## 23-25. 10 businesses, then 50, then population learning

50 businesses test distribution, onboarding, acquisition quality, long-tail
weirdness, support burden, cost, latency, privacy, diversity, correction burden —
not hand-polished sites; manual rescue at 50 is a defect signal. Learn from
failures ACROSS businesses WITHOUT sharing identities: identity-minimized,
tenant-safe, provenance-aware, purpose-bound, reversible/versioned outcome
learning. Example: footer hours conflicting with owner-authoritative structured
hours should not win merely because observed later — teach the generalized rule,
not "Business A closes at six." ORCA eventually observes repeated correction
classes across businesses, reproduces, patches generic machinery, falsifies
against historical cases, runs tenant-local tests, rolls forward, measures
whether the class declines.

## 26-28. Regression corpus, counterfactual replay, Jev as policy instrument

Every embarrassment becomes permanent adversarial material (false-green object
UI, wrong-journal path, tenant spoof, secret leak, HOLD redispatch, bad mobile
crop, unsupported Ask answer, lost owner correction, stale projection, wrong
business identity, duplicate external action, misleading MC completion,
unreachable runtime, generic ugly site, wrong media role, bad owner escalation).
NEVER PAY FULL PRICE FOR THE SAME FAILURE CLASS TWICE. COUNTERFACTUAL REPLAY:
replay old business snapshots, missions, Ask questions, Jev decisions, owner
corrections, planner decisions through the new system WITHOUT executing
historical external effects; compare old decision / new decision / known outcome.
Jev eventually becomes a policy-development instrument, not policy authority:
shadow evidence may support proposing a deterministic policy change through the
proper authority mechanism. The model never grants itself authority.

## 29-31. Attention economics and architecture health

ORCA optimizes HUMAN ATTENTION: which things genuinely need Nolan, the owner,
evidence, deterministic policy, Jev, experimentation, or can safely remain
UNKNOWN. Mission Control becomes an attention compressor: thousands of machine
events, zero human interruptions — because no human authority was needed, not
because humans were bypassed. OWNER ATTENTION BUDGET: evaluate materiality,
consequence, reversibility, information gain, whether FYD can proceed safely,
batching, whether evidence will resolve it soon. Ask owners only high-value
questions. MC eventually reports architecture health: duplicate semantic owners,
bypass paths, authorities, truth stores, manual recovery procedures,
customer-specific branches, unreplayed events, UNKNOWN backlog, dead code,
dependency count, special cases, human intervention count. If every customer
adds infrastructure: stop — you're building a consultancy disguised as software.

## 32-33. Economics and the downward migration of intelligence

Measure real unit economics once businesses use it: cost/business/day, model
cost, browser/executor cost, storage growth, support minutes, owner minutes,
onboarding cost, acquisition cost, conversion, retention, gross margin,
value-producing actions, avoidable model calls, avoidable human interventions.
Economics become another falsifier. And whenever a model repeatedly makes the
same decision correctly and the rule becomes expressible, move it downward:
EXPENSIVE MODEL REASONING -> repeated evidence -> stable semantic rule ->
deterministic validation/policy -> model freed for novel ambiguity. PING gets
cheaper and more reliable as it learns. Reverse when deterministic machinery is
overfitted. Best evidence wins.

## 34. The website generator becomes a BUSINESS EXPERIENCE COMPILER

Eventually: business semantics + owner intent + customer task + media + trust +
brand + channel + viewport -> experience. The website is one compiled experience;
a customer question is another; an owner edit is another. Do not create this as
a grand framework prematurely. Let the 10-business corpus force it into
existence.

## 35. Data capitalization: only much later, on the right foundation

Owner control before economic models; no centralized raw-human-data
marketplace. Much later, with evidence of demand: portable business knowledge,
user-controlled data products, consented research datasets, verified provenance,
revocable licensing, compensation mechanisms. Foundation stays: THE PRINCIPAL
CONTROLS THE DATA. PING PROVES WHERE IT CAME FROM. PURPOSE CONTROLS USE.
REVOCATION IS REAL. LEARNING DOES NOT REQUIRE IDENTITY LEAKAGE.

## 36. The long-range maturity ladder

STAGE 1 CORRECT (doesn't lie about state). 2 DURABLE (restart/replay/recovery).
3 AUTONOMOUS (ORCA generates legitimate work). 4 USEFUL (owners accomplish real
work). 5 GENERAL (different businesses, no customer branches). 6 CURRENT
(continuous reconciliation). 7 PROCEDURAL (learns repeatable procedures).
8 OPERATING (authorized low-risk procedures execute autonomously). 9 LEARNING
(outcomes improve generic machinery within tenant/privacy bounds). 10 ECONOMIC
(demonstrably creates more value than cost). 11 COMPOUNDING (more businesses
make the product better while marginal human effort/business falls).

unsupported-claim rate, owner corrections, correction survival, grounded Ask
