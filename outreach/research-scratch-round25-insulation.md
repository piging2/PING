### Ecosystem notes (insulation)

The Western Colorado insulation pool is genuinely thin under the own-site email/form rule. Grand Junction has the densest cluster of insulation businesses (Accurate Insulation, EnergyPro Insulation, Pantuso Insulation, plus directory-only names like Alpine Insulation, Ace Insulation, and Grand Valley Home Insulation), but most established firms run phone-only own sites or exist only in directories. Durango/Pagosa Springs has a lively spray foam scene (Eagle Insulation, OnPoint Insulation, Foam Insulation Specialists, Piedra Spray Foam) tied to mountain-town winterization and second-home work, plus the 4CORE Home Upgrade program network in the southwest that lists many of these same firms as preferred contractors. That program connection is a real pressure point: rebate and energy-efficiency questions create qualification and paperwork load even when the contractor does not expose any rebate software publicly. Seasonal timing is favorable (mid-September pre-heating-season weatherization rush: attic top-ups, blown-in work, air sealing, ventilation, ice-dam prevention), but digitally contactable prospects are scarce. No genuinely new lead-management software was observed across the segment.

### Insulation-specific software/playbook finding

No new insulation estimating, energy-audit, Snugg Pro, rebate-processing, CRM, Podium, Jobber, or Housecall Pro evidence was observed for any Western Colorado insulation contractor in this batch. The only verifiable software signals are website platforms: Eagle Insulation runs on WordPress (blog pages), Piedra Spray Foam runs on Wix, EnergyPro Insulation runs on Squarespace, and Sprayfoam Solutions uses a hosted site-builder/CDN (irp.cdn-website.com). Separately, AeroBarrier product literature describes a computer-controlled sealing process that tracks ACH50 progress and generates a completion certificate; that is insulation-adjacent field technology from third-party materials, not lead-management software in any candidate's verified stack. One playbook note for the segment: insulation shops are field-heavy (crews on spray foam rigs, attics, rooflines) and tend to publish same-day or 24-hour callback promises, which makes instant-response lead capture a strong, concrete pitch angle even when their visible software is nothing.
