### Segment A ecosystem notes
Western Colorado's resort and second-home belt (Telluride, Aspen/Snowmass, Steamboat Springs, Crested Butte, Pagosa Springs, Ouray, Durango) creates structural demand for home watch: absentee owners need scheduled eyes on properties through freeze season, and most operators are owner-operators doing the checks themselves, which is exactly when inbound calls go to voicemail. September/October is the seasonal on-ramp: owners winterize and leave, and insurance carriers increasingly require documented vacancy inspections. Pitch angles that fit: missed-call capture during the fall surge, after-hours emergency triage for second-home owners, and review-proofing on Google/Angi (the trade's trust currency, per the national home-watch coverage this month noting "ghost kitchen" lead-gen operators burning homeowner trust, which makes documented local operators more valuable, not less).
