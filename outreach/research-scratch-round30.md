## Segment heat summary (September 2026)
- Fencing: late build season before ground freeze; ag/ranch fence is a Western Slope staple with owner-built crews on remote jobs out of cell range; free-estimate inbound while crews are in the field is the consistent pattern. Tier A contact routes are rare, which makes Alpine Fencing and The Fence Whisperer the segment's best eligible prospects.
- Concrete: tail of the season (freeze-thaw window closing); foundation/flatwork and garage-floor coatings are the urgent demand triggers; sealcoat-style urgency applies to exterior concrete. Shops that publish response-time promises (next-business-day, 2-hour) create the cleanest missed-call angle because the promise is on their own site.
- Seasonal overlap note: both trades share the "crews in the field, estimate requests land unattended" pattern; email-first outreach suits Alpine Fencing, Grand Valley Concrete Polishing, and Marathon Concrete Coatings.
