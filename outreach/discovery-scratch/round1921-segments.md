## 2. Seasonal notes

Late September is the pre-winter window for this segment:

- Drafty windows and failed seals: complaint volume rises as overnight temps drop.
  Seal-failure and fogged-glass calls are urgent and go to whoever answers first.
- Screen work: insect season is ending, so new-screen sales slow, but screen
  removal/storage and storm-window changeovers are a legitimate service touchpoint.
- Glass shops: pre-winter hardware, weatherstrip, and seal checks; broken-window
  calls become cold-weather emergencies.
- Retractable screens/awnings: winterization and off-season service scheduling.

Missed-call angle: every staged candidate is a small field crew. Owners are on
ladders, on installs, or on the road when the phone rings. A broken window in
October cold does not leave a voicemail twice.

## 7. Segment heat

Moderate-low. The Western Slope window/door/glass market is fragmented but
heavily picked over by prior rounds; the remaining independents are a
supply-house dealer (Black Canyon), family glass shops (B&B, Mountain Glass
Guys), and screen specialists with thin web footprints. No franchise
encroachment among the staged candidates. The Rifle/Carbondale/New Castle
corridor is thin: the only specialist surfaced there besides the staged
Mountain Glass Guys is intake-restricted, and Roaring Fork Glass has no own
site. Screens remain the least-worked corner of the segment and the best
hunting ground for the next pass.

## 8. Pitch angles

- Pre-winter urgency: failed seals, fogged glass, and drafty windows convert
  fast in October cold. Every missed call is a job the next shop takes.
- Field-crew profile: all three staged candidates are owner-operator or small
  family crews. The pitch is missed-call capture and quote-request follow-up
  while they are on installs, not a website lecture.
- Dealer angle (Black Canyon): contractor takeoff and delivery coordination;
  quote follow-ups that currently wait on callbacks.
- Screen angle: end-of-season screen repair plus winterization reminders as a
  repeat-touch revenue line.
- Stack claims stay graded UNKNOWN unless the site itself shows tooling.
  No website revamp proposed for any staged candidate; all three sites are
  functional and the owners are tradespeople, not the non-technical weak-site
  profile.
