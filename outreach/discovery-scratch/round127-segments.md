## Segment heat

Fence/deck staining and repair: Late September is the final reliable exterior staining
window before colder and wetter weather sets in across the Western Slope. Demand triggers
are UV/weather damage, failed or peeling stain, prep before winter, HOA maintenance
cycles, and the 1 to 4 year refinishing cycle that stain lifespans dictate. The work is
photo-driven: homeowners describe weathered wood, contractors need photos and site
details to scope prep (pressure washing, sanding, stripping, board replacement) and then
follow up on estimates while crews are in the field. Search surface in the corridor is
thin and dominated by broad painters, lead-broker directories, out-of-area specialists,
and fence builders already covered in earlier passes.

Log-home restoration: Western Colorado log homes face high UV exposure, freeze-thaw
cycles, failed stain, chinking separation, rot, drafts, and moisture intrusion. Fall is a
pre-winter inspection and sealing window; larger exterior restoration work competes for
the remaining suitable weather. Projects demand rich intake: photos, home age and
condition, location and access, exposure, rot/moisture details, inspection scheduling,
and prolonged estimate follow-up. The PING pitch here is structured intake and
consultation/quote follow-up, not generic missed-call messaging. The segment is genuinely
thin locally, with one dominant independent specialist (Pencil Log Pros, 16+ employees)
and the rest of the surface being out-of-area firms, kit manufacturers, or
directory-only names.
