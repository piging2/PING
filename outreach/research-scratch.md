## Ecosystem additions (2026-09-09)

**Foundation repair & basement/crawlspace waterproofing (new segment, 6 candidates).** Mid-September 2026 is prime inspection season in Denver metro: monsoon rains and summer watering have swollen the area's expansive clay soils, so homeowners are discovering seepage, wall cracks, bowing basement walls, and drainage failures right as the pre-freeze window opens. Foundation and waterproofing jobs are high-ticket and almost always won or lost at first contact, and the owner-led shops here are typically in the field on jobs all day. Segment is clean of national brands in the small-shop tier (Groundworks, Thrasher, Ram Jack dealers all skipped as corporate/franchise). Watch for lead-broker style sites ("our network's experts" copy) that look like contractors but are not.

**Masonry & stucco (new segment, 10 candidates).** Mid-September 2026 is the last good stretch of Denver's exterior work window, and freeze-thaw season is approaching fast, exactly when homeowners discover cracked stucco, failing tuckpointing, and leaky chimney crowns, driving a rush of repair inquiries. Most shops are family-run crews of one to five people with the owner on the scaffold or at the job site, so incoming calls routinely go to voicemail or get answered after hours. Core pitch fit: AI call capture answers every ring, qualifies the repair, and books the free estimate, while automated follow-up keeps quotes from going cold. Skip pattern: multi-state branded stucco sites and larger remodelers that bundle stucco as one of many trades.

**Drywall & texture (new segment, 9 candidates kept).** September is the pivot point in Colorado as exterior work winds down and homeowners turn to basements, texture re-dos, popcorn ceiling removal, and water-damage repairs, so these shops want to keep crews busy with interior projects. The market is fragmented: dozens of owner-led shops of one to twenty people, most taking intake by phone or a basic contact form, many owners doubling as estimator and scheduler. Strongest fits are owner-operators (Benjamin at B&D Drywall, owner-run Flow Drywall) where every missed call is missed revenue; already-staffed firms are lower priority. Stay excluded: franchise systems (PatchMaster, Hole in the Wall, All Four Walls) whose local operators have less buying authority, plus thin AI-template sites and no-own-site directory-only operations.
