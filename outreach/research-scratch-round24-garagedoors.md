## Segment heat / timing notes
- Demand triggers: broken torsion springs (loud bang, door opens 6 to 8 inches then stops) are the single most urgent trigger; Colorado freeze-thaw cycles and winter cold cause spring metal fatigue, lubricant thickening, and track misalignment; summer heat and humidity swell and strain doors. A door stuck open is a security emergency, a door stuck closed can trap a car.
- Pitch angles: first-to-answer-wins on emergency calls (several shops advertise 24/7 but run daytime-only office hours, so after-hours missed calls are real); AI concierge for after-hours emergency triage (is the door stuck open or closed, is anyone hurt, offer secure-the-garage guidance) plus instant booking; several shops lean on phone-only contact routes, so missed-call capture is the cleanest value story.
- Skip patterns: the segment is saturated with templated city-page SEO sites and lead brokers (centennialgaragedoorrepair.net style) and a national franchise layer (A1, Precision, Dave's); verify the business is Colorado-owned before qualifying. A1 Garage Door Service uses the same 5081 Kingston St address as Don's Garage Door Repair, so watch for address-sharing brands.
- Software observation: unlike the Western Colorado HVAC segment, these shops show little evidence of Housecall Pro or similar booking wizards; most routes are plain phone numbers or simple WordPress contact forms. Johnson Garage Doors has a book-online link (provider not identifiable). No strong booking-friction signal, so lead with missed-call capture rather than booking-friction relief.
