## Ecosystem notes

- Movers (Western CO, batch 1, 6 candidates, 2026-09-09): strongest independent clusters are Grand Junction, Montrose/Ridgway, Durango, Glenwood Springs, and Steamboat Springs; Delta, Gunnison, Cortez, and Rifle are mostly served by regional firms with no strong town-based websites. Movers are highly seasonal and quote-driven, with firms asking customers to book 3 to 6 weeks ahead in peak periods, so speed-to-quote and after-hours capture are the highest-value pain points. Several firms run phone-first intake, which makes after-hours quote qualification and automated follow-up a credible angle. Skip patterns: van-line agents (Atlas, Bekins), national brokers, directory-only results.
- Gutters (Western CO, batch 1, 10 unverified leads, 2026-09-09): Western Colorado gutter shops are overwhelmingly small, owner-operated, gutter-first businesses with weak or free-tier web presence (business.site pages, directory-only listings), which means most have no after-hours intake. Timing is strong: September is pre-winter gutter season, with heat tape, snow stops, and clean-outs in the service mix, so winterization urgency plus leaf season makes missed-call and follow-up automation timely. The biggest pool (Durango, Gunnison, Steamboat, Fruita, Palisade, Clifton, Delta, Craig, Meeker) is dominated by directories (Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, jbhaveman) with few standalone local sites surfacing; plan Facebook-page checks for phone-only shops.
- Cross-segment observation: Western CO pools remain thin under the strict own-site email/form rule, consistent with the round 11 yield note. Movers yielded a clean qualified batch; gutters need one more browser-capable verification pass to convert the 10 leads into qualified candidates.
