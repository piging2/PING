## Ecosystem notes (segment heat/timing, 2026-09-10)

- Landscaping: mid-September is the pivot point for Western Colorado landscapers. The aeration and overseeding window is open, leaves are starting to drop, irrigation winterization bookings are filling, and snow routes are being lined up. Crews are in the field all day during this rush, so the phone rings when nobody can answer and fall-cleanup quotes sit without follow-up. That makes fast lead capture, scheduling, and customer follow-up especially relevant right now, and the pitch-fit runs through all nine candidates. Segment skews owner-led with thin older websites; email routes cluster in Grand Junction, form routes dominate the rest.
- Pool/spa: mid-September is the industry's choke point. Winterization and fall prep are the urgent seasonal hook, and ski-town second homes plus short-term rentals in the Vail Valley and Steamboat drive high-stakes service demand where customers book whoever answers first. Route-based shops (Hot Water Productions, The Hot Tub Guy) are the strongest segment because technicians drive all day while urgent calls come in, so reliable call capture and follow-up is a concrete pain rather than a vague benefit. The owner-operator tier (Chaparral, As You Wish) has a one-person bottleneck where every missed call is a lost job. Segment is thinner than landscaping under the own-site email/form rule and heavily phone-primary; four strong brands (Rimrock, Ajax, Colorado Pool + Spa Scapes, Spa Country) remain phone-primary or unverified, so phone outreach is the next channel if form and email coverage runs dry.
