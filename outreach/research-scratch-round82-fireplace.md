## Segment timing and pitch-angle note

September is pre-winter peak prep season for this segment. Homeowners book chimney cleaning, inspections, service, and stove/fireplace installations now, so September through November is the highest inbound-call window of the year. Every candidate above is phone-primary (none has online scheduling or visible CRM capture observed; only True North has a quote form), and several post weekday-only or limited hours. The consistent pitch is missed-call lead capture plus instant quote follow-up: after-hours and in-field calls during the fall rush, with website revamp as the secondary angle for the three candidates with no own website (Hearth 'N Home Products, Ute City Fireplaces, High Country Hearth, The Fireplace Doctors, Dimpfl's, Alpine Stove).
