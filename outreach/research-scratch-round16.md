## Ecosystem notes

- Segment heat (mid-September): window cleaning enters its fall rush (pre-winter exterior spruce-ups, holiday lighting season is a cross-sell: Western Slope Window Cleaning and others bundle Christmas light installs, and booking for that starts now). Garage doors are steady year-round with weather-spike demand: broken springs, off-track doors, and opener failures do not wait for office hours, and every shop sells fast response. Second-home and rental markets in Glenwood Springs, Aspen, and Durango add absentee-owner urgency for both trades.
- Demand signals: Grand Junction has the densest window-cleaning cluster (6+ own-site shops), Montrose has the densest garage-door cluster (Prutch's, Montrose Garage Doors, Professional Garage Doors, Wenco in Delta), Durango has 3-4 window-cleaning own sites. Glenwood Springs, Rifle, Cortez, Gunnison, Craig, Meeker, and Aspen yielded no independently verifiable own-site window cleaners or garage door shops under the strict rule; those towns are phone-only or directory-only.
- Common skip reasons observed: phone-only own sites (Glassy, A+ Window Washing Durango), directory-only with no own site (Glassy, A Clear View), franchise/regional multi-city templated pages (Renewal by Andersen GJ, Prutch's city pages, DaBella, Window World GJ, Glass Doctor), third-party-only emails (Above All, Grand Valley, A Quality Garage Doors), window replacement shops mislabeled as window cleaning (American Windows and Doors, Bratton, EcoWatch listers).
- New learning: both trades are more competitive than expected in Grand Junction and Montrose specifically, with several shops advertising sub-hour response, same-day quotes, and text-back promises they cannot keep while crews are on ladders or trucks; the pitch that lands is not "you miss calls" (they know) but "your own site already promises speed you cannot deliver without backup." Also, garage door shops in this market live on their inboxes far more than other trades (Gmail/Yahoo addresses are the norm, not the exception), so email-first outreach is the natural channel here.
- Pipeline note: only 2 candidates are fully verified under the strict own-site rule this round. The 13 near-misses above are the highest-yield follow-up: one own-site contact-page verification pass each would likely convert 6-10 of them into qualified candidates.
