## Segment-heat note

Timing: mid-September is pre-winter planning season for custom builds on the Western Slope (permits and site work before freeze); builders are also coming off Parade of Homes season (GJ fall parade visibility), so "follow-up on parade inquiries and consultation requests" is a live pain. Pitch angles that fit this segment: (1) lead qualification triage, separate serious build inquiries from tire-kickers before the owner spends an hour on a consult; (2) missed-call capture, owners are on job sites all day with no dispatcher; (3) estimate/consult follow-up discipline on 6-18 month sales cycles, nothing leaks; (4) after-hours capture for out-of-town buyers researching lots remotely (Durango/Pagosa second-home traffic). Buildertrend/CoConstruct users are not targets for scheduling replacement; the pitch is capture + qualification + follow-up, layered on their existing portal. Revamp angle applies to almost nobody in this segment (most have decent sites); the thin-site shops mostly lack own sites entirely and are excluded by the qualification bar.
