## Segment heat / timing notes for the ecosystem notes

Septic / sewer / well / pump:
- Septic pumping runs on 2 to 5 year recall cycles, so most shops live on recall plus emergency demand; several sites explicitly promise free estimates and same-day/emergency service, which creates the missed-call pain.
- 24/7 emergency positioning is everywhere (Goodwin, Owens, San Juan Pump's no-water urgency), but several shops run office-only hours; after-hours calls are the clearest AI receptionist angle.
- Durango is a septic hot spot: Durango Septic, Mountain Septic, plus LePew (Four Corners) and Alpenglow (Gunnison). All small, family-run or owner-operator, phone-first.
- Well and pump shops (San Juan Pump, Valley Pump) have water-outage urgency: a homeowner with no water calls the next shop in the list if the first rings out.
- No scheduling software was observed on any site in this segment (Squarespace, Duda, hand-built, WordPress). Stack research for these nine needs a live widget check during outreach. Segment software grade: UNKNOWN across the board.
- Pitch angle that repeats: the shop that stakes its reputation on answering the phone (Durango Septic literally says it) but has a small crew in the field.

Excavation / dirtwork / grading:
- Demand clusters: site preparation, foundations, grading and drainage, septic installation, utility trenching, demolition, hauling, land clearing.
- Pitch angles: missed bid requests, estimate follow-up, permit coordination, site-evaluation scheduling, routing residential versus commercial inquiries.
- Water, drainage, erosion control, and river restoration work (Tate Civil, Environmental Excavation) creates detailed intake beyond a simple quote form.
- Five phone-only operators (Ben Dowd, Professional Excavating, Grover Excavating, Inno-Ex, Metcalf Excavation) are most exposed to missed calls while crews run equipment.
- Metcalf's public description indicates spring and summer reclamation and stormwater work, then fall and winter road and emergency operations: year-round but seasonally shifting intake.
- Tate Civil's stated one-business-day inquiry response is a strong speed-to-lead angle.
- Software: platform CONFIRMED for a few (Wix x2, WordPress, GoDaddy x2); the rest UNKNOWN. No booking widgets or CRM badges observed anywhere in the segment.
- Note: Double T's own site uses generic landing-page-generator styling ("Excavating Company Near Me" title) but is their own domain, not a multi-city template farm, so it stays qualified.
