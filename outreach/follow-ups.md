# Follow-up log

Every follow-up touch and every review-triggered message is logged here. Pipeline statuses F1 SENT / F2 SENT flip only after Gmail or Messenger acceptance.

## Ladder rules (standing, 2026-09-11)
- F1 at day 4 to 7 after first send: short bump under 50 words, references the original angle, one question max, opens with the not-a-customer line.
- F2 at day 10 to 14: final touch with a NEW angle (a bad review seen since, seasonal timing, a specific new observation) and an easy out.
- Never more than three total touches (initial plus F1 plus F2). Never follow up a "no interest" or any decline. Stop the ladder permanently on any reply.
- Before each eligible follow-up, re-evaluate the newest intelligence: if there is a materially better reason to contact them, the follow-up uses that context instead of repeating the original pitch. Never invent a trigger.
- Follow-ups count inside the 10-per-UTC-day Gmail cap and take PRIORITY over new cold sends.
- Review-triggered messages: max one per business per 90 days; suppressed while a follow-up ladder is active. Reference the pain category, never the reviewer, never the review text.
