## Ecosystem notes

Locksmiths (Western Colorado): seasonal timing is mild but year-round with spikes in winter (frozen locks, dead batteries at trailheads) and vacation season (short-term rental smart-lock installs in mountain towns); commercial rekey work picks up around back-to-school and property turnover. Heat: HIGH. Callers are in acute distress and dial the next company in seconds, so instant AI answering is the most defensible ROI pitch in any trade. Pitch angles: after-hours emergency lockout capture, commercial access-control and panic-bar job scheduling, safe sales/service upsell, review capture from high-volume happy customers, vacation-rental smart-lock installs for Durango/Steamboat shops. Trade-specific software worth learning: WordPress dominates these sites (CONFIRMED at Honk-Key), Convertri appeared as a STRONG INDICATION at one Durango shop, and typical shops run simple contact forms and phone-only operations with no CRM at all. The bigger prize is integrating with dispatch-light workflows rather than heavy FSM products.

Water damage restoration (Western Colorado): seasonal timing is the whole business, spring runoff and snowmelt flooding, summer monsoon and flash floods, winter pipe bursts, sewage backups year-round. Heat: MEDIUM, but lead quality is excellent (average job values are high, insurance-paid). Independent pool is genuinely thin: franchises (SERVPRO, ServiceMaster) dominate Western Colorado search results and many independents are just regional pages; the viable independents cluster around Grand Junction and the Roaring Fork. Pitch angles: 24/7 emergency intake when phones ring overnight, free-inspection lead capture, clean insurance documentation workflows, fast follow-up with adjusters and property managers. Trade-specific software worth learning: Xactimate is the insurance estimating standard (CONFIRMED on RemediH2O's own site), IICRC certifications are the credibility marker every shop leads with, and restoration shops run on moisture-log and job-documentation tools (RestorVision, DASH, PSA) plus standard FSMs. Anyone pitching this trade should learn the Xactimate workflow cold.
