## Ecosystem notes

- Segment heat (mid-September): electrical is in the pre-heating and pre-winter reliability window (generator evaluations, panel upgrades, service calls, second-home winterization, lighting/shade automation projects, 24/7 emergency positioning). Pest control is in the late-summer/early-fall stack (wasps/yellow jackets, elm seed bugs, spiders, rodents moving indoors, real-estate termite inspections, recurring-plan renewals), and same-day promises make response speed the whole pitch.
- Demand signals: Durango/Bayfield yielded two strong electrical qualifiers (Apex, C/T); Steamboat one (Coon); Glenwood Springs one (Littell) plus the larger R&A; Grand Junction/Gunnison one (EC Electric, a two-town operator). Pest control qualified in Grand Junction (Jerry's, Penguin, King), Delta/Montrose (Pest Away), Fruita (Chrome), Montrose (Penguin), and Steamboat Springs (Valley Varmints). Cortez, Gunnison (pest), Craig, Meeker, Pagosa Springs, Aspen, Carbondale, and Rifle yielded no independently verifiable own-site qualifiers this round.
- Common skip reasons observed: phone-only own sites, directory-only with no own site, parked/lead-gen domains, lead-broker "network" sites, franchise/chain multi-location structures, and geographic mismatches where the own site describes an operation outside the Western Colorado focus.
- New learning: electrical shops in this market almost never publish a decision-maker name on their own sites (JMJ and Stagecoach are the exceptions, both named owners but no own-site route captured); pest shops are slightly more likely to name owners (Penguin's Spencer Christensen, Lady Bug's Christene Cheairs). Western Slope pest firms post Gmail/Outlook/AOL addresses directly on their sites more often than other trades, so email-first outreach is the natural channel there.
- Pipeline note: 12 candidates fully verified under the strict own-site rule this round (6 electrical, 6 pest). The 3 near-misses above are the highest-yield follow-up: one own-site contact-page verification pass each would likely convert all three (all three already have named owners on their own sites).
