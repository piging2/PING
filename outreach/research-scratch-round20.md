## ECOSYSTEM NOTES (segment heat, seasonal timing, pitch angles)

- Segment heat: both Western CO pools are thin under the strict own-site email/form rule, confirming the 2026-09-09 yield note. Appliance pool is dominated by dealer-service hybrids (Western Slope Appliance, Bob's Place, Tri-River) and single-phone family shops; ~24 appliance candidates checked, 2 qualified, 1 near-miss for verify. Fencing: ~21 candidates checked, 5 qualified. The rest are phone-only, directory-only, dead, national templates, or out of territory.
- Appliance urgency: this trade runs on urgency. A dead fridge in summer goes to whoever answers first, and Best Appliance Service Montrose publicly booking 2-3 weeks out confirms demand exceeds capacity. "Missed call = lost job" is the sharpest lever in this segment.
- Fencing seasonal timing: late summer/early fall is still build season before the ground freezes; every shop pushes free estimates, so estimate intake speed is the differentiator. Bid shopping is heavy (cedar, vinyl, chain link, iron, gates); homeowners collect several bids and the shop that responds first wins.
- Pitch angles discovered:
  1. Gate/automation niche (England Fence, Steamboat Gate Systems): high-ticket, consultation-heavy quotes involving terrain, digging conditions, gate sizes, operators, and financing spread over multiple touches. Slow response while crews are on jobs sends buyers to the next bidder. Strongest AI intake and quote-chasing fit in the segment.
  2. Ranch/agricultural fencing (AgriTek, Durango Fence): crews are on remote ranch jobs out of cell range; estimate capture and triage while the crew is unreachable, so nothing sits in voicemail until Monday. Same dynamic as Barrett Fence.
  3. After-hours/overflow call capture layered onto existing online schedulers for appliance shops: even shops with a scheduler route urgent calls to one phone line, and a dead fridge in summer does not wait.
  4. Recurring-maintenance and rental-contract scheduling automation for family appliance shops with commercial accounts (Century Appliance model): contract customers and rental turnover eat the owner's day.
  5. Showroom + materials-yard model (Valleywide): DIY and pro-install customers across Western CO and Eastern Utah with inquiries routed across team members; instant intake and routing beats slower shops on bids.
