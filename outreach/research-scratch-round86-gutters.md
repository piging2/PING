## Segment heat notes

- Fall gutter season is opening. Leaf fall, pre-winter drainage checks, and ice dam preparation create real inbound demand right now; this is the segment's best window of the year.

- Phone-first intake dominates. Nearly every remaining shop is an owner-operator on a ladder or jobsite when calls arrive, which makes MISSED_CALL_LEAD_CAPTURE the most defensible primary angle.
