## Ecosystem notes (round 13, 2026-09-09)

Plumbing (Western Colorado): shops are overwhelmingly phone-first, and almost none of the smaller independent outfits publish an email or contact form on their own sites, which throttles this channel but sharpens the pitch: these owners live by the phone while on jobsites and leak inbound opportunities on every missed call. Demand is in shoulder-season mode for mid-September 2026: irrigation and swamp-cooler work winding down, boiler and heating service about to ramp, second-home and short-term-rental turnover still steady in resort markets like Steamboat, and the 24/7 emergency pages keep ranking even when offices only staff weekdays. Regional players in Montrose and Grand Junction skew toward family-owned multi-tech operations with 15 to 30 years of history, while Cortez, Gunnison, Craig, and Meeker run thinner with owner-operators and small crews. The strict own-site email/form rule was the real yield constraint: phone-only own sites were binned as near-misses even when the fit was strong, and several lookalike names resolved to parked domains or out-of-region businesses.

HVAC (Western Colorado): a patchwork of independent, family-run, and employee-owned shops rather than national chains, anchored in Grand Junction, Montrose, and Glenwood Springs with spokes into resort and rural towns (Aspen, Basalt, Durango, Hotchkiss, Cortez). Heating dominates: furnaces, boilers, and hydronic/radiant systems rule the mountain towns while the Grand Valley adds evaporative coolers and AC. September is the hinge month: furnace tune-ups, install-quote follow-up, and first-cold-snap breakdowns stack up, plus ski-town second homes needing reliable service from owners who are not on site. Nearly every shop runs its own website with a working contact form or published email, making direct outreach practical. The shops that feel the most pain are mid-size independents with real crews, where a missed call or slow follow-up during the autumn rush costs an actual booked job. Skip patterns this pass: phone-only own sites (Arctic, Pioneer, Advanced Refrigeration, All Star Durango, Lunsford), parked/dead domains, hijacked or redirecting domains, directory-only listings, and a Wheat Ridge-headquartered branch (Major Heating) deprioritized as not clearly local.
