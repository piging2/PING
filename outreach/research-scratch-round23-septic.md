## Segment heat / timing notes

- Septic pumping is on a 2 to 5 year cycle, so most of these shops live on recall and emergency demand; several sites explicitly mention free estimates and same-day/emergency service, which creates strong missed-call pain.
- 24/7 emergency positioning is everywhere in this trade (Goodwin, Owens, San Juan Pump's no-water urgency), but several shops have office-only hours; after-hours calls are the clearest AI receptionist angle.
- Durango-area septic demand is high: Durango Septic, Mountain Septic, plus LePew and Alpenglow serving the Four Corners and Gunnison Valley. These are small, family-run or owner-operator shops with phone-only routes, which is exactly the profile that benefits from call capture.
- Well and pump shops (San Juan Pump, Valley Pump) have water-outage urgency; a homeowner with no water will call the next shop in the list if the first rings out.
- No scheduling software was observed on any site in this segment (one Squarespace site, one Duda site, several hand-built or WordPress sites). None showed a booking widget, so stack research for these nine will need a live widget check during outreach. Segment software grade: UNKNOWN across the board.
- Pitch angle that repeats well: the shop that stakes its reputation on answering the phone (Durango Septic literally says it) but has a small crew in the field. Every one of these nine fits that mold.
