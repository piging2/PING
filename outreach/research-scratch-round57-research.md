## Seasonal timing (2026-09-15)

Segment A: installers are opening design and estimate books right now. Observed this run: Wheatley Creek starts installs in September with the most flexible dates, then the schedule tightens toward Thanksgiving; Monument Lighting's own site frames September as the start of holiday booking season. Mid-September is the peak advance-booking window for holiday lighting. Every week of delay costs install-calendar slots.

Segment B: late-summer storm season is tailing off and the pre-winter frozen and burst-pipe ramp is ahead. 24/7 emergency positioning is near-universal among Segment B operators, so after-hours missed-call capture remains the single strongest angle.
