## Ecosystem notes (Western Slope solar, 2026)

- Federal tax credits expired 12/31/2025 under the 2025 federal budget bill (stated on Solar Design Group's own site). Every remaining lead now costs more to win, which is the core argument for better capture and follow-up.
- La Plata Electric Association issued a February 2024 news release responding to increased solar generation in Durango, signaling utility rate and net-metering pressure in the Four Corners area. Interconnection and net-metering terms vary by utility across the Western Slope (Holy Cross Energy, Delta-Montrose Electric Association, Grand Valley Power, Yampa Valley Electric Association, Gunnison County Electric Association, Xcel territory), and installers like Atlasta and Brightside build permit and interconnection handling into their sales process.
- Installer rebate paperwork (GEO, LPEA) is handled by shops like Durango Solar Works, adding admin load that fits automation.
- Orphaned-system repair is a real sub-market: Sunsense Solar markets rescue of systems installed by other firms, and Shaw Solar and High Noon Solar both sell maintenance contracts, a good recurring-revenue angle for PING service reminders.
