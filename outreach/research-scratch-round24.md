## Segment heat / timing notes (for ecosystem notes)

Asphalt / paving (Western Colorado):
- Seasonality: Colorado paving season runs roughly May through October. Sealcoating needs sustained pavement temps above 50F and dry conditions, so September is the tail end of the booking window: property managers and HOAs rush to get lots sealed before winter, and missed calls now are gone until spring. Commercial lot work (striping, patching) also spikes as businesses fix pavement before snow.
- Demand triggers: HOA and commercial property manager sealcoat and restriping scheduling; residential driveways needing replacement after winter freeze-thaw damage; pothole and patching calls after spring melt; municipal and subdivision road projects in summer.
- Pitch angles: crews are on pavers, rollers, and sealcoat rigs all day and cannot answer; after-hours and weekend quote requests from homeowners and property managers go to voicemail; estimate follow-up (driveway jobs are won by the fastest quote); seasonal maintenance reminders (sealcoat every 24 to 36 months is a natural recurring touchpoint); multi-branch shops like SealCo need consistent call handling across offices.
- Skip patterns: Grand Junction is crowded with paving shops (most verified candidates sit there); Glenwood/Rifle/Carbondale and Durango/Cortez/Pagosa coverage is thinner and worth another pass; Montrose and Delta yielded only Armor ProSeal verified; Cortez, Pagosa Springs, Craig, and Meeker produced no own-site-verified shops this round. Franchise and lead-broker template sites (Jet-Black style, white-label "CPS" content) appear in this segment and should keep being filtered out. Several strong local shops (Kauffman, High Country, JB's, CM Asphalt) have weak or no websites, which is both a pipeline gap and an inbound angle: thin web presence correlates with phone-only workflows and high missed-call pain.

Garage doors (Denver metro):
- Demand triggers: broken torsion springs (loud bang, door opens 6 to 8 inches then stops) are the single most urgent trigger; Colorado freeze-thaw cycles and winter cold cause spring metal fatigue, lubricant thickening, and track misalignment; summer heat and humidity swell and strain doors. A door stuck open is a security emergency, a door stuck closed can trap a car.
- Pitch angles: first-to-answer-wins on emergency calls (several shops advertise 24/7 but run daytime-only office hours, so after-hours missed calls are real); AI concierge for after-hours emergency triage (door stuck open or closed, is anyone hurt, offer secure-the-garage guidance) plus instant booking; several shops lean on phone-only contact routes, so missed-call capture is the cleanest value story.
- Skip patterns: segment is saturated with templated city-page SEO sites and lead brokers (centennialgaragedoorrepair.net style) and a national franchise layer (A1, Precision, Dave's); verify Colorado ownership before qualifying. A1 Garage Door Service shares the 5081 Kingston St address with Don's Garage Door Repair, so watch for address-sharing brands.
- Software observation: unlike other trades, these shops show little evidence of Housecall Pro or similar booking wizards; most routes are plain phone numbers or simple WordPress contact forms. Johnson Garage Doors has a book-online link (provider not identifiable). No strong booking-friction signal, so lead with missed-call capture rather than booking-friction relief.
