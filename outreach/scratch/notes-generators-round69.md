## Notable findings

1. The Western Colorado independent generator-service segment is genuinely sparse: roughly a dozen search waves across 15+ towns surfaced exactly one new qualifying shop. Results are dominated by national networks, lead-gen city pages, directories, manufacturers, and Front Range companies.
2. The winning pattern this round: solar/backup-power shops that are *authorized generator dealers with own-site published email* (Sunwise + Kohler Power). These are the reachable generator-adjacent independents in a region where pure generator shops barely exist.
3. Many "generator" hits are shared-number city pages (888-705-3728 style) recycling the same copy across towns; treat identical copy + toll-free number as a skip without further research.
4. One prior near-miss graduated: Sunwise Solar was a round-60 near-miss because its own website had not been located; own-site fetch this round confirmed the Kohler Power dealer claim and the published email, which is why it is now a final row.
