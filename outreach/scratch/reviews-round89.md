## Trigger definition (only these count)
A review posted ON OR AFTER 2026-09-08 that is: 2 stars or fewer (any Google review), OR 3 stars AND mentions phone / response / wait / scheduling problems. Undated negatives are intel-only, NEVER triggers. Non-Google platforms (Angi, Yelp, HomeAdvisor, Indeed, BBB) are out of trigger scope; recorded as intel only. No reviewer names, no review-text quotes, no identifying content recorded. Pain categories only.

## Caveats
- Google Business Profile review pages are JS-heavy and not readable through these tools; evidence comes from aggregator mirrors (Birdeye) and own-site Trustindex widgets. A brand-new Google review posted in the last ~24h might not have surfaced.
- Birdeye fetches display ratings/review counts but not per-review dates or the full negative-review text set; low ratings implied by sub-5.0 averages could not be dated and count as intel-only.
