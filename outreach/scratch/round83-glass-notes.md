## Segment timing and heat (2026-09-18)

* Rocky Mountain Glass is the hottest timing signal: its own contact page says it is slammed, prioritizing existing customers, and only accepting projects from general contractors. Intake triage and missed-call capture are directly relevant right now.
* The Mountain Glass Guys is hiring experienced glaziers, a growth signal.
* Elmer Glass is actively asking customers for project-story reviews, a review-automation opening.
* Fall approaching in the mountains usually lifts window and storm-related repair demand heading into winter; shops with same-day or free-estimate intake are the best fit for missed-call capture.

## Pitch angles

* Missed-call and after-hours capture: Sun Glass Montrose, Crested Butte Glass, The Mountain Glass Guys, Contreras Glass. All run free-estimate or quote intake with lean teams.
* Contractor lead triage: Rocky Mountain Glass. They are demand-capped and filtering to general contractors, so an automated qualifier that sorts contractor versus homeowner inquiries without adding phone time fits their stated situation.
* Website revamp: Epic Custom Glass (stale wording) and Elmer Glass (aged template). Revamp only for clearly non-technical owners per the standing rule.
* Vendor and partnership framing: Sun Glass Montrose (commercial storefront, curtain wall, automatic doors) is the strongest B2B fit.
* AEO and local discoverability: every candidate above. Several own sites are thin on structured local content.
