# Research Round 76: carpet & upholstery cleaning + handyman / property maintenance (Western Colorado)

Date: 2026-09-17. Two segments, 18 deduped candidates (8 carpet, 10 handyman).

## Segment selection rationale

**Chosen: (1) carpet & upholstery cleaning, (2) handyman / property maintenance.**

The suggested concrete and well/pump segments were actually researched very recently: concrete was re-swept on 2026-09-17 in research-round75-concrete.md (pool described as exhausted, only one new qualifier), and well drilling was researched on 2026-09-16 in research-round67-welldrilling.md (only four new qualifiers after 16 prior names were deduped). A third candidate, deck builders, was test-swept this round and deferred: the dedicated-deck-builder pool on the Western Slope is genuinely thin (BBB shows only ~8 "deck builder" results near Durango, most are GCs; Montrose/GJ "deck" results were Front Range companies or a California location-spam template; prior rounds already hold Durango Decks Inc., Valleywide Fence and Deck, CL Deck and Porches, Colorado Custom Covers and Decks, Colorado Fence & Deck, DeckTec Outdoor Designs). The one solid deck find is recorded below as a deferred-segment note rather than padded into a segment. Carpet cleaning and handyman/property maintenance have not been deeply worked in recent rounds (round 41 touched 6 carpet names on 2026-09-13, mostly staged/reference-only; handyman coverage is scattered directory mentions), both skew heavily to small owner-operated shops with weak web presence, and both are entering their fall demand season.

**Verification caveat this round:** the page-fetch tool failed mid-round (terminal for the round; not retried). Only Swiss Pro Carpet Cleaning's own site was fully fetched and verified. All other candidates' websites are own domains surfaced via business profiles (not yet render-verified), and their phones come from directory/business profiles, NOT from own-site publication. Form verdicts are therefore "unverified from fetched page" throughout except where noted. Phone/email below are labeled accordingly; do not treat directory-reported phones as own-site-confirmed.
