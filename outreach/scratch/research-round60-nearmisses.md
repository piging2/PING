## SEGMENT NOTES

Heat and segment observations:
- Genuine small independent standby-generator specialists in Western Colorado are sparse. Most segment-relevant businesses are local electrical contractors who offer generator evaluations, installs, and maintenance as one service line, plus one dedicated generator dealer (Integrative Electric) and a family-owned regional home-services company (M&R).
- Search results are heavily polluted by national lead-generation pages (Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yelp ads), manufacturer dealer locators, generic city SEO pages with shared 888 numbers, and Front Range companies. Every candidate must be verified on its own domain.
- Generac published a 2026 Colorado standby-generator buyer survey with a useful market fact: 81 percent of surveyed Colorado homeowners who bought a Generac standby generator in 2026 cited "outage protection" as the primary purchase reason. Generac also reports the first step in the buying process is online research, which strengthens the AI-discoverability angle for every generator installer on the prospect list.

Seasonal timing (September):
- Pre-winter readiness is the strongest angle: test before the first outage, annual maintenance reminders, battery and exercise-cycle checks, propane/natural-gas backup for heating, refrigeration, medical devices, well pumps, and remote properties.
- Outage-season demand spikes create missed-call risk, a natural fit for missed-call capture.

Pitch angles to reuse for this segment:
- Automated annual maintenance reminders and maintenance-plan renewals.
- Missed-call capture during outage spikes.
- Generator evaluation intake and qualification (free on-site evaluation flows, as offered by T & G Electric and Stagecoach Electric).
- Installation follow-up and warranty reminders.
- Customer education on sizing, fuel type, transfer switches, and service intervals.

Skip patterns to keep applying:
- National chains and franchises; corporate marketing support means no local buying authority.
- Front Range-only companies; target is Western Colorado first.
- Directory-only profiles and generic SEO city pages.
- Phone-only own sites with no email or own-domain form.
- Solar or electrical firms with no explicit generator evidence on their own sites.
