# Research Round 78 ecosystem notes — dumpster rental + propane services (Western Colorado, 2026-09-17)

18 deduped NEW candidates (10 dumpster rental/roll-off, 8 propane services). 8 draft-eligible with own-site-verified contact; 10 phone-only rows.

## Segment heat

**Dumpster rental: WARM-HOT.** Thinner than plumbing/HVAC (round70 took the obvious Grand Junction names), but the remaining independents are high-quality owner-operated shops: Alpine Roll-Off Services (Rifle, own-site email + form, online booking launched Aug 2026) and Dump-It Dumpsters (Montrose, 20+ yr local, email + form) both rate HOT. Montrose clusters the rest (Oak Grove, Steel Rhino, Coyote). Cortez/Pagosa/Steamboat yielded mostly directory-only or chains; Gunnison gave Western Sanitation (family since 2023).

**Propane services: WARM, thin by nature.** Most Western Slope markets are chain-dominated (AmeriGas, Ferrellgas, Suburban; JC Propane acquired by DCC 2022, EnergyUnited became ThompsonGas). Only 8 genuine independents surfaced across 17 towns. Standouts: Comfurt Gas (Buena Vista, 3rd-gen since 1948, DIY-grade site, own-site order form) and White River Energy (Meeker, community-founded 1997, own-site form + refill request) both HOT.

## Seasonal timing

- Dumpster: fall construction/cleanout season is the demand driver; contractors and roofers book now before snow. Angle: after-hours quote calls go to a competitor hauler, not a voicemail.
- Propane: heating season starts Oct-Nov; price-inquiry and runout-call volume spikes are weeks away. Angle: missed-call coverage and refill-request follow-up, not generic chatbots.

## Pitch angles

1. **Missed-call capture (7 of 18).** Both segments are phone-first with office-hours staff. Dump-It, Alpine, Mountain West Propane (after-hours calls route to a cell) are the strongest fits.
2. **Website revamp (4 of 18, non-technical owners).** Comfurt Gas ("HOME | mysite-1" DIY site), Bison Rolloff (Wix single-page, phone-only), Coyote Rolloff (thin site, since 2024). Per Nolan's rule: revamp pitch only for very non-technical owners with weak sites, backed by the TenantOS portfolio asset.
3. **Automation, not portals (3 of 18).** Several propane shops already run payment/account portals (RCCBI webhub, own refill portals). Do not pitch "get a portal." Pitch request confirmation, after-hours capture, and review generation on top of what they already have.
4. **New-business setup (1 of 18).** Coyote Rolloff (Montrose, since 2024) and 5R Haul Off (Cortez, no website, new directory footprint) are young operators: set it up right from day one (capture every call, instant booking, reviews from the start). No 60-90-day SOS filings found this round.
