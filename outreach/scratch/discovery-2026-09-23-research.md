### Segment intelligence

**Water treatment (indoor water systems).** Evergreen, not seasonal: well-water households and hard-city-water homes need filtration, softeners, and RO year round. Health-urgency angle is real (families paying for bottled water or worried about well contamination), and the ticket is whole-home systems, which means follow-up-heavy sales cycles and missed-call capture on estimate requests. Pool is thin because the corridor is dominated by one national franchise (Culligan, Grand Junction, 819 Kimball Ave, 970-243-0628) and one regional multi-office operator (The Source H2O, HQ Centennial, Grand Junction office 784 Valley Ct Unit B4, 970-242-9742), with the one verified independent already canonical (Smart Living Water, 2829 North Ave #103, 970-773-6439). Rural-water-hauling angle checked: a 2015 report documents the Little Park Road/Glade Park cistern-delivery operator shutting down for health reasons with hundreds of residents needing water, and no verifiable replacement operator surfaced (HD Water Supply is Bailey/Front Range; MCMS Inc is Loveland, both out of corridor). Intelligence value for future rounds: rural hauled-water demand exists but no web-visible local supplier; a new independent water hauler in Mesa County would be a radar hit worth watching for.

**Holiday lighting installation (seasonal + permanent).** Peak relevance RIGHT NOW: installers start hanging in September/October, November slots tighten, post-Thanksgiving is the most limited window (confirmed on a Grand County operator's own site: Wheatley Creek Services, wheatleycreek.com, installations begin September, phone 970-531-1308, email hello@wheatleycreek.com). Multi-touch friction is structural: install, mid-season maintenance, takedown, and storage create a natural recurring-contact sequence, which is a MISSED_CALL_LEAD_CAPTURE and scheduling angle. Pool is thin in strict corridor geography: Monument Lighting (Grand Junction, canonical WARM) is the incumbent; pipeline already carries Liqui Green Turf and Tree Care (Round 89, 970-256-1928, dedicated holiday-lighting page) and Vivid Christmas Lighting (now merged into Monument Lighting); prior Round 109 logged the strict-geography pool as producing 0 new qualified prospects after Rounds 39/57/89. Corridor demand is real (a 2022 townsquare article documents GJ-area businesses advertising "We Hang Christmas Lights" on trucks and signs, naming only Liqui Green), but most such operators are sign/truck-only with no web presence, and the web-visible entrants are franchises (Grand Mesa Trimlight dealer network; Christmas Decor of Grand Junction, 250+ market franchise; Colorado Christmas Lights, Boulder-based statewide SEO operation already pipeline-tracked from Round 89).
