## Comparative note

Straight Edge Striping has the most credible AEO opportunity, and the gap is structural, not cosmetic. It already has the two hardest AEO ingredients: a GBP listing tied to its domain and phone, and recent named Google reviews embedded on its own homepage — exactly the trust signals answer engines cite. It also has the deepest service-page architecture of the three, mapping to specific commercial queries (ADA compliance, thermoplastic markings, sealcoating). The missing pieces are high-leverage and additive: an FAQ page answering buyer questions ("how often should a lot be sealcoated", "what does ADA striping require"), a town-level service-area list for Western Colorado, business hours, and fixing the "0+" placeholder stat counters — all cheap, all citable.

RER and State Line both grade weak but for different reasons. RER's ceiling is higher: it has a published email, phone, and a real services page, but live Lorem Ipsum on a subpage and zero per-service depth make it a riskier AEO bet today. State Line has service-page depth but no reviews, no third-party corroboration, a nationally ambiguous brand name, and keyword-stuffed titles: the AEO work there starts with trust infrastructure (reviews, GBP linkage, de-stuffing), not content.
