## Segment shape

This segment is real but thin in these towns. Aspen and the Vail Valley (Vail, Avon, Edwards) produced five qualified or near-qualified dedicated operators. Telluride, Steamboat Springs, and Crested Butte/Mt. Crested Butte produced zero dedicated home-watch operators on own sites this round. Their search surfaces are dominated by rental property managers, realtors, and security or automation companies, not home-watch trades. The five qualified rows skew toward small owner-operated outfits with thin or template web presences, which fits the revamp-and-capture opportunity model, but none have live-verified contact routes yet.
