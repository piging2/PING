## Observations for the engine
1. Painters are a phone-first trade: every qualified candidate routes phone-first; no own-site published emails were found anywhere in this segment in this pass. The value pitch is missed-call capture during ladder/scaffold hours, not form or email optimization.
2. Stack grading is uniformly UNKNOWN: no painter in this pass names business software on its own site. Any stack claim would be fabricated. The only observed tech fact is RJ's Painting's site being on the Duda builder, which is a website-platform observation, not a business-system signal.
3. Interior-repaint seasonal pivot is the honest fall angle for the high-country shops (Crested Butte, Telluride, Gunnison, Steamboat-adjacent): exterior season ends in weeks; shops booking winter interiors now are the ones with scheduling pain.
4. Thin-verification risk: only RJ's Painting had its own site page-verified. All other phone numbers in this file are place-listing-tied-to-own-domain and MUST be re-verified against live own-site pages before any outreach. Do not let a phone number from this file go straight into a call list.
