## Ecosystem notes (for pipeline.md merge)

- Irrigation & sprinkler (Western CO, batch 1, 6 candidates, 2026-09-09): fragmented and strongly local market, many long-running owner-led specialists but a large share of directory-only operators and contact-less brochure sites, especially Grand Junction, Rifle, Steamboat Springs. Roaring Fork Valley, Montrose, and Gunnison shops often pair irrigation with landscaping, lawn care, or pump work, diluting pure sprinkler fit. Winterization is explicit on most viable shops; the missed-call/after-hours intake angle is seasonally urgent with early October freezes approaching. Common dead ends: live sites with phone only (Rainmaker Montrose, Living Waters Delta, Mountain Oasis Durango), dead domains (CM Sprinklers), SEO/page farms (Buzz Sprinkler Service), broader landscapers where irrigation is peripheral (Aspen Valley Landscaping, Spring Creek Land & Waterscapes, Evergreen Pump). Steamboat Springs, Craig, and Meeker produced no own-site operators at all; strongest remaining uncovered pocket is Montrose/Delta, where a contact form on Living Waters or Rainmaker would convert two near-misses into qualified candidates.
- Septic/well/pump (Western CO, batch 1, 9 candidates, 2026-09-09): demand clusters around emergency backups and overflowing systems, real-estate and transfer-of-title inspection deadlines, rural tank locating and pumping, grease-trap maintenance, scheduled holding-tank and vault pumping, plus no-water and pump-failure emergencies, pressure failures, and water testing/treatment on the well side. Even established family firms are overwhelmingly phone-first, many publishing only a phone number on their own sites, making valid email/form candidates scarcer than businesses themselves; owner-operators doing field work with no office staff are the norm. Best fit angles: after-hours intake and emergency scheduling, triage between urgent backups and planned installations, inspection scheduling tied to real-estate deadlines. Skip patterns hit: franchises (Roto-Rooter/Corday, Rooter King), Idaho-based SEO page farm (Silver Valley Septic), directory-only (ASAP Sewer and Septic), coming-soon/placeholder sites (bjpump.co), Facebook-only operators (Samuelson Pump Co Glenwood), unreachable sites (brseptic.net). Note: Durango Septic LLC explicitly markets against voicemail, a strong positioning match.
