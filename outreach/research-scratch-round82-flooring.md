## Segment notes: timing and pitch angles
- Timing: mid-September is the pre-winter install window in mountain towns (Steamboat, Pagosa, Durango, Cortez). Second-home owners and ski-area rental turnovers push flooring projects now; free-proposal and estimate requests spike while crews are maxed out.
- Pitch angles for this segment:
  1. Missed-call capture during showroom hours: floor showrooms run lean; staff are with walk-in customers while estimate calls roll to voicemail. Every missed call is a multi-thousand-dollar job.
  2. Free in-home estimate / proposal follow-up: almost every shop in this segment offers free estimates; same-day acknowledgement and automated follow-up on unscheduled proposals is the highest-leverage automation.
  3. Out-of-town second-home-owner coordination: mountain-town shops serve absentee owners; structured update/follow-up cadences win those jobs.
  4. Website revamp ONLY for the no-website or brochure-site shops (Rubio's, Flooring By Design, Cortez Flooring, Flairmont) and per the standing rule, only framed for non-technical owners with weak sites.
  5. Rebrand cleanup (Apres Flooring Warehouse): new 2026 identity; name consistency across GBP, review platforms, and directories is immediately valuable.
- Software landscape observed: nothing above UNKNOWN/WEAK except GoDaddy Website Builder at Apres (STRONG). No Jobber/Housecall Pro/Workiz booking widgets, no online payment links, no CRM signals anywhere in this batch. Gmail and personal ISP email intake are common. The segment is operationally unsophisticated, which makes simple automation pitches land.
