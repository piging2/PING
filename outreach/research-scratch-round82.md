## Segment and ecosystem notes

### Fireplace / hearth dealers
- Timing: September through November is the highest inbound-call window of the year (chimney cleaning, inspections, service, installs). Every candidate is phone-primary; several post weekday-only hours.
- Pitch: missed-call lead capture plus instant quote follow-up during the fall rush; website revamp as secondary for the no-website shops. True North's online quote form with no instant response is the cleanest automation angle.
- Pool: Montrose and Glenwood Springs had the density; Cortez yielded nothing usable (national lead-gen spam only). Geographic expansion options for future hearth rounds: Gunnison, Salida-adjacent, Durango-adjacent, Eagle.

### Carpet / flooring
- Timing: pre-winter install window in mountain towns; second-home owners and ski-rental turnovers push projects now while crews are maxed out.
- Pitches: (1) missed-call capture during showroom hours (lean staff, walk-ins block the phone); (2) free in-home estimate/proposal follow-up, same-day acknowledgement is the highest-leverage automation; (3) out-of-town second-home-owner coordination; (4) WEBSITE_REVAMP only for no-website/brochure shops; (5) Apres rebrand cleanup (name consistency across GBP/reviews/directories).
- Software landscape: operationally unsophisticated segment, nothing above WEAK except Apres on GoDaddy Website Builder (STRONG). No Jobber/Housecall Pro/Workiz widgets, no online payments, Gmail and personal ISP inboxes common. Simple automation pitches land.
