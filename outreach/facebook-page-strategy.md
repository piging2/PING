# PING Social Facebook Page strategy

Status (2026-09-10): Nolan is creating a new Facebook Page for PING Social. Page URL goes in `facebook-page.md` once it exists.

## Why it matters
- Gives every like and comment a home to point at: profile views become page follows.
- Page follows are a warm audience for future posts and outreach, and inviting is free, native, and low risk.
- A real business page also makes PING look established when prospects check it after a DM or comment.

## Invite mechanics (Facebook native)
- Anyone who reacts to a Page post can be invited to follow the Page via the Invite button on the post's reaction list. This is the primary growth lever.
- "Invite friends" from the Page exists but is rate limited; use sparingly and only after genuine interaction.
- Never invite the same person twice. Log every invite in `facebook-engagement.md`.

## How it plugs into the engagement passes
1. Comments on Western Colorado trade Pages stay the top of funnel: punchy, specific, built to spark replies and profile views.
2. When the session is healthy and the Page exists, each engagement pass also opens the Page's recent posts, reviews reactors, and invites up to 20 new reactors per pass to follow the Page. Human pacing, same as likes and comments.
3. Prospects who reply to a comment or view the profile get a follow invite only after a real exchange, never cold.
4. No Page posts are created, boosted, or edited without Nolan asking. Engagement and invites only.

## Content (drafted on request, never auto-posted)
- The Page needs a few posts worth following before invites convert: practical tips for home-service owners (missed-call capture, quote follow-up, review requests). Drafts go in `~/workspace/outreach/facebook-page-posts.md` when Nolan asks.

## Rules
- Never buy followers, never use engagement pods, never spam-invite.
- Page name, about text, and branding are Nolan's call.
- If Facebook flags or limits invites, stop invites immediately and report it.
