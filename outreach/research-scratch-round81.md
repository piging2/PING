## Segment and ecosystem notes

### Generator installers/service

- Timing: September is the pre-winter readiness window. Strong angles: annual maintenance reminders, battery and exercise-cycle checks, free-evaluation intake, warranty and maintenance-plan follow-up, missed-call capture during outage spikes, qualification by fuel type, transfer switch, property access, and critical loads
- Demand pattern: outage-driven spikes hit small shops hard; weekday-only offices (Stone Peak) miss after-hours calls exactly when outages occur
- Pool status: Western Colorado is nearly exhausted. Round 60 (2026-09-15) found zero new generator qualifiers; most generator work is a service line of electrical contractors already tracked (Steamboat Electric, M&R Electrical, Durgin Electric, Colorado Energy Systems). Stone Peak Power is the only fresh generator specialist found this round
- Pitch caution: Stone Peak explicitly advertises "Never Speak to a Bot", so the pitch must frame PING as human-controlled overflow and capture, not replacement

### Home watch / property caretaking

- Timing: pre-ski-season second-home winterization is the current window (freeze protection, storm checks, snow removal setup, hot tub winterization)
- Demand pattern: resort markets (Telluride, Steamboat, Aspen, Vail Valley) have dense second-home absentee-owner demand; urgent calls come from leaks, freeze alarms, storms, and guest/vendor coordination needs. Owners pay for eyes-on and fast coordination, not just scheduled visits
- Best pitch angle: communication and coordination, not replacing the caretaker. Structured owner-request intake, immediate routing of urgent issues, follow-up after vendor visits, scheduled reports and photo/checklist workflows, keeping the owner in control of decisions
- Telluride and Steamboat produced the best new possibilities this round. Aspen and Vail were mostly covered in round 67; remaining Aspen names are future research pool
- Note: HomeWatchIT appears as a recurring stack in this segment (Secure Home Watch). That is a product PING should learn (software playbook), since report automation and owner communication are adjacent to PING's value
