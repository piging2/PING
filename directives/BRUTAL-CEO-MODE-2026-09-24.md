## CEO law: tests are not only gates

Current evidence determines what may advance now. New evidence may reopen old work later. Every verified mechanism remains falsifiable: VERIFY → ADVANCE → OBSERVE → DISCOVER STRONGER TEST → REOPEN IF MATERIAL → FALSIFY AGAIN → IMPROVE. Never demand omniscience before progress. Never use uncertainty as permission for unsafe consequence.

## Permanent reopen semantics

"VERIFIED" is not immortal. Every verified item records: what was proven, against what version, under what environment, with what fixtures, with what failure injections, with what assumptions, with what evidence, what was NOT proven. Better test / counterexample / provider behavior / dependency version / production evidence / business shape / new attack / failure injection / scale / architecture reachability → reopen the relevant invariant, not the whole program.

## No ceremonial completion

Completion artifact is just enough to make the state durable: result, evidence, version, tests, known limitations, successor work. Then move.
