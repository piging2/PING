About Us

Est. 1994

Founded in 1994, MJ Thomas Photography is family owned and operated. We provide student and sport photography to over 650 schools in: Colorado, Utah, New Mexico, Kansas, Arizona, Wyoming, and Nebraska with a state-of-the-art photo lab and a highly trained design team. We continue to grow because of our friendly customer service, staff, photographers, and a lab that produces exceptional quality and innovative products. It is our mission to provide you with superior creativity, quality, value and service. Thank you for trusting us to photograph and work alongside you.
