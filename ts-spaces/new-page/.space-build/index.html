04 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you ask.

So, is this just an answering service?

No. Answering is one part. The system can collect job details, handle routine questions, route urgent requests, book the right next step, and keep follow-up moving based on rules you approve.

Will it book every caller onto my calendar?

Only if that matches your process. We can qualify by service, location, urgency, or other rules before a booking happens. Your business decides what a good job looks like.

What happens when the AI does not know?

It should not bluff. A good setup knows its limits, captures the information, and hands the situation to the right person.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

Not by default. If a tool works, keep it. We look first for a clean integration or a better handoff around it.

Do I need to be a big company?

No. Owner-operated shops often feel every missed call and repeated task more directly. The system should fit the size and pace of the business.
