Goal ID: goal_1402f19c169d

## Description
Nolan reset FYD Social to an object-first product: each business exists as one live object, and its presence is a tiny circular projection that rents the unused margins of websites instead of replacing them. The build is proving the Circle on Happy Place and Coppersmith, driven by the Website Builder Authority Decisions and the template boundary law that FYD owns infrastructure while customers own their data.
