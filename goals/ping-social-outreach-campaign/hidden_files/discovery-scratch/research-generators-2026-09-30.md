## Ecosystem note: the standby-generator segment in the Western Slope corridor

- **Segment heat is real but shallow.** This is the thinnest trade segment researched so far. The corridor has effectively ONE dedicated generator shop (Gunn's, already tracked and possibly closed/stale, see note below). Everything else is general electricians who do generator installs as a line item. That is itself the finding: the segment exists, but outreach copy must speak to "electricians who install Generac/Kohler/Cummins," not pure generator dealerships.
- **Seasonal timing is favorable.** Search traffic and buyer intent peak September-November as homeowners prep for winter outages, and the segment's LinkedIn/social content right now is "think about winter backup power while it's still warm." High-ticket whole-home installs ($10k+) are decided in fall.
- **Why-now layers for outreach:** winter outage season on the Western Slope; rural propane/natural-gas homes; affluent mountain-adjacent buyers (Carbondale, Telluride-adjacent Montrose) who price whole-home systems. Do NOT fabricate claims about any prospect's outage history.
- **Pitch angles that fit the segment:** (a) missed-call capture during storm/outage spikes, when generator inquiries surge and a missed call goes to the next electrician; (b) annual maintenance-plan reminders, since generator service contracts are recurring revenue and easily forgotten; (c) quote speed on high-ticket installs, because a $12k Generac quote that takes 5 days to arrive loses to one that arrives same-day; (d) after-hours triage, because a dead generator during an outage is an emergency and a voicemail is not a plan.
- **Website-revamp angle (Nolan's 2026-09-11 call):** candidates 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 all have weak or nonexistent sites and non-technical owner profiles. Candidates 1 and 2 have modern-but-thin sites where a dedicated generator landing page is the concrete, honest recommendation.

**Flag for parent on a tracked account:** Gunn's Generator Services (all three stores, already in pipeline) shows mixed signals as of 2026-09-30: its `gunns-generator-services-inc.business.site` URL 404s and some listings show closed status, while its USDOT record (SAFER, USDOT 982268) shows ACTIVE as of 03/2025. Worth a re-verification pass before any send.
