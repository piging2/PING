# Round 1721 segment notes: carpet & upholstery cleaning, Western Colorado (2026-09-24)

## Segment heat
- Dense independent pocket in Grand Junction (7 candidates) and Rifle (3 candidates), thinner in Montrose (2), nothing qualified in Delta proper (Proteck is deduped; Big Bear Chem-Dry is a franchise; Service Steam has no website), nothing found in Fruita, Palisade, Clifton, Collbran, Loma, Mack, De Beque, or Carbondale (Belmont Clean was deduped from the pipeline).
- Most independents are owner-operator shops with phone-first contact and keyword/SEO-style domains (carpetcleaninggrandjunctionco.com, carpetcleaninggj.com, gjcarpetclean.com) — thin digital surface is the segment norm.
- Franchises are present and aggressive in the corridor: Zerorez (Fruita/GJ), ServiceMaster 24 HR (GJ), Big Bear Chem-Dry (Delta), Oxi Fresh (GJ), Stanley Steemer (GJ), Coit (GJ), Heaven's Best (Rifle, Montrose County). All excluded per policy; the independents position themselves against exactly these names (Fresh Start's GBP copy literally lists Stanley Steemer, Coit, and Oxi as competitors).

## Seasonal timing (fall move-out + holiday prep)
- Late September: college rental turnover in Grand Junction (CMU) plus fall lease rollover = move-out clean demand. Fresh Start's copy explicitly markets rental turnaround and property-management work; they are the strongest seasonal fit in the set.
- Pre-holiday deep cleans (Thanksgiving/Christmas) start booking in October/November; owner-operators with phone-only contact and weekend closure (Eagle One closed weekends; Mann's Tue-Fri only) lose holiday-prep inquiries to franchises with online booking.
- Mann's (Montrose) is closed weekends; Eagle One (Rifle) closed weekends; Thompson's and Anvil Points are two-to-three-person family teams — all high missed-call exposure while the crew is on the truck.

## Pitch angles
- Lead capture angle: "missed calls while you're on the truck" lands on all 12; strongest on Mann's, Eagle One, Thompson's, Anvil Points (tiny teams, no online booking visible).
- Seasonal angle for Fresh Start: property-manager/rental-turnaround volume + same-day/next-day promise = high after-hours call load. PING's missed-call capture directly maps.
- The George family (Cleaner Carpets By George + Rugs by George, same address, distinct phones): area-rug specialty + commercial floor-care programs; pitch the commercial recurring angle, but note they may operate as one business — outreach should treat them as one household.
