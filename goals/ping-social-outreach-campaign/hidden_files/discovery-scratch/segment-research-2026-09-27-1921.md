## Segment rationale
Residential/interior painting is a strong fall/winter segment on the Western Slope. Exterior season ends late September/early October; shops pivot to interior repaints, cabinet refinishing, drywall repair, and garage-floor epoxy for winter booking. Intake is estimate-driven (phone plus quote forms), owners are often in the field while estimates come in, which fits missed-call/lead-capture angles. Small independent owner-operated shops dominate the corridor.
