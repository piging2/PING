## Segment ecosystem notes

- Segment heat: excavation/dirt-work operators are dense across Mesa County (GJ/Fruita/Loma/Clifton) and Delta County (Delta/Austin/Olathe), thinner on the North Fork (Paonia/Cedaredge) and west Mesa County (Mack). Rural one-to-three-truck operators are the norm; the owner answers the phone from the cab.
- Seasonal timing (late Sept): driveway/grading and road-work rush before freeze-up; pond and site-prep projects closing out; snow capability (Huff, Jones) matters in 4-6 weeks. Winter = hauling + snow work for equipped operators.
- Pitch angles discovered: (1) missed-call capture is the strongest angle — "leave a message and I'll call you back" appears verbatim on Farnsworth's site; (2) 24hr emergency line on B&S = after-hours intake; (3) revamp candidates: Farnsworth (Weebly), Precision (dead site), Huff (GoDaddy brochure); (4) young companies (Adaptive, est. 2023) = reviews + capture from day one.
- Stack pattern across segment: brochure sites on Wix/Weebly/GoDaddy/Squarespace; Gmail or Outlook intake; no CRM signals anywhere; phone-first funnels everywhere. Friction chain: observed site builder + personal email -> no intake structure -> quotes lost when owner is on a machine -> opportunity for AI call/lead capture; confidence medium-high where contact info is site-published.
