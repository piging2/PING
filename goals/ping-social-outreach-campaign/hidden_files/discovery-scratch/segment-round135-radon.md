## Segment intelligence
1. Market shape: the Western Slope radon mitigation pool is thin and nearly exhausted. The established GJ independents (Square One, Stanton) are already in pipeline; the two fresh qualifiers are a legacy GJ shop the campaign somehow missed (Radon Resolve, since 2001) and an adjacent-town specialist (Basalt). Remaining untracked operators are directory-only micro-shops (Rifle) or inspectors with radon as a future/add-on service. New candidates will come from inspector bundles and adjacent towns, not from new pure-play mitigators.
2. Chain roll-up pattern confirmed again: ACE Radon (Rifle, via mail@aceradon.com) is now absorbed into Protect Environmental (aceradon.com redirects there) — a national chain. This is the third acquisition-type event in recent campaign history (SavATree/Alpine Tree, Groundworks/Foundation Repair of Western Colorado). Chain re-verification at outreach time is mandatory for this segment.
3. Seasonal timing reaffirmed: late September is the pre-surge window (closed-house testing season Oct–Mar per CDPHE); round-61 intel still holds. Radon demand is heavily real-estate-transaction driven (testing tied to closing contingencies), so the missed-call capture angle is the strongest pitch: a missed call on a transaction test can kill a deal. Radon Resolve's realtor/transaction focus and American Radon Mitigation's content-heavy site both fit missed-call + AI discoverability angles.
