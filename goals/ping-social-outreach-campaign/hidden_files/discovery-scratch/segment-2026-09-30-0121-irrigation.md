  (Rainmaker, Black Canyon, Lush Green, Boyd's, Misty Mountain) or directory-only one-man shops
  (Montrose Sprinklers, Casey's, Majestic). True sprinkler-first independents with own sites:
  Chuck's (Fruita, unroutable), Planet Earth (Montrose area, contact unverified), Aztec (GJ,
  already pipeline). Expect 1-2 net-new per full re-sweep at most.
- Seasonal timing: NOW is the peak window — Peakview (COS, out of geography but same climate
  zone) publishes "scheduling begins mid-September, winterize all October"; Chuck's own site
  carries a written winterizing guarantee as its differentiator, i.e. blowout scheduling pressure
  is THE competitive axis. Pitch angle: "October books itself full; every missed call in October
  is a blowout slot your competitor fills."

  ditch water with a pump") — worth naming in copy; Front Range shops don't speak this language.
