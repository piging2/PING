# LEARN — outreach-engine round 44 (2026-09-13)

## Observation: emerald ash borer reaches the Western Slope — tree-service demand signal

VERIFIED: emerald ash borer (EAB) confirmed in Carbondale, CO by state officials in September 2026 (Denver Post, first Western Slope detection; arrived via firewood from the Front Range). Approximately 15% of Colorado's urban forest is ash (Colorado State University estimate cited in the article).

WHY IT MATTERS (generalizable, campaign-level): EAB kills untreated ash trees within years of arrival. Municipal + residential removal, treatment, and replanting work follows detection by 1-3 seasons. Western Slope tree services (the round-44 research batch: 17 tree-care businesses across Montrose, Delta, Grand Junction, Glenwood Springs, Carbondale) gain a legitimate, time-bound demand tailwind: removals, systemic treatments, and fire-mitigation-adjacent work. This is a segment-level market signal, not a single-prospect fact.

UNCERTAIN: exact demand magnitude and timing per town; whether any single prospect will act on it. Do not pitch EAB as a claim about a prospect's business; use it as the "why now" layer on tree-service outreach (MISSED_CALL_LEAD_CAPTURE + seasonal storm-prep angles).

SOURCE EVIDENCE: article text captured in round-44 sweep research (browser.search, 2026-09-13). Raw article: Denver Post via pagesuite (Carbondale town arborist Carl Meinecke, town landscape technician Allison Uri).

## Observation: chain consolidation keeps eating the independent tree-care pool

VERIFIED this round: Alpine Tree Service (Grand Junction, own site) announces it "has joined forces with SavATree"; Glenwood source confirms Aspen Tree Service is "now part of the SavATree family". SavATree is a national chain. Both were otherwise viable independents; both are now dead ends per the no-franchise/no-chain rule (corporate marketing support, no local buying authority).

PATTERN (generalizable): in trades with national consolidators, yesterday's good prospect becomes tomorrow's SUPPRESS. Verification must re-check chain affiliation at outreach time, not just at discovery time, because acquisitions happen between research and send.
