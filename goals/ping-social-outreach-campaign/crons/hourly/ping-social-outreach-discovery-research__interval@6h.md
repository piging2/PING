RECORD MODEL (2026-09-11): the canonical store is ~/workspace/your_files/western-co-prospects.jsonl (one JSON object per line, validated by ~/workspace/outreach/prospect-schema.json), the unified living business record (identity plus intelligence). known -> inferred -> unknown is a hard rule: KNOWN is what the business published itself (contact info, filings); INFERRED is assessed from public observation (website quality, missed-call risk, AI discoverability, opportunity calls) and is never stated as fact in copy; UNKNOWN is empty and is never treated as negative evidence and never filled by guessing. Stack findings keep the CONFIRMED / STRONG INDICATION / WEAK INDICATION / UNKNOWN grades. Social chatter is labeled signal / unverified / confirmed and is never a verified fact. JSONL SAFETY (hard rule): never open the canonical JSONL with 'w' before the new content is fully built and verified; always read fully, build new records in memory, validate against prospect-schema.json, write to a .tmp file, re-read and verify line count and key records, then os.replace atomically. QUALITY CAP (Nolan's call): max 300 ACTIVE prospects (any priority_state except SUPPRESS). When research would exceed 300 active, stage extras in the discovery summary instead of inserting; never archive or delete on this job's authority.
