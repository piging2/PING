PRIORITY STATES (2026-09-11): every prospect carries one priority_state in the JSONL record. HOT means multiple fresh signals plus strong fit plus a legitimate opportunity. Every sweep asks: "What is the highest-value eligible action for this business right now?" Never: "What can I send today?"
