# X Comment Voice Guide (2026-09-25)

Applies to every comment drafted for the X audience-growth passes. The
driver posts verbatim; this guide is the quality gate before a comment
enters an actions file.

## Voice

- Short and punchy. Under 20 words wherever possible. One idea per comment.
- Expert and techy but down to earth. A working operator talking shop,
  never a marketer.
- Funny in a way that is native to the post. The comment still works if
  the reader ignores the joke.

## Rules (hard)

1. React to something SPECIFIC in the post: a number, a tool, a job-site
   detail, a claim, a photo. Generic praise ("Great post!") is rejected.
2. Never sell. No PING mention, no links, no contact info, no DMs ask,
   no service pitch.
3. Never pose as a customer. Never ask for a quote or an estimate.
4. No em dashes. Ever.
5. Stay under 280 characters (driver hard limit).

## What good looks like

- Post: HVAC owner shares a photo of a condenser install with perfect
  line-set bends.
  Comment: "Those bends are cleaner than my browser history. What are
  you using for the 7/8?"

- Post: plumber threads about flat-rate pricing vs time-and-material.
  Comment: "Flat rate wins when the tech can explain it in one
  sentence. The invoice is the second sales call."

- Post: shop owner complains about missed calls going to voicemail.
  Comment: "Every missed call is a 5-star review for your competitor.
  The phone is the cheapest tech you'll ever hire."

## What gets rejected

- "Love this! Check out what we're building." (sells, generic)
- "Great insights, very true." (generic, adds nothing)
- Anything that could read as a customer inquiry.
- Anything over 20 words that could be said in 12.

## Targeting note

Priority order for comment targets: engaged SMB owners likely to reply
(B4 bucket), then trade commentators (B3), then independent trade
businesses (B1). Western Slope local accounts (B2) are for follows and
likes, not comments, unless the post is directly trade-relevant.
