This is where PING could get interesting.

Opposition

People who strongly disagree with Musk.

Yes, even them.

Because disagreement creates conversation.

## The most important rule

Don't make PING a Musk fan account.

Make it a technology intelligence account that happens to participate in the same technical ecosystem.

That gives you credibility.

For example:

Elon says X about AI.

PING shouldn't automatically repost it.

PING should ask:

What does this mean?

Then:

Claim
↓
Evidence
↓
Related research
↓
Contradictory evidence
↓
Industry reaction
↓
PING's analysis

That's much more valuable.
