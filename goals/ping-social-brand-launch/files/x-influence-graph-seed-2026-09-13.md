## Method notes and limitations
- Three read-only sweeps via the Pig's headed Chrome, 2026-09-13, ~35 page loads
  total. No clicks, no follows, no likes, no comments.
- Primary signal: who Tier 1 converses with (replies tab parent-tweet authors) and
  mentions/quotes. The likes tabs rendered only each account's own posts and yielded
  no discovery signal through this method.
- Sample window skews to recent weeks; with_replies pages surface a mix of July
  through September 2026 activity.
- Follower counts captured from profile pages where the DOM exposed them; a few
  org accounts did not render a parseable count.
- This is the DISCOVER stage: observe broadly, compress ruthlessly. Qualification
  (which of these merit follows, likes, or deeper watching) and the interaction
  doctrine's decision tree apply next, before any API credit is spent.
