The biggest strategic point is this: the new creator APIs are not the moat; they're proof that FYD should avoid owning commodity discovery infrastructure. Upfluence itself now separates its permanent REST infrastructure from conversational MCP access, and its MCP can perform campaign actions. Upfluence Help FYD should go one step further by inserting PING's capability/approval/evidence layer between intelligence and effects.

And ATProto's recent permissions work is especially important for your long-term optionality. It supports granular resources/actions and permission sets, and recommends progressive authorization rather than demanding everything at login. AT Protocol That should become a PING design principle: connect cheaply/read-only first; request stronger capabilities only when the user asks FYD to do something requiring them.

That gives you the architecture you actually want: objects that can increasingly discover their own opportunities and market themselves, while the owner—not the model, provider, or object—retains authority over consequential actions.
