# MARKETINGOS CONTENT DIRECTIVE — TECHNICAL AUTHORITY THROUGH EVIDENCE

(Nolan, 2026-10-01 ~07:52 MDT, binding. Verbatim directive to give the crew.)

Do not build another MarketingOS authority, store, generic agent, memory system, scheduler, or analytics substrate. Search existing PING primitives first. MarketingOS is a domain over the existing mission/evidence/knowledge/action spine.

Optimize for **qualified technical propagation, not raw engagement**.

Every serious artifact must begin from a defensible technical surprise and carry a machine-readable claim ledger. Separate CONFIRMED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN and REFUTED. Preserve counterevidence. Never upgrade epistemic state during prose generation.

Before publication, run prior-art collision analysis and an independent hostile technical review. Record objections and their resolution. Expose important unresolved limitations in the artifact rather than hiding them.

Prefer artifacts generated from real PING engineering evidence: incidents, traces, falsified assumptions, deterministic replays, adversarial tests, corrections and measured outcomes. **Failure + explanation + repair + reproduction is premium source material.**

Introduce computed content features only, never authorities: SurpriseDensity, EvidenceStrength, Reproducibility, PracticalConsequence, AudienceFit, NoveltyClass and ExplanationCost. Scores recommend; they never publish.

Preserve one canonical technical artifact. All videos, shorts, posts, diagrams, articles and emails are lineage-linked derivatives of its claims/evidence. A derivative may omit a claim; it may never silently strengthen one.

Treat expert disagreement as intelligence. Technical corrections, counterexamples, prior-art references and reproduction attempts feed research missions rather than being flattened into sentiment.

Measure the full chain:
`ARTIFACT → AUTHORIZATION → DISTRIBUTION → VERIFIED EFFECT → QUALIFIED ATTENTION → EXPERT RESPONSE → ADOPTION/INTENT → OUTCOME → ATTRIBUTION → LEARNING`.

Never infer causality from sequence alone. `NO SIGNAL YET` remains different from `NEGATIVE`. `WORKER_COMPLETED` remains different from `EFFECT VERIFIED`. `ENGAGEMENT` remains different from `BUSINESS OUTCOME`.

Mission Control must project what is known, why it is known, what remains disputed, what was authorized, what actually happened, and what changed in the playbook.

**The brand behavior is the product demonstration:** PING does not ask the audience to trust its sophistication. It publishes enough evidence for a skeptical engineer to challenge it.

## Supporting doctrine (Nolan's synthesis, same message)

- The intelligence architecture is already substantially better than the content strategy on top of it. Do not spend the next cycle inventing more MarketingOS infrastructure. Make the content exhibit the same rigor as the system.
- Content engine for senior/staff/principal engineers: a surprising technical claim + unusually strong evidence + a reproducible artifact + a useful abstraction + intellectual restraint + a reason to argue about it.
- Every serious PING technical release has five layers: (1) The surprise — one sentence violating an experienced engineer's intuition. (2) The executable proof — minimal reproduction, replay, trace, failing invariant, adversarial test. (3) The mechanism — exactly why. (4) The generalizable primitive. (5) The boundary — what the evidence does NOT establish.
- Claim ledger is a mandatory pre-publication artifact: Claim | State | Evidence | Counterevidence | Reproduction | Publication treatment. A content compiler, not just fact checking. The writer must not be able to accidentally turn INFERRED into PROVEN during prose generation.
- Make PING's failures the product demo. Let PING attack itself publicly. Recurring structure: We believed X. We designed invariant Y. An adversarial test broke it. Here is the trace. Here is why our model was wrong. Here is the smallest repair. Here is the replay proving it. Here is what remains unknown.
- SurpriseDensity as a computed feature (not an authority): surprising verified conclusions / reader attention required. Counterintuitiveness x Evidence Strength x Reproducibility x Practical Consequence x Novelty-to-Audience / Explanation Cost. Score = recommendation, never publication authority.
- Build an evidence moat: the asset is the number of differentiated, reproducible observations PING owns. INCIDENT → TRACE → INVARIANT → REPRODUCTION → REPAIR → FALSIFICATION → RESULT. MarketingOS asks: is there a defensible artifact hiding in this engineering residue?
- Three novelty classes, recorded explicitly: empirical, mechanistic, explanatory. Prevents accidental "we invented this" language.
- Prior-art collision mandatory before a thesis becomes a publication candidate. Classify: KNOWN / KNOWN BUT DIFFERENT APPLICATION / KNOWN PRINCIPLE + NEW EMPIRICAL RESULT / APPARENTLY NOVEL IMPLEMENTATION / APPARENTLY NOVEL RESULT / UNKNOWN.
- Adversarial reviewer institutionalized: separate worker whose objective is to prevent publication. Attacks causality, sample size, novelty, benchmark fairness, confounders, unverifiable numbers, cherry-picking, terminology, security disclosure, reproducibility, prior art, overgeneralization, sensationalism, title-vs-experiment. Objections become structured evidence: OBJECTION → RESPONSE → SUPPORT → RESOLUTION (RESOLVED / PARTIALLY_RESOLVED / UNRESOLVED). Unresolved objections can become the most trustworthy sentence in the article.
- Reproducibility grades R0-R5 as publication metadata (R0 assertion only, R1 trace/screenshots, R2 code/methodology disclosed, R3 deterministic local reproduction, R4 clean-environment reproduction, R5 independent reproduction).
- Optimize for qualified propagation: Consumption → Attention → Understanding → Technical propagation → Expert response → Adoption → Commercial intent. A post with 300 views and three principal engineers discussing the invariant beats 100,000 generic views.
- Disagreement is first-class signal: AGREEMENT / TECHNICAL_CORRECTION / COUNTEREXAMPLE / TERMINOLOGY_DISPUTE / PRIOR_ART_REFERENCE / REPRODUCTION_ATTEMPT / REPRODUCTION_SUCCESS / REPRODUCTION_FAILURE / IMPLEMENTATION_QUESTION / LOW_INFORMATION_REACTION. Corrections feed INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH missions.
- Content branches from one canonical technical artifact with lineage. Every derivative inherits the same claim IDs. A derivative may omit a claim; it may never silently strengthen one.
- Mission Control shows epistemic quality, not vanity analytics: what we claim, why we believe it, what contradicts it, what is new, what was published, who authorized it, what happened afterward, what was learned, what changed. Preserve UNKNOWN.
- Architecture correction: do NOT equate WORKER_COMPLETED with OUTCOME. Chain: PROPOSAL → AUTHORIZATION → DISTRIBUTION ATTEMPT → PROVIDER RESULT → INDEPENDENT EFFECT OBSERVATION → OBSERVATION WINDOW → OUTCOME → ATTRIBUTION ASSESSMENT → LEARNING. Attribution strength: TEMPORALLY_ASSOCIATED / PLAUSIBLY_CONTRIBUTED / EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED / CAUSALITY_UNESTABLISHED.
- Objective: maximize the probability that a defensible technical idea propagates through the right audience without sacrificing epistemic integrity. Hierarchy: truth floor first, novelty second, compression third, propagation fourth, outcome fifth, learning sixth.
- MarketingOS stays SMALL and PARALLEL. Training example zero proved the payload shape. INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH has no registered live consumer. If the lane advances: MissionRuntime → existing worker → mission.result → existing claims/knowledge machinery. Smallest delta. No new authorities.
- Marketing article stays UNPUBLISHED. Correctly stopped at approval. Secondary to FYD.
