## Why no render was produced
The POC's hard invariant (script-gate.cjs, validate-final.cjs, deployment gate):
every narrated/rendered sentence must be an exact substring of a ledger
claim's text, carrying {claim_id, event_id}. With zero claim text in the live
ledger, any render would require inventing sentences, which is precisely what
the POC exists to forbid (GENERATED_PRESENTATION masquerading as claim text).
The fail-closed design worked as intended: it refused to produce an
evidence-violating artifact.
