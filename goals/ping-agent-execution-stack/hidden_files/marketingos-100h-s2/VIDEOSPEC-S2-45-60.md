(Remotion-class deterministic composition, Veo 3.1-class generation for ambient only), never vendors as

## PURPOSE

Argue FOR a product standard the piece does not claim to have met: continuity-A, deterministic replay of a
decision from an immutable event plane. The audience should leave able to state the two questions (audit
answers "what changed"; replay answers "prove it"), the survey result (34 systems, zero grade A), the
precedents that prove the standard is engineerable (Temporal, Fin back-test), the honest ceiling of the
category (signed derived streams, lossy histories), our own class-bound reckoning, the costs (Fowler,
GDPR), the falsifiers, and the named follow-up experiments. No product is sold. No capability is claimed
that the ledger does not support.
