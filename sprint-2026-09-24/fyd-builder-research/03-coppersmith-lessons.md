# 03 — What Coppersmith's Site Does Better: Domain Knowledge to Preserve

Source: https://coppersmithplumbing.com/ (WordPress/Elementor, fetched 2026-09-25).
Original Coppersmith is not automatically the target design, but its information architecture encodes
25 years of plumbing/HVAC conversion knowledge. FYD must preserve or improve it.

## Information architecture (what to preserve)

- **Nav is a service taxonomy, not a page list:** Home, Services, Plumbing, HVAC, Cooling, Heating, Ventilation, Shop, Gallery, Tips, About Us, Contact Us. Service LINES get top-level nav; the business sells by trade, and the nav teaches that. (FYD's fixed Home/About/Services/Explore destroys this.)
- **Hero = brand voice + phone:** "You Don't Need A Plumber, You Need A Coppersmith" — memorable, ownable, differentiates on craft identity ("coppersmith" = skilled tradesperson, not generic plumber). Subhead: "serviced Western Colorado for over 25 years... latest techniques or product recommendations." CTA is a phone number (970-245-3869), not a form. For emergency-prone trades, the phone IS the conversion.
- **Repairs & Maintenance banner:** "We'll get your home or business up-to-date." + "Schedule a Visit" — a second, distinct conversion path (not just install/quote).
- **"Experience Quality Service" intro + 4 specializations** (Plumbing / Ventilation / Heating & Cooling / HVAC), each with a one-line scope definition and distinct audiences (new construction residential/commercial, office complexes, furnace/AC/heat pump/mini-split, complete systems). Service discoverability = named lines with scope, not a flat list.
- **Popular Product Installations + "Shop now":** product-led conversion. Named products (American Standard Colony Pro, Moen 7622, Delta Trinsic Pro 1.8, Kohler Purist 1.5...) with a "Schedule Installation" form (name, email, phone, preferred date, product select). This is a parts-to-installation funnel — real revenue path FYD's generic site drops entirely.
- **Testimonials: 3 long, specific reviews** (19-year customer, 2004 install, boiler/AC/evap-cooler jobs, named reviewers). Specificity = credibility. These are the site's strongest trust asset and they sit mid-page, not buried.
- **Footer IA:** Services links, Quick Links (Service Agreement, Financing, Rebates, Contact Us, Privacy Policy — the "serious business" trust links), Get In Touch (address 630 Maldonado Street, Grand Junction CO 81501, phone, Facebook). Footer closes with credibility, not decoration.

## Conversion design (what to preserve)

- Phone number repeated: hero, banner, footer. Always visible, formatted, tappable.
- Two conversion modes: urgent (call now) and planned (schedule installation with product + date).
- "Schedule Installation" is a real lead form with a disclaimer ("We cannot guarantee openings for the date you choose") — honesty as conversion design.
- "Tips" and "Gallery" in nav: content marketing + proof, the long-tail engagement paths.

## What FYD currently does to this site

- Replaces the service taxonomy with Home/About/Services/Explore.
- Replaces the brand-voice hero with "Coppersmith Plumbing – HVAC – Mechanical" + one sentence.
- Drops Shop/products/installation funnel entirely.
- Drops the three testimonials' prominence (SocialProof is a late fixed slot).
- Phone shown as raw "Call 970-245-3869" with no conversion framing; "Visit website" CTA links away to the original site (the FYD page abdicates).
- Generic AC-condenser hero photo; no service-line media.

## Design caveat

Elementor output is busy and dated; FYD should NOT clone its visual design. Harvest the IA (service taxonomy nav, dual conversion modes, product-led funnel, testimonial specificity, trust footer) and express it through the FYD design family.
