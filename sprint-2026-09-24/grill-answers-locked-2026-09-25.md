## Thesis (locked)

FYD is the first product that turns the business you already have into an evidence-backed digital presence that understands, updates and operates itself within your control.

FYD is the first product that understands a real business from evidence, turns that understanding into a living digital presence, and keeps the presence synchronized with reality and owner intent.

PING is the infrastructure that gives AI durable, evidence-backed organizational memory, authority and continuity instead of forcing it to rediscover reality every time it acts.

PING is the evidence-backed continuity substrate that lets humans and AI know what an organization is, why the system believes it, what changed, what is authorized, and what happened when something acted.

## 30-day product law (coordinator sentence)

Do not optimize FYD for generating more websites; optimize FYD for maintaining one coherent, evidence-backed understanding of a business through observation, owner correction, privacy, projection, action, failure, reobservation and regeneration, and make that intelligence spectacularly obvious through the website.

Perfect-for-30-days version: understand one real business correctly and keep that understanding correct as reality and owner intent change.

The 20% that must survive: Acquisition -> Observation -> Evidence -> Object/Relationship -> Owner Intent -> Projection -> Ask/Why -> Capability/Effect -> Event/Replay.

## Four immediate builder decisions (locked 2026-09-25)

1. MEDIA: publicly visible does NOT equal authorized to copy/redistribute. Owner-authorized first-party business media: ingest permitted media, digest, generate derivatives, retain provenance, serve optimized versions. Third-party/public sources without rehost right: retain source reference + metadata, no permanent copy. MediaAsset separates source observation, rights/authorization basis, immutable digest, generated derivatives, visibility, provenance.
2. OWNER GATE: demo owner-mode only for the current controlled test, clearly marked as simulated authorization. No public publish, provider write, business claim, or consequential external action may rely on the demo toggle. Those require actual verified authority.
3. VISITOR ASK FYD: full evidence-backed Q&A narrowly grounded to the entity graph. Unsupported business-specific questions say what FYD does not know. General model knowledge only as a visibly separate mode, never blended into business answers.
4. COMPARISON METRICS: keep mobile UX, contact discoverability, service discoverability, performance. ADD factual correctness/evidence coverage and successful customer action. A faster site that publishes a wrong emergency number loses.

## Collapsed architecture law (from Nolan, locked)

FYD compiles a business into an operational model: SOURCE WORLD -> OBSERVATIONS -> EVIDENCE -> CLAIMS -> ENTITIES/OBJECTS -> RELATIONSHIPS -> OWNER INTENT -> CAPABILITIES -> PROJECTIONS -> OUTCOMES. Website is projection #1; Ask is #2; search, Circle, social protocols, agent context are further projections. None are canonical truth.

Knowledge transitions vs presentation intent never collapse. "We no longer offer drain cleaning" = knowledge transition affecting website, Ask, search, future social, future automations. "Put drain cleaning lower" = presentation intent affecting the relevant projection.

Renderer is non-authoritative: receives SiteSpec + ObjectGraph projection + ViewerContext + VisibilityPolicy and renders them. Never invents factual business claims. Generated factual copy must first exist as a typed derived/generated claim with evidence bindings and classification. Binding verifier belongs at the PROJECTION BOUNDARY. Material factual claim failing binding policy fails closed or renders explicitly unverified/generated where policy permits.

Preserve internally: DIRECT OBSERVATION / OWNER ASSERTION / DERIVED FACT / INFERENCE / GENERATED COPY. Never make them look identical. This powers Why, Ask, reconciliation, correction, confidence behavior, future automation.

Observed X vs owner Y: preserve both. Projection may prefer Y per policy. Evidence history keeps X. Re-observation of X -> CONFLICT / STALE SOURCE, never silent overwrite of Y. This is continuous reconciliation.

Every material factual claim provenance-addressable. Visitors: quiet/on-demand. Owners: easy inspection. Disputes/high consequence: prominent. Moat = availability of provenance, not visual clutter.

Ask FYD answers from current object graph + relationships + evidence + visibility + viewer context. Distinguishes KNOWN / CONFLICTED / UNKNOWN (coarse) with support classes SUPPORTED DIRECTLY / SUPPORTED BY MULTIPLE EVIDENCE / DERIVED / CONFLICTED / UNSUPPORTED. Never fills business gaps from model priors. First test: "What services does this business offer, and how do you know?"

AI owns: messy-source understanding, ambiguity, entity resolution candidates, summarization, site planning, copy, conversation, research, proposal generation. Deterministic software owns: validation, canonicalization, IDs, signatures, policy, permissions, exact matching, visibility, event writing, projection, replay, rate limits, execution receipts. Never spend intelligence on deterministic work.

SiteSpec is an intermediate representation: INPUT ObjectGraph + OwnerIntent + ViewerContext + DesignTokens + CapabilitySet -> OUTPUT typed SiteSpec. Renderer executes deterministically. No bespoke generated JSX as customer state. SiteSpec never becomes a general programming language.

Regeneration state separation: SOURCE-DERIVED STATE / OWNER-ASSERTED-CONTROLLED STATE / GENERATED PRESENTATION STATE. Source refresh updates source-derived only; never silently destroys owner assertions or presentation intent.

Approval tiers: LOW (presentation reorder, hide/show optional section, theme/design token, non-factual layout) -> apply reversibly + undo. MEDIUM (public factual correction, contact presentation, service visibility, business description) -> proposal + simple confirmation. HIGH (pricing, credentials, ownership, employee identity, external publication, messages, booking, provider mutation, financial/legal claims) -> explicit authorization. CRITICAL -> strong authority + explicit confirmation + receipt. PING safety without bureaucracy.

Claiming: IDENTITY / CLAIM / VERIFICATION / CONTROLLER / CAPABILITY stay separable. Supports multiple managers, agencies, employees, franchises, delegation, revocation, disputes.

External effects resolve through ACTOR / INTENT / TARGET OBJECT / CAPABILITY / POLICY / AUTHORITY / EXECUTION / RECEIPT / OUTCOME. No provider-specific action authorities. No giant AUTONOMOUS=TRUE. Autonomy per action class + scope + constraints.

Outcome loop (eventual): visitor asked, lead submitted, phone clicked, appointment requested, quote requested, booking completed, review received, customer converted. OBSERVATION -> UNDERSTANDING -> ACTION -> OUTCOME -> LEARNING. That is where the system compounds.

Acquisition: HTTP FIRST -> structured extraction -> mature crawler -> browser escalation -> intelligence only when required. Benchmark (Crawl4AI 0.9.4, Lightpanda 0.4.1, Playwright) but never couple canonical objects to any crawler; they produce OBSERVATIONS. Arbitrary URL = hostile input: scheme validation, DNS/IP controls, private/link-local/metadata blocking, redirect revalidation, resource/time limits, content limits, browser isolation, safe media handling, sanitized logs. No crawler control fields casually exposed to users. Public URL != redistribution permission.

Schema leverage: prove with Business / Service / Person / Location / Post-Project, not 100 schemas. Schema should drive validation, types, object forms, component eligibility, relationship rules, capability descriptions, Ask context, search indexing, protocol mapping.

HARVEST (commodity): crawling/browser automation, JSON-LD processing, OAuth, protocol wire formats, schema validators, media transforms, search engines, MCP transport, federation libraries. CRDTs only if concurrent offline editing becomes a real requirement; never under canonical PING history now.
BUILD (differentiation): business proceduralization, evidence reconciliation, object semantics, owner intent, cross-projection consistency, Ask context, action planning, outcome learning.

MCP = interoperability surface, not canonical semantics. Keep canonical authority internal; expose standardized agent/tool boundaries. ActivityPub / ATProto / Nostr / Farcaster = future projection/action backends; harvest ATProto's schema discipline, stable DID vs handle, granular permissions, typed records. Never let them dictate PING identity/object semantics.

Wedge: owner-operated local service business with existing digital presence and no digital operations team. Do not simultaneously optimize for creator/developer/musician/enterprise/influencer/e-commerce.

Next boundary order: Ask FYD grounded in evidence -> Why This on one material claim -> confirm/correct/hide -> prove correction survives regen -> plain-English presentation change -> typed SitePatch -> approval by consequence -> second source observation -> prove source diff does not overwrite owner intent -> ONE real owner. Only then expand.

The six states that must never collapse: Observation != Truth. Evidence != Claim. Owner intent != observed reality. Projection != canonical state. Authorization != identity. Execution result != verified outcome.

FYD perfectly does this: GIVE IT A REAL BUSINESS. FYD UNDERSTANDS THE BUSINESS. THE OWNER CORRECTS IT. FYD REMEMBERS. FYD BUILDS FROM IT. FYD ANSWERS FROM IT. FYD KEEPS IT CURRENT. FYD HELPS A CUSTOMER ACT ON IT.
