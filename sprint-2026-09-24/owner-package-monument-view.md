## 4. OWNER JOURNEY: The Exact Script of the Owner Test Loop

Written in plain language, exactly as the owner would experience it. No technical jargon.

1. **You get a link from Nolan.** A short message from someone you know locally, saying he has been building a tool that turns what the internet already says about a business into a clean, honest web presence, and he made a private demo of yours. He is clear: this is a product test, not a sales pitch, and saying no is fine.
2. **You open it on your phone.** It looks like a simple website for Monument View Coffee Shop. A banner at the top says it is a demo preview, not your real site, and that everything in it was generated from public information, not written by you.
3. **You see your business understood (and misunderstood).** Some things are right: the address, that you are a family-run coffee shop. Some things may be wrong or missing: hours, menu items, details FYD guessed at. Each fact shows where it came from, so you can tell what is real and what the machine filled in.
4. **You correct one thing.** You tap something wrong, fix it in plain words ("We close at 3 on Sundays, not 5"), and submit the correction. It asks you to confirm before anything changes.
5. **You preview the change.** The preview updates and shows your correction in place, clearly marked as owner-corrected.
6. **You approve it.** A simple approve step: you confirm the corrected version is right. Nothing goes public; this stays a private preview.
7. **Refresh still shows your fix.** You close the page, reopen it later, and your correction is still there. It survived: what you told it stuck.
8. **You are asked what you thought.** A short debrief with Nolan: was this useful, what was confusing, would you ever want the real version. No commitment asked.

**What the owner never sees:** anything about SiteSpec, events, cryptographic keys, projections, or graph internals. They see a website, an "ask about your business" box, a correction form, and an approve button.

**Success for this test:** one real owner completes the full loop (see understood, correct one thing, preview, approve, refresh persists) and gives honest feedback. That is the entire bar.
