## Transferable marketing mechanisms (separate from local opportunities)

These are the reusable patterns found during this sweep, usable anywhere, distinct from the Grand Junction-specific list above:

1. **Vendor-hub organizer node.** A permanent multi-vendor space (antique mall, market hall) concentrates dozens of micro-operators under one organizer. One relationship with the organizer reaches all vendors. Cheapest reproducible implementation: a vendor-hub owner conversation about how their vendors handle missed calls and customer follow-up. Nolan-hours: ~1/month per hub.

2. **Dealer-hosted enthusiast night.** A dealership (Harley, auto) hosts free evening gatherings that attract contractor-dense crowds. The $10 swap-meet vendor space is the cheapest physical presence available. Transferable to any dealer with an enthusiast community. Nolan-hours: ~2 per event.

3. **Volunteer work as trust entry.** Trail and river cleanup days put you shoulder-to-shoulder with trades-adjacent locals for hours. No pitch, just work. Trust earned this way converts better than any networking event. Transferable to any community with outdoor volunteer infrastructure. Nolan-hours: ~4 per day.

4. **Seasonal market final-day attendance.** A farmers or makers market's final day of the season concentrates vendors who are evaluating their year and planning next year: the exact moment a missed-call or follow-up conversation is relevant. Transferable to any seasonal market. Nolan-hours: ~3.
