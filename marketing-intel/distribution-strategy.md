## 1. Receipts Before Punchlines

What ships: THE PING RECEIPT device and LIVE render mode for all current
draft patterns.
Sequence: Receipts go first because they unlock CLOWN and EDUCATE. Show
FOUND -> VERIFIED -> STORED -> REUSED -> DECISION -> RESULT with real
dates before any punchline.
Cadence: 1 LIVE receipt per day for 5 days, each approved individually.
Shippable checklist: evidence object has real dates behind every arrow;
receipt-cam capture from the Pig browser; human approval; no auto-tagging.
Risk: If dates are missing the receipt looks like a claim and breaks trust.
Success metric: profile visits, replies asking about the receipt,
conversations started, saves.

## 2. Score Then Schedule

What ships: the ranked live queue scoring system applied to the drafts.
Sequence: Score all drafts on evidence_quality x novelty x entertainment x
market_relevance x actionable_insight before choosing order. Highest score
posts first. Lowest score stays in draft until improved.
Cadence: score refresh twice weekly; at most 2 items move to queued per
refresh.
Shippable checklist: five documented scores per pattern; evidence_quality
source linked; human approval on the score; queued state set manually.
Risk: Scoring without evidence creates theater, the same problem the
Marketing Circus mocks.
Success metric: drafts held back for lack of evidence; average time from
draft to approved; reply rate on the top-scored post.

## 3. Durable First, Quiet Queue Second

What ships: THE MARKETING CIRCUS device across the patterns, split
durable vs non-durable.
Sequence: Post durable structural patterns first (conflicting phone
numbers, no online booking path, 24/7 promise with no after-hours path).
They keep full score under freshness windows. Save non-durable novelty
for when the durable queue is quiet.
Cadence: 2 durable posts per week; 1 non-durable only with 3+ approved
items waiting.
Shippable checklist: pattern tagged durable or non-durable; evidence
lineage from the quiet outreach engine; EDUCATE render ready; human
approval; no auto-publish.
Risk: Posting a stale non-durable pattern without downgrade looks out of
touch.
Success metric: views per durable post; conversations about booking-path
fixes; profile visits from Western Colorado trades.

## 4. Capability Gated Punchlines

What ships: CLOWN render mode for AGENCY MAGIC TRICK and THE SAAS STACK
GRAVEYARD, gated to proven capabilities only.
Sequence: No CLOWN ships unless the capability behind it is provable
(e.g. do not clown the missing booking path until Q10 or a Pig browser
booking demo is provable). EDUCATE ships while the capability sits in the
arbitrage backlog. CLOWN unlocks after proof.
Cadence: EDUCATE 2x per week while capability is in backlog; CLOWN 1x per
week only with capability proof.
Shippable checklist: capability proof artifact; EDUCATE script stating the
problem plainly; CLOWN script pattern-level, never personal; human
approval; any SaaS price named is real and sourced.
Risk: A punchline without capability proof makes you the circus you mock.
Success metric: replies asking how PING solves it; conversations started;
saves; click-through to the capability demo.

## 5. Human Gate At Every Stage

What ships: pipeline states draft -> approved -> queued -> posted, with
human approval at each move.
Sequence: Draft from the tandem pipeline, Nolan reviews evidence,
approved, queued manually, posted manually. No step auto-advances.
Cadence: approval batch daily at a fixed time; max 3 moves per batch;
posting within Western Colorado owner work hours.
Shippable checklist: evidence state VERIFIED and STORED; all three render
modes ready from the same evidence object; Nolan's approval recorded;
auto-tagging confirmed off; no auto-publish flag.
Risk: Bypassing human approval to keep cadence ships unproven claims.
Success metric: draft rejection rate at approval; zero auto-publish
incidents; zero auto-tag incidents; replies noting the respectful tone.

## 6. Western Colorado Proof Loop

What ships: LIVE host voice ("Okay, PING just found something
interesting...") on Western Colorado observations, EDUCATE follow-up.
Sequence: LIVE goes first (real observation), EDUCATE 48 hours later,
CLOWN only with capability proof. Local relevance first.
Cadence: 1 LIVE per week tied to a real market pattern (business never
named), 1 EDUCATE follow-up.
Shippable checklist: evidence from the Western Colorado pool first;
location noted, business not tagged; the "why it matters" tied to missed
calls or scheduling friction for 1-15 person shops; human approval;
receipt-cam where the Pig browser was used.
Risk: Naming or tagging a specific business turns a pattern into a
personal callout.
Success metric: profile visits from Western Colorado; conversations with
trades in those towns; replies mentioning their own booking or phone path.

## 7. Q1 To Q10 Ladder

What ships: THE PING RECEIPT plus capability pipeline items in arbitrage
order.
Sequence: Q1 missed-call text-back first (pairs with the 24/7 pattern),
then Q10 booking-path composition, then Q3 review loop, Q5 reactivation,
Q4 competitor watch. Each receipt unlocks the next CLOWN as proof
accumulates.
Cadence: one capability-tied story every 10 to 14 days, no faster than
proof can be shown.
Shippable checklist: arbitrage item moved to proven with lineage evidence;
receipt with real dates; all three render modes ready; human approval.
Risk: Promoting later items before Q1/Q10 are proven makes sequencing feel
random and weakens the actionable-insight score.
Success metric: conversations about a specific capability; demo requests;
replies referencing the RESULT, not just the problem.
