# Western Colorado Competitive Technology Map

Living document. Nolan's directive 2026-09-14. Updated by the existing
marketing-intelligence rotation; no new schedule owns it.

Grading rule (hard): every technology claim is OBSERVED (public page, headers,
vendor docs, the business's own statements), INFERRED (reasoned from public
signals, stated as inference), or UNKNOWN. Never present INFERRED as
OBSERVED. Never fill UNKNOWN with guessing.

## 7. Existing PING / Pig / Orca equivalents

PING: evidence lineage, decision provenance, persistent knowledge, verified
intel promotion. Pig: browser interaction, posting, engagement, form
verification. Orca: execution infrastructure. The outreach engine, tandem
pipeline, and intel rotation are the composed equivalents of agency
research desks, schedulers, and reporting tools.

## 8. Capability gaps

What we cannot currently reproduce, stated precisely with the smallest
actual missing piece. A gap is not "we should build X"; it is "mechanism M
requires piece P, which we do not have."

## 9. Arbitrage opportunities
