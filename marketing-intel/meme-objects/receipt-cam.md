# PING Receipt Cam

The recurring format. The receipt makes the joke credible. Every edition
follows the same five beats, in order. The first edition is filled from
real evidence; the format is the template for all future editions.

---

## PING RECEIPT #1: The 24/7 Promise

**CLAIM**

"24/7 emergency service."

**RECEIPT**

7 of 12 mapped Grand Junction trade websites make the claim. 0 of 12 show
any live chat or real booking widget for the after-hours customer.
(competitive-tech-map.md, 2026-09-14 seed. OBSERVED.)

**FINDING**

The after-hours customer journey ends at a phone call nobody answers or a
contact form nobody reads until morning.

**CLOWN**

"The emergency apparently ends when the office closes."

**OPPORTUNITY**

Missed-call text-back plus after-hours intake. A fast text response
bridges the missed call and the human callback. (Arbitrage Q1.)

---

## The format (reuse for every edition)

1. CLAIM: the public promise, quoted from observed sources.
2. RECEIPT: the observed customer journey, with evidence refs and grade.
3. FINDING: what the gap actually is, in plain language.
4. CLOWN: the punchline. Pattern-level, never personal, never naming a business.
5. OPPORTUNITY: the deployable fix, linked to the arbitrage queue where one exists.

Rules: the receipt is never funnier than it is true. The clown beat never
asserts more than the receipt supports. Provisional receipts carry their
hedge in every beat, including the clown beat.
